Meet Dr. Sneh Pandey — and the moment that changed everything.

Dr. Sneh Pandey, MD

Medical Director

Board-Certified: Internal Medicine Obesity Medicine Specialist ADHD-CCSP Certified

Licensed in TX, PA, FL

Dr. Pandey didn't set out to build a telehealth practice. He set out to be a good doctor.

He trained in internal medicine. He worked inpatient and outpatient. He saw patients across the full spectrum of care — from acute crises to chronic conditions that quietly eroded people's quality of life over years. He was good at his job. He cared about his patients.

But the system kept getting in the way.

Ten-minute appointments for problems that needed an hour. Patients who came in with years of unanswered questions and left with a referral and a follow-up date three months out. Adults who had clearly been struggling for decades — exhausted, overwhelmed, quietly falling behind — being told they were "a little stressed" and sent home.

ADHD patients, especially, kept showing up in his mind long after their appointments ended.

Because ADHD in adults doesn't look the way most people think it does. It doesn't look like a hyperactive kid who can't sit still. It looks like a 38-year-old woman who's been called "scattered" her whole life and internalized every bit of it. It looks like a man in his 40s who's been grinding on willpower alone for two decades and is completely burnt out. It looks like someone who's smart, capable, and still can't figure out why the simplest things feel impossible.

These people weren't getting proper evaluations. They weren't getting time. They weren't getting answers.

So Dr. Pandey built something different. Not a volume clinic. Not another platform that rushes you through an intake and calls it care. Something that gave patients what he always wanted to give them inside the traditional system — and never had enough time to.

Real appointments. Real conversations. Real answers.

That's Siya Health.

"Modern healthcare forgot the human side of medicine. Siya Health was built to bring it back." — Dr. Sneh Pandey, MD

What we actually believe.

We believe that getting a proper ADHD evaluation shouldn't require a 6-month wait, a lucky referral, or knowing the right words to say to be taken seriously.

We believe that adults with ADHD have spent enough time being told to try harder, be more organized, just focus, just prioritize. They don't need more advice. They need answers.

We believe that a 10-minute appointment is not medicine. It's triage. And most of our patients deserve — and have always deserved — something much better than triage.

We believe in telling patients the truth, even when it's complicated. Especially when it's complicated.

And we believe that the right diagnosis, delivered with care, by someone who genuinely understands what they're looking at, can change a person's entire relationship with themselves.

That's not an exaggeration. We've watched it happen. Over and over again.

That's why this practice exists.
