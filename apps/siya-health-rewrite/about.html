Dr. Pandey has treated patients across inpatient and outpatient medicine, managing complex medical conditions across the full spectrum of care. He has also treated over 5,000 patients in medical weight loss using GLP-1–based therapies and metabolic care.

His approach combines clinical depth with practical, real-world treatment plans—designed for results, not just diagnoses.

After years in traditional healthcare, one thing became clear: the system often prioritizes speed, volume, and billing over thoughtful, patient-centered care.

Siya Health was built to change that.

More time with patients.
Clear, evidence-based decisions.
Care that actually fits real life.

ADHD is one area where this matters deeply. Many adults go years without clear answers. Dr. Pandey focuses on thorough, evidence-based evaluations that lead to real clarity—not quick labels.

Why Siya Health Exists

Too many people wait months for answers—about ADHD, weight, or basic metabolic care. They're told to "try harder," or that their struggles don't warrant a real evaluation. That's not good enough.

We built Siya Health to remove the friction: same-week appointments, board-certified providers, and care that treats you like a whole person—not a chart. ADHD, weight loss, metabolic health—all under one roof, delivered with respect for your time and goals.
