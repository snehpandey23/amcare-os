Dr. Pandey has extensive experience treating adult ADHD patients through telehealth, combining evidence-based assessment with personalized care plans. His ADHD-CCSP certification reflects specialized training in ADHD care for adults.

Why I focus on ADHD care

So many adults struggle silently with focus, organization, and follow-through—often for years. They've been told to "try harder" or that it's "just how they are." I believe everyone deserves a thorough evaluation and a clear path forward. ADHD is highly treatable, and getting the right diagnosis can be life-changing.
