Why patients choose Derek Timbs

I began my own health journey at 21 after struggling with weight—that experience still shapes how I talk about medical weight loss and metabolic change with patients.

I worked in ER and family practice before spending 16 years in occupational medicine, then shifted fully to preventive and weight-loss care in 2024. At Siya, I see adults in Texas (licensed in Texas and Ohio) who want clinician-led GLP-1 or metabolic care with clear monitoring and honest scope-of-practice boundaries.

  • GLP-1 & metabolic expertise—semaglutide, tirzepatide, and adjunct options discussed transparently.

Care philosophy

Weight and hormone care deserve the same rigor as any other prescription—screening, contraindications, titration, and follow-up spelled out in plain language.
