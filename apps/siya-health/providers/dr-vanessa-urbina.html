Why patients choose Dr. Urbina

My path includes medical training at the University of Miami and years in community family medicine—urgent needs, chronic conditions, and the everyday health questions that do not fit a 10-minute slot.

At Siya, I bring that same continuity mindset to telehealth: ADHD when attention and life demands collide, weight and metabolic care when habits and biology interact, and primary care when you need a medical home that answers the phone.

  • Whole-person visits—ADHD, weight, and primary concerns in context, not silos.
  • Lifestyle-first when appropriate—evidence-based options explained plainly.
  • Telehealth with continuity—follow-up that respects your calendar and your goals.
