Who should avoid phentermine for weight loss?

Educational only: This page is for general education—not personal medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. See a licensed clinician for your situation.

Clinician-informed

Educational content informed by clinical practice patterns—not personal medical advice.

Quick FAQ. This page answers one focused question. For in-depth evidence, treatment discussion, and care pathways, see Phentermine for weight loss: safety and effectiveness (full guide).

Short answer

Phentermine is an FDA-approved short-term appetite suppressant for select patients when benefits outweigh risks. It is contraindicated in many cardiovascular, hyperthyroid, and medication-interaction scenarios. Monitoring blood pressure, heart rate, mood, and sleep is essential.

Detailed answer

It is not a first-line substitute for comprehensive obesity care in all patients.

Myth vs reality

Myth Normal labs mean metabolic health is fine.

Reality Insulin resistance and sleep apnea often hide behind “normal” panels.

Myth GLP-1 replaces lifestyle change.

Reality Protein, strength training, and sleep still anchor long-term outcomes.
