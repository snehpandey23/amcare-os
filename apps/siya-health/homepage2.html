Why healthcare keeps failing busy professionals

  • Never open when you need it

    Doctor’s offices run 9-to-5. So does your job. Somehow you’re expected to be sick on their schedule, not yours.

  • Taking a day off shouldn’t cost you a day’s pay

    No PTO, no FMLA, no insurance that actually covers it — getting care becomes a financial decision before it’s ever a medical one.

  • An answer that comes too late isn’t an answer

    A callback three days later, after you’ve already had to make the decision without it — that’s not care, that’s delay with extra steps.

  • Brushed off, then handed a prescription

    Your symptoms get a pill, not a real look. Nobody stayed long enough to ask what’s actually going on underneath it.

  • One body, five specialists, zero coordination

    You’re carrying all of it in one body — but finding one place that treats the whole picture, instead of a new doctor for every symptom, shouldn’t be this hard.

Common patterns we see in busy professionals

  • Exhausted without an explanation

  • Weight feels stuck no matter what you try

  • Brain fog and focus problems that won’t clear

  • Lifelong focus struggles

  • Can’t manage sleep — especially with shift work

  • Hair loss or not gaining muscle despite effort

  • Persistent neck or back pain that won’t let up

You’re not failing. The system is.

Meet the founders

Siya Health was founded by Dr. Sneh Pandey, MD and Dr. Swati Pandey, MD — physicians, parents, and partners. Between demanding medical careers and raising a family, we lived the exact problem Siya Health was built to solve: healthcare that doesn’t fit around a real schedule. We’ve seen it from both sides — as providers working inside a system that wasn’t built for anyone’s time, and as patients trying to get care for ourselves and our family within it. Siya Health exists to give busy professionals — including the doctors treating them — the flexibility neither side ever had.
