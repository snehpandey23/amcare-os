How we diagnose

Structured evaluation—not a quick label or an online quiz score alone.

Licensed clinicians review your history, how symptoms show up over time, and what else might overlap—then use objective inputs when they may change the plan. This is clinician-led care, not automated diagnosis.

  • Structured clinical history across settings and over time—not a single checklist visit
  • Validated tools when clinically appropriate—as part of evaluation, not as a standalone answer
  • Review of prior records and context when you share them
  • Labs or other objective inputs only when they may clarify the picture—not a default test menu

Screening is not diagnosis. A licensed clinician determines appropriate next steps; treatment—including medication—is never guaranteed.
