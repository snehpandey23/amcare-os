How we diagnose

Integrated means we look at the whole picture behind one symptom—not a claim of a multi-specialty network.

Stress, weight, fatigue, and ADHD-type focus problems often travel together. Licensed clinicians take a structured history across those threads, then use objective inputs when they may change the plan. This is clinician-led care in one place—not automated diagnosis, and not a promise of in-house therapists or social workers.

  • Whole-picture history: how focus, stress, sleep, weight, and energy show up together over time
  • Validated tools when clinically appropriate—as part of evaluation, not as a standalone answer
  • Review of prior records and context when you share them
  • Labs or other objective inputs only when they may clarify the picture—not a default test menu

If medication is part of your plan, we don’t rush it. Stimulant medication isn’t started at the first visit. You’ll review and sign a treatment agreement first, and care includes ongoing check-ins—things like scheduled follow-up, pill counts, and urine screens when they’re part of keeping treatment safe.

Screening is not diagnosis. A licensed clinician determines appropriate next steps; treatment—including medication—is never guaranteed.
