How care unfolds with Siya

One process for every patient — whatever you’re noticing. We don’t start you on a separate “ADHD path” or “weight path.” We start with you.

1

Discovery call

A free Meet & Greet where you share what’s going on — fatigue, focus, weight, hormones, or all of the above. No label required to start.

2

Structured evaluation

Standardized assessments and clinical history review when they help. We look across symptoms together — not one complaint in isolation.

3

Personalized care plan

A holistic plan built from that evaluation. Metabolic concerns may include lifestyle guidance and medication when appropriate. Focus concerns may include a medication plan when clinically right. Always explained in plain language.

4

Ongoing care coordination

Your care team follows up on weight, mood, focus, and sleep — so nothing falls through the cracks between visits.

5

Longitudinal outcome tracking

Results tracked against your clinical history over time — for example PHQ trends or cognitive assessment results — so you can see progress, not just appointments.
