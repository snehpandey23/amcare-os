Why Siya Health exists

The system is slow. Answers about ADHD shouldn’t be.

Too many high-functioning adults slip through the cracks—good jobs, full calendars, invisible struggle. They wait months for an appointment, get misread as “anxious,” or leave with a stack of advice that ignores how their brain actually works.

We built Siya Health because you’re not lazy—and more discipline isn’t always the solution. You deserve a licensed team that takes adult ADHD seriously, uses structured tools alongside clinical judgment, and respects your time.

Same-week access where possible. Transparent membership-style pricing. Telehealth that still feels like real medicine—not a chatbot.

How care works

Four steps from curious to clear.

Step 1

Free ADHD screening

Two minutes. Honest questions. Instant signal on whether evaluation makes sense.

Step 2

Meet & Greet

Talk with our team, ask anything awkward, see if we’re a fit—before you commit.

Step 3

Full evaluation

60–90 minutes with a licensed, ADHD-CCSP–trained clinician—history, validated tools, objective testing when appropriate.

Step 4

Ongoing care

Follow-up for medication monitoring, dose changes, or coordinated support—so the plan sticks.
