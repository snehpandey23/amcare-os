# Post-labs patient return (Spruce / email)

Use after a patient orders labs or uploads results. Tone: calm, not salesy.

## Short Spruce / secure chat

Subject/context: Lab results ready

> Got your results? Don’t panic over a single number. Reference ranges don’t diagnose disease.
>
> When you’re ready, book a free Meet & Greet or a visit so we can interpret them in context:
> https://www.siya.health/redirect/meet-greet
>
> Calm guide: https://www.siya.health/labs/how-to-read-results

## Email (post-order)

> You’re opening (or have opened) Siya’s laboratory storefront for ordering. When results arrive, bring them back to us—we help with interpretation and next steps.
>
> How to read results: https://www.siya.health/labs/how-to-read-results  
> Preventive / annual labs context: https://www.siya.health/labs/preventive  
> Follow-up plans: https://www.siya.health/pricing

## Do not

- Deep-link Rupa as the “interpretation” destination
- Promise a diagnosis from labs alone
- Push a sticky “buy blood” CTA
