# California employer pilot page — removed

**Status:** Removed. `/employers/california-pilot` permanently redirects to `/employers`.

The per-employee rates previously documented here were illustrative and are not accurate. Employer pricing is scoped per employer and is not a published number. Do not regenerate `employers/california-pilot.html`. Do not run `scripts/generate-employer-ca-pilot-pitch.mjs` to republish a rate. That script is a no-op and is not part of `npm run build`.

Individual self-pay pricing stays on `/pricing`.
