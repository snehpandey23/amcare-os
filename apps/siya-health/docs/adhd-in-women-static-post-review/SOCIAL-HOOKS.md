## Hook 1 — Relatable

**Headline:** ADHD in women often looks quiet.  
**Body:** Inattention, overwhelm, and emotional intensity — not always hyperactivity. Many women are told it’s anxiety or “just stress” for years. Screening is not diagnosis.  
**CTA:** Read the Health Guide · Talk to a Clinician

---

## Hook 2 — Myth bust

**Headline:** Hyperactivity isn’t the only ADHD story.  
**Body:** Inattentive presentation is common in women. Daydreaming, disorganization, and rejection sensitivity can fly under the radar until adulthood. Evaluation does not guarantee medication.  
**CTA:** Read the Health Guide

---

## Hook 3 — Patient-voice (forum-safe)

**Headline:** “I wasn’t bouncing off walls — I was drowning quietly.”  
**Body:** Late recognition is common when symptoms were masked as perfectionism, people-pleasing, or anxiety. Structured evaluation with a licensed clinician still matters.  
**CTA:** Read the Health Guide · Book ADHD Evaluation

---

## Hook 4 — Professional (clinician-facing)

**Headline:** Screen for inattentive ADHD when adult women present with chronic overwhelm.  
**Body:** Sex differences in presentation and referral bias delay care. History across settings beats stereotype checklists. Siya Health is physician-led telehealth — not a psychiatry practice.  
**CTA:** Share the Health Guide with patients
