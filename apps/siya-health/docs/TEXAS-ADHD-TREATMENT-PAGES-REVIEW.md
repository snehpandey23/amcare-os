| Conflict | Pages | Recommendation |

| **Texas-wide treatment intent** (`ADHD treatment Texas`, `online ADHD treatment Texas`) | Four city pages + `/adhd-care` + `/adhd-diagnosis-texas` + `/blog/online-adhd-diagnosis-texas` | Build a **Texas ADHD Hub** (below) as the statewide owner; city pages support with “in Dallas / in Houston…” modifiers |

| **Cost queries** | `/blog/adhd-evaluation-cost-texas` vs city FAQs mentioning $199 | Leave cost page as cost owner; city FAQs should link *to* cost page (already mostly do) |
| **Austin thinner page** | Austin ~1.7k words vs peers ~1.9–2.3k | Not cannibalization—depth gap. Expand Austin in a later pass if rankings stall (not in this review) |

### No acute cannibalization among the four

Each primary is city-qualified. Google can differentiate `ADHD Treatment Dallas` vs `ADHD Treatment Houston` cleanly **if** titles, H1s, and local body stay city-specific (they do) and a hub clarifies statewide hierarchy.
