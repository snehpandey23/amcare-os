## Outline

1. **Recognition** — Tired isn’t one disease; it’s a pattern that deserves a differential, not shame.  
2. **What fatigue is (and isn’t)** — Sleepiness vs low energy vs post-exertional; when urgent.  
3. **Common overlapping drivers** (educational, not exhaustive): sleep apnea / poor sleep, iron/ferritin, thyroid, mood, ADHD executive load, midlife hormones, metabolic, medications, life load.  
4. **“Normal labs” ≠ fine** — Link preventive / how to read results.  
5. **ADHD vs fatigue** — Lifelong attentional pattern vs new exhaustion; both can coexist.  
6. **What to track for 14 days** — Practical change: sleep window, snoring, periods/bleeding, mood, meds list.  
7. **When to seek care** — Telehealth PC appropriate vs ER/red flags.  
8. **CA/telehealth availability** — Honest: licensed states; not fake city SEO.  
9. **FAQs** (8–12) — iron, thyroid, apnea, burnout, ADHD, labs orderable online.  
10. **Related entities** — Brain fog, iron, thyroid, sleep apnea answer, midlife, ADHD screening.  
11. **CTA** — Book Meet & Greet / Primary care; optional ADHD screening if lifelong.
