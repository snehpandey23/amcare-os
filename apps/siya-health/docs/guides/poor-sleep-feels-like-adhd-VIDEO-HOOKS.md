# Video hooks: Poor Sleep Feels Like ADHD

Short-form scripts (30–60s). End card: “Full Health Guide — link in bio” → `/answers/poor-sleep-feels-like-adhd`.

---

## Script A — “ADHD or sleep?”

**Open (0–3s):** “You don’t need more coffee—you might need a sleep workup.”  
**Beat 1:** List: brain fog, snappy mood, can’t finish tasks.  
**Beat 2:** “That’s ADHD *and* bad sleep on the same checklist.”  
**Beat 3:** Snoring / unrefreshing sleep → apnea screen. Lifelong chaos since kid → ADHD eval.  
**Close:** “We broke it down in a free Health Guide—Meet & Greet if you want a real plan.”

---

## Script B — Brain fog

**Open:** “Brain fog isn’t a diagnosis—it’s a clue.”  
**Middle:** Sleep debt vs apnea vs mood vs thyroid vs ADHD.  
**Close:** “Start with sleep history. Link to guide below.”

---

## Script C — Stimulant safety angle

**Open:** “Thinking about ADHD meds? Ask about sleep first.”  
**Middle:** Untreated apnea + stimulants = risk conversation with your clinician.  
**Close:** Educational guide—not medical advice. Book a Meet & Greet.
