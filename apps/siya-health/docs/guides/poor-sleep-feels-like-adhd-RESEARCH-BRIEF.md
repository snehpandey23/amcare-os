## Clinical thesis

Poor sleep quantity, poor sleep quality (fragmentation), and circadian misalignment produce attention lapses, irritability, working-memory strain, and subjective “brain fog” that overlap with adult ADHD presentation. Differential diagnosis requires sleep history, apnea screening, developmental ADHD history, and comorbidity assessment—not symptom checklists alone.
