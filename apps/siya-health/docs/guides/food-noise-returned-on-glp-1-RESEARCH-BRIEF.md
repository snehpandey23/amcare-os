## Clinical thesis

“Food noise”—intrusive thoughts about eating—often quiets when GLP-1 therapy is working, but it can return if the dose is reduced or stopped, if adherence lapses, if stress and poor sleep spike, or if underlying habits and environment were never addressed. GLP-1 medicines blunt appetite signaling; they do not erase emotional eating, binge patterns, or metabolic drivers alone. Return of food noise …
