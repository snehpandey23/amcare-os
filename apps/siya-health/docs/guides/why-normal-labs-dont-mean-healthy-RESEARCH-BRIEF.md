## Clinical thesis

“Normal labs” usually means values fall inside population reference ranges—not that you feel well, sleep well, or are free of early metabolic strain. Insulin resistance, obstructive sleep apnea, subclinical thyroid shifts, iron deficiency, depression, and medication effects can all cause fatigue, brain fog, cravings, and weight gain while routine panels look acceptable.
