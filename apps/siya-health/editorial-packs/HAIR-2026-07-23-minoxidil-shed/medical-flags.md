# Medical flags — HAIR-2026-07-23-minoxidil-shed

**Publish organic?** YES with educational disclaimer

## Safe
- Early shedding *can* happen; does not happen to everyone; experiences vary
- Follicle cycle shift framing (educational, not guarantee of new growth)
- Typical timelines as ranges — not promises
- Encourage clinician contact for persistent/worsening loss, irritation, unwanted hair, other symptoms
- Hair loss isn't one-size-fits-all; evaluate cause before product choice
- Physician-led telehealth · licensed clinicians
- Positioning: trusted source — **not** product promotion / not selling a specific minoxidil brand

## Avoid
- Guaranteed regrowth / density / “it means it's working” as universal claim
- Instructing patients to continue/stop without clinician advice
- Before/after miracle imagery
- Shame for shedding or pattern hair loss
- Product SKU / brand package promotion
- Meet & Greet / Find the Right Starting Point / Start Here / Join Waitlist / Book with {Name}
- Siya Circle as primary CTA
- Psychiatry practice positioning
