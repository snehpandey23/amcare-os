## Caption

Early shedding after starting minoxidil can feel alarming — and social feeds often amplify that fear.

Clinically, some people notice temporary shedding early as follicles shift growth cycles. It doesn't happen to everyone. Timelines for assessing response are often measured in months, not weeks.

Don't rely only on social media. Persistent or worsening loss, scalp irritation, unwanted hair growth, or other concerning symptoms deserve a clinician's review.

Hair loss isn't one-size-fits-all. Evaluation first — product second.
