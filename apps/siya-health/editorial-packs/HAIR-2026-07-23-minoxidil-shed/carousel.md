### Slide 2 — What Can Happen
Sometimes, increased shedding can happen early in treatment. Minoxidil may shift hair follicles into a new growth cycle. As a result, some older hairs may shed before new hairs begin to grow. This doesn't happen to everyone, and experiences vary.

**Visual:** Timeline — Old Hair → Shedding → New Growth.

### Slide 3 — What to Expect
Hair growth takes time. Typical timelines can include:  
• 2–8 weeks: Some people notice temporary shedding  
• 3–6 months: Early improvement may begin  
• 6–12 months: Better assessment of treatment response

**Visual:** Clean horizontal timeline with icons.

### Slide 4 — When to Talk to Your Healthcare Provider
Don't rely only on social media. If you experience:  
• Persistent or worsening hair loss  
• Scalp irritation  
• Unwanted hair growth  
• Other concerning symptoms  
A healthcare professional can help determine whether your treatment should be adjusted.

**Visual:** Physician consultation + subtle checklist.
