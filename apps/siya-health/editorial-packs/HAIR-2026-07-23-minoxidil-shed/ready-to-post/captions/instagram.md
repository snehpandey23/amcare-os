## Caption

Your hair started shedding after Minoxidil... Does that mean it's not working?

Sometimes early shedding can happen. Minoxidil may shift follicles into a new growth cycle — so some older hairs shed before newer ones grow. This doesn't happen to everyone, and experiences vary.

Hair growth takes time. Many people need months — not days — before judging response. Don't rely only on social media. If shedding persists or worsens, or you notice scalp irritation, unwanted hair growth, or other concerning symptoms, talk to a healthcare professional.

Hair loss isn't one-size-fits-all. The right treatment starts with understanding why you're losing hair — not just choosing a product.
