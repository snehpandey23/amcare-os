# Verified vs inferred findings

| Class | Examples | Owner |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Verified** | Duplicate paragraphs, wrong clinical block on wrong topic, garbled prose, CTA counts, link caps, geo directories, schema/H1/canonical defects, ARIA id collisions, missing alt | Engineering + scanners |
| **Inferred** | Abrupt flow, visual balance, tone drift, “feels templated,” whether a city page deserves to exist | Editorial review |

Rule: Verified items close with automated tests. Inferred items need human sign-off.
