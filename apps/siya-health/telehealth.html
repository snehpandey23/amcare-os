Common reasons patients book visits

If any of these sound like your situation, a virtual visit may be the right next step.

Urgent Care

Feeling sick and need help today? Colds, sinus infections, UTIs, rashes, headaches, and other common concerns.

Prescriptions

Running low on a chronic medication or need a new prescription reviewed? Hair loss, ED, birth control, and ongoing refills—when clinically appropriate.

Mental Health

Anxiety, depression, or stress affecting daily life? Evidence-based evaluation and treatment from home.

ADHD

Wondering if ADHD explains your focus struggles? Evaluation and medication management with same-week availability.

Sleep Disorders

Not sleeping well or dragging through the day? Insomnia, shift work disorder, excessive sleepiness, and sleep apnea evaluation.

Labs & Blood Tests

Men's & Women's Health

Chronic Disease Management

Managing diabetes, hypertension, or another ongoing condition? Regular follow-up and medication adjustments without office visits.
