Does This Sound Familiar?

Adult ADHD in adults does not always look like hyperactivity. Many people describe it as feeling capable but inconsistent.

Trouble focusing

You start with good intentions, but your mind keeps jumping elsewhere.

Forgetfulness

You miss texts, appointments, deadlines, or small details.

Procrastination

You delay tasks until pressure makes them urgent.

Time blindness

You underestimate how long things take or feel like you are always running behind.

Mental overload

Everyday tasks feel harder than they should.

Emotional ups and downs

You feel easily frustrated, reactive, or overwhelmed.
