A Message From Dr. Sneh Pandey

Medical Director on stigma, thorough evaluation, and long-term ADHD care.

Dr. Sneh Pandey, MD · Medical Director · ADHD-CCSP · Internal Medicine · Obesity Medicine. Many adults struggle for years before seeking an ADHD evaluation. For too long, symptoms are misunderstood as laziness, lack of motivation, or simply not trying hard enough.

Attention and executive function difficulties often affect school, work, relationships, and self-confidence in ways that compound over time. A thorough evaluation matters—not because every patient needs the same tests, but because your history, symptoms, and goals are unique.

Treatment is not just medication. When clinically appropriate, care may include lifestyle strategies, therapy coordination, sleep support, and ongoing follow-up—with medication considered only when it aligns with your clinical picture.

Our goal is lasting improvement in quality of life and day-to-day functioning. That starts with being heard, being evaluated carefully, and building a plan you can follow long term.
