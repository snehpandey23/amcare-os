How Our ADHD Evaluation Works

A clear clinical path from first conversation to a plan you can follow.

  1. Structured clinical interview Your history, symptoms, and how daily life is affected—reviewed with a licensed physician.
  2. Validated assessment tools Used when clinically appropriate to support—not replace—clinical judgment.
  3. Physician review & diagnosis when appropriate Findings explained in plain language. Diagnosis is never assumed from a screening score alone.
  4. Personalized treatment planning Options may include lifestyle strategies, therapy coordination, testing, medication, or further evaluation.
  5. Ongoing follow-up Monitoring and adjustments when care continues—so progress is tracked over time.

Every evaluation is individualized. Medication is discussed only when clinically appropriate and is never guaranteed.
