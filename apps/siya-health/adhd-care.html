How our ADHD evaluation model works

Structured, clinician-led assessment—not a standalone psychiatry or psychology practice.

DSM-based diagnostic evaluation

Your clinician applies DSM-5-TR ADHD criteria using structured clinical judgment—not questionnaires alone.

Validated tools (as appropriate)

Your clinician may use one or more validated assessment tools as clinically appropriate. Tools may include ASRS, DIVA, Wender Utah Rating Scale, SWAN, Creyos, and other methods. Assessment tools support clinical evaluation but do not independently establish a diagnosis.

Functional impairment screening

We assess real-world impact—including work, relationships, and digital distraction patterns—not just checklist scores.

Severity tracking & documentation

Baseline and follow-up tracking support treatment rationale, informed consent, and ongoing medication management when clinically appropriate.

Siya delivers ADHD care through a primary care–led model with licensed medical providers. Every patient does not receive every tool—your clinician selects what is clinically appropriate for your history and presentation.
