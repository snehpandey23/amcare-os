# Cookie Policy

**Effective: October 31, 2025**

## Overview

This Cookie Policy describes how Siya Health Inc. and Siya Healthcare, PLLC (“Siya Health,” “we,” “us”) use cookies and similar technologies on **siya.health**. This policy supplements our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).

We do **not** operate a fully functional consent-management platform (CMP) on this site. We provide this notice for transparency. You may control cookies through your browser settings.

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, and tags used for analytics, advertising, security, and site functionality.

## Technologies we use

We may use the following categories of cookies and similar technologies:

### Strictly necessary / site functionality

Cookies required for basic site operation, security, form routing, and remembering simple preferences (for example, dismissing our cookie notice).

### Analytics

- **Google Tag Manager (GTM)** — tag management container that may load other scripts.
- **Google Analytics / GA4** — helps us understand how visitors use the site (pages viewed, approximate geography, device type).

### Advertising

- **Google Ads** — conversion and remarketing tags that help measure ad performance and reach interested audiences.

### Third-party forms & chat

- **LeadConnector / GoHighLevel (GHL)** — booking widgets, chat widgets, and form embeds may set cookies or use similar storage for session management, attribution, and form functionality when you interact with scheduling or intake tools linked from our site.

## How we use these technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

- Operate and secure the website
- Measure site performance and traffic patterns
- Understand which pages are useful to visitors
- Support advertising measurement (Google Ads)
- Enable external booking and chat tools (LeadConnector / GHL) when you choose to use them

## Your choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block third-party cookies, clear stored cookies, or receive alerts before cookies are placed.

- [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)
- [Google Ads personalization settings](https://adssettings.google.com/)

Blocking some cookies may affect site functionality (for example, form widgets or saved preferences).

## Cookie notice on this site

We display a simple, non-blocking banner:

> “We use cookies and similar technologies for site functionality, analytics, and advertising. See our Cookie Policy.”

If you click **Accept**, we store your acknowledgment in **localStorage** on your device. This does not block access to the site.
