A Typical Care Journey

Illustrative example — not an individual patient’s story. This narrative is a composite walkthrough for employers. It is separate from the verified, attributed patient reviews below.

  1. First login

    Someone on your team signs in through the employer-branded login, gets verified against your roster, and books with your dedicated providers — at zero cost to them.

  2. Intake & cognitive baseline

    They complete a real intake and objective cognitive testing, so the first visit starts with a clear baseline — not guesswork from a rushed chat.

  3. Provider evaluation & plan

    They meet the care team (usually Wendy for scheduled visits, with Dr. Pandey supervising and available when things get complex). Together they build a plan that can include primary care, ADHD/mental health support, metabolic or hormonal work, and a sensible follow-up rhythm.

  4. Ongoing management

    Check-ins keep going, and the care team helps with pharmacy coordination, prior authorization, and lab routing — so the plan doesn’t stall between visits.
