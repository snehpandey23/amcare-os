FAQ

Is medication mandatory for ADHD in adults?

No respectable clinician frames stimulants as mandatory universal truth. Adults may pursue structured behavioral scaffolding, psychotherapy integration, occupational accommodations, metabolic stability first lines—medications clinically appropriate selectively—not morally coerced hurriedly nor promised instantly.

Does ADHD telehealth California differ from psychiatric office cadence realistically?

ADHD telehealth California services range from continuity-rich medication management ladders to psychotherapy-forward coaching hybrids. Structure matters—not brand labels alone. Transparent practices articulate visit pacing, escalation triggers, refill policies explicitly—versus opaque subscription churn.

Should Californians prioritize coach apps over clinicians?

Coaching adjuncts optionally fine—but diagnosing, differential medical screening, prescribing, controlled substance stewardship remain licensed clinician realms legally. Avoid conflating productivity influencers with HIPAA-accountable prescribing accountability.

Will treatment plans mention sleep, nutrition, menstrual cycles?

Whole-person scaffolding addresses sleep architecture, caffeine timing interplay, hormonal shifts exacerbating distractibility sometimes, iron status occasionally—clinical judgment decides testing—not TikTok anemia paranoia melodrama though.
