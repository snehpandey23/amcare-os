Online ADHD Diagnosis in Texas: Cost, Process & What to Expect

You’ve been putting off getting evaluated for years. Maybe you’ve told yourself it’s not that bad, or you’ll figure it out. But the missed deadlines, the half-finished projects, and the constant mental fog have a name—and there’s a clear path forward.

If you’re in Texas and wondering whether an online ADHD evaluation is right for you, you’re not alone. More adults than ever are turning to telehealth for ADHD diagnosis and treatment. Here’s what you need to know.

What Does an Online ADHD Diagnosis Cost in Texas?

Pricing varies widely—from a few hundred dollars for a basic evaluation to over $1,000 with some traditional clinics. At Siya Health, our comprehensive ADHD evaluation is $149, and that includes the full 60–90 minute visit with a board-certified provider.

Why is that important? Because a thorough evaluation isn’t a quick questionnaire. It involves clinical history, standardized screening tools, and ruling out other conditions like anxiety. You deserve a real assessment, not a rubber-stamp.

How the Online ADHD Diagnosis Process Works

The process is straightforward but structured:

  • Step 1: Complete a free screening (2–3 minutes) to see if you qualify for an evaluation.
  • Step 2: Book your evaluation and meet with a licensed provider via secure telehealth.
  • Step 3: Receive a diagnosis (if appropriate), personalized treatment plan, and follow-up options.

All visits happen from home. No driving across town. No waiting rooms.

What to Expect During Your ADHD Evaluation

Expect a real conversation. Your provider will go through your history, your symptoms, and how they affect your daily life. They’ll use validated tools and may screen for anxiety or other conditions that can look like ADHD. At the end, you’ll get a clear answer—and if ADHD is the right diagnosis, a plan that fits your life.

Is Online ADHD Diagnosis Legitimate?

Yes. When conducted by licensed, board-certified providers using the same clinical standards as in-person care, online ADHD diagnosis is valid and widely accepted. Our clinicians follow evidence-based protocols and document everything properly. Insurance isn’t required—we offer transparent pricing so you know what you’re paying upfront.

Getting evaluated online

If you’re in Texas, you can get a structured ADHD evaluation online with a licensed provider. We offer:

  • A brief free ADHD screening to see if evaluation is right for you
  • A $149 full evaluation (60–90 minutes) with a board-certified clinician
  • Optional follow-up plans starting at $79/month, with controlled-medication follow-up at $149/month when clinically appropriate (see pricing)

Appointments are often available within days. Evening and weekend slots fill quickly.

Privacy, convenience, and clarity—without the wait.

FAQ

How long does an online ADHD evaluation take?

A thorough evaluation takes 60–90 minutes. Quick online “assessments” that give you a result in 5 minutes are not the same as a proper clinical evaluation.

Can I use insurance for online ADHD diagnosis in Texas?

We operate with transparent, cash-based pricing. Many patients prefer this because there are no surprise bills or prior-authorization delays.

Is the diagnosis valid for prescriptions?

Yes. Our providers are licensed in Texas and can prescribe ADHD medication when clinically appropriate.

What if I’m not sure I have ADHD?

That’s exactly what the free ADHD screening is for. You can ask questions and decide whether a full evaluation makes sense for you.
