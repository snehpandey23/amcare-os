Why Siya Health Exists

Dr. Pandey is board-certified in Internal Medicine and Obesity Medicine. He is licensed in California, Texas, Florida, and Pennsylvania. He's practiced across inpatient and outpatient care, and has treated thousands of patients—including 5,000+ in medical weight loss. That experience shaped how he thinks about care.

Modern healthcare often prioritizes volume, billing, and scale over time, thoughtfulness, and patients. It affects everyone—patients who feel rushed, and providers who feel they can't practice the way they were trained.

Siya Health was built to be different: more time with patients, clear evidence-based decisions, and no unnecessary system friction.
