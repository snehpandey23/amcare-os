You're not failing. Your symptoms deserve a real workup.

Many adults arrive after years of being told to "try harder." Fatigue, focus problems, weight changes, and hormone shifts often overlap—and primary care waiting rooms rarely have time to connect the dots.

Siya Health is physician-led telehealth: licensed clinicians take structured histories, use validated tools when clinically appropriate, and explain what they see before recommending treatment. Screening is not diagnosis. Evaluation does not guarantee medication.

We serve adults in California, Texas, Pennsylvania, and Florida through Siya Healthcare, PLLC clinicians. Siya Health Inc. provides administrative support—you receive care from licensed providers.

Why Siya Health Exists

Dr. Sneh Pandey, MD is board-certified in Internal Medicine and Obesity Medicine. He is licensed in California, Texas, Pennsylvania, and Florida. He's practiced across inpatient and outpatient care, and has treated thousands of patients—including 5,000+ in medical weight loss. That experience shaped how he thinks about care.
