Free ADHD Screening

This screening uses the WHO Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale (ASRS v1.1), a validated tool used by clinicians worldwide. It takes about 2 minutes.

Please think about how you have felt and conducted yourself over the past 6 months when answering.
