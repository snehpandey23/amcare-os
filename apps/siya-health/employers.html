What we kept seeing

People came in because focus was slipping. An ADHD questionnaire, the ASRS, did not settle it. The same complaint showed up with sleep, stress, pain, alcohol or other substances, and with a calendar that leaves no time. A screen is a questionnaire. The visit is where the picture gets sorted.

Here is what one employee’s first month can look like. Memory and attention start low. Stress (PSS), sleep quality (PSQI), and mood and anxiety (PHQ-9, GAD-7) start high. After a month on a plan made with the clinician — breathing, mindfulness, food, movement, and medication only if it is prescribed — those marks can move. Open the Creyos demo.
