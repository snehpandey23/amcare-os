What we kept seeing

What we've seen across 2,700+ patients treated at Siya is a consistent pattern among working professionals: people come in unable to get through their work — not productive, fatigued, unmotivated, unable to hold focus through meetings and tasks. Most don't know if that's ADHD, depression, stress, or nothing clinical at all. We don't start from a questionnaire — we test. A full clinical evaluation determines what's actually driving it, and from there we treat it specifically.

What the research shows

These figures are from published studies of employed adults. Each figure is one study. None of them is Siya’s patient data.

35 extra lost workdays a year

Workers with ADHD, compared with workers without it, in a U.S. national sample.

Kessler et al., Journal of Occupational and Environmental Medicine, 2005. Overall figure is 35 days. In that same study, professional jobs were about 12 days and blue-collar jobs about 56. PubMed 15951716
$4,336 lost performance per worker, per year

In one manufacturer’s workforce, employees with ADHD also had about a 4–5% lower performance score, about twice the odds of sick leave, and about twice the odds of a workplace accident.

Kessler et al., Psychological Medicine, 2009. Psychological Medicine
$90–118 billion a year

U.S. absenteeism and presenteeism tied to psychological distress, across the workforce in 2021. Injury, turnover, disability, and early retirement are not in this total.

Asfaw, Alterman, and Pana-Cryan, American Journal of Industrial Medicine, 2026.
Lost performance at work

Drop in at-work productivity loss for a work-focused depression program, versus usual care, at about four months. Absence days fell 53% in the program versus 13% with usual care. The bars are the size of that improvement, not a month-by-month curve.

What we're aiming for

What we're building at Siya: longitudinal monitoring, care coordination, and early detection and treatment, aimed at the same kind of outcomes this research points to — fewer sick days, less absenteeism, better ability to meet deadlines. This is our model and our hypothesis for how whole-person care translates to the workplace — not yet Siya-specific outcomes data.
