Symptoms adults experience

Many adults live for years with untreated ADHD symptoms. Our clinicians provide structured evaluations to determine whether ADHD may be contributing to difficulties with focus, organization, or productivity.

Common adult ADHD symptoms

  • Difficulty focusing or finishing tasks
  • Chronic disorganization and forgetfulness
  • Impulsivity or restlessness
  • Challenges with time management
  • Struggles with planning and prioritization

What we provide

  • Adult ADHD evaluation and diagnosis
  • Telehealth ADHD assessment and screening tools
  • Medication management with ongoing follow-up
  • Care coordination with primary care providers
