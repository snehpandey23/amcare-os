## 1. Clinical definition

**Insulin resistance (IR)** = reduced biological response to insulin in target tissues (muscle, liver, adipose), requiring higher insulin concentrations to maintain normoglycemia. It is a *physiologic state*, not a single lab diagnosis code.

- **Compensatory hyperinsulinemia** often precedes overt hyperglycemia for years.
- **Prediabetes** (ADA 2025): A1C 5.7–6.4%, FPG 100–125 mg/dL, or 2-h OGTT 140–199 mg/dL.
- **Type 2 diabetes** develops when β-cell compensation fails.

ADA 2025 notes that early T2D may show “normal or elevated” insulin with relative secretory failure; IR may improve with weight loss, activity, and pharmacotherapy but is **seldom fully normalized**.

## 2. Landmark & guideline evidence

| Source | Finding |
|--------|---------|
| **DPP (NEJM 2002)** | Lifestyle (≥7% weight loss, ≥150 min/wk activity) ↓ diabetes incidence **58%** vs placebo; metformin 31%. |
| **Finnish DPS (NEJM 2001)** | Lifestyle ↓ diabetes **58%** over ~3.2 years in IGT. |
| **DIETFITS (JAMA 2018, Gardner)** | 609 adults: no significant 12-mo weight-loss difference low-fat vs low-carb; **no diet–genotype or diet–insulin secretion interaction**. |
| **Bradley et al. (Diabetologia 2004)** | In insulin-resistant obese women, low-fat, low-carb, and high-protein diets all improved insulin sensitivity; benefits tracked **weight loss**, not macronutrient magic. |
| **Umbrella review (Diabetologia 2021, PMID 34796367)** | Greatest weight loss: VLED/formula replacement; low-carb **not superior** to higher-carb/low-fat for weight in T2D meta-analyses (high GRADE). |
| **ADA Standards 2025** | Prediabetes screening, lifestyle referral, resistance training with weight-loss pharmacotherapy, CGM consideration in T2D on non-insulin agents. |
| **Visceral fat meta-analysis (Sci Rep 2016)** | Strongest HOMA-IR correlation: **visceral fat mass** (r≈0.57), then total fat, BMI, WC. |
| **IRAS Family Study (PMC 2022)** | VAT and HOMA-IR predict incident metabolic syndrome **independent of BMI category**. |
| **Sleep RCT meta-analysis (Sleep Med Rev 2022)** | Sleep manipulation affects insulin sensitivity markers. |
| **Single-night partial sleep deprivation (JCEM 2010)** | ~19–25% ↓ insulin sensitivity (hepatic + peripheral). |
| **Chronic mild restriction in women (Diabetes Care 2023)** | ~6.2 h/night × 6 wk → **14.8%** ↑ insulin resistance (20.1% postmenopausal). |
| **Non-obese IR weight loss (PubMed 30497926)** | ~4.3 kg loss improved IR and CV risk markers; waist/adipocyte size predicted improvement. |
| **ADHD → T2D (MR 2024)** | Causal pathway largely **mediated by BMI**; impulsive eating as plausible mechanism. |
| **ADHD outpatients metabolic syndrome (PMC 2022)** | Elevated MS/IR prevalence vs expectations. |

## 3. Mechanisms (patient-relevant)

1. **Visceral/ectopic fat** → lipotoxicity, inflammation, impaired insulin signaling (liver/muscle).
2. **Hyperinsulinemia** → favors fat storage, blunts lipolysis, can drive hunger/cravings after glycemic swings.
3. **Weight-loss plateau biology** → metabolic adaptation, lean mass loss, adherence fatigue (not “broken metabolism” alone).
4. **Sleep/stress** → cortisol, sympathetic tone, appetite hormones; independent of weight in short-term sleep trials.
5. **ADHD overlap** → impulsivity, irregular meals, evening rebound eating, stimulant appetite effects, sleep debt—not separate from IR biology.

## 4. Patient discourse synthesis (Reddit, Quora, PAA themes)

### Common misconceptions
- “My A1C is normal, so I can’t have insulin resistance.”
- “Insulin resistance only matters if I have diabetes.”
- “I must go keto/low-carb because I’m insulin resistant.”
- “Insulin resistance is caused by eating sugar” (oversimplified; energy balance + adiposity dominate).
- “Fasting insulin / HOMA-IR is a perfect home test” (lab variability, no universal cutoffs).
- “Skin tags or acanthosis nigricans = diagnosis without labs.”
- “Supplements (berberine, cinnamon, ACV) replace medical care.”
- “If I can’t lose weight, my metabolism is permanently damaged.”

### Common symptoms (reported + clinical correlates)
- Waist gain / central adiposity
- Fatigue, brain fog **1–3 hours after meals** (postprandial slump—multifactorial: glycemic load, sleep, reactive hypoglycemia in subset)
- Strong carb/sweet cravings, urgency to eat
- **Food noise** (persistent food preoccupation; overlaps GLP-1 experience—link food-noise article)
- Difficulty losing weight despite “doing everything right”
- Skin tags, acanthosis nigricans (suggestive, not diagnostic alone)
- Elevated BP, triglycerides, low HDL trending over years
- PCOS, fatty liver mentions in forums

### Frequently asked questions (PAA-style)
- What is insulin resistance in simple terms?
- Can you have insulin resistance without diabetes?
- Can A1C be normal with insulin resistance?
- Why is it hard to lose weight with insulin resistance?
- What is visceral fat?
- Is prediabetes the same as insulin resistance?
- Does insulin resistance cause fatigue after eating?
- How does sleep affect insulin resistance?
- Does ADHD make weight loss harder?
- What labs should I ask for?
- Will metformin help weight loss?
- Do GLP-1 drugs fix insulin resistance?

### Where clinicians under-explain
- Difference between **prediabetes labs** and **hyperinsulinemia** before A1C shifts
- Why **weight loss magnitude** matters more than diet brand names
- **Visceral fat** vs scale weight
- **Maintenance** after loss (DPP Outcomes Study: benefits persist but require ongoing habits)
- **ADHD** as modifiable driver of eating pattern, not moral failure
- When **pharmacotherapy** (metformin, GLP-1) fits evidence vs lifestyle-first
- Limits of fasting insulin / HOMA-IR in primary care
