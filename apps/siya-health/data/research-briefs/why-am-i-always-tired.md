## B. Common misconceptions

| Misconception | Reality |
|---------------|---------|
| “Normal CBC/CMP = no medical cause” | Iron without anemia, B12, thyroid, OSA, IR often missed on basic panels |
| “7 hours in bed = 7 hours sleep” | Sleep apnea, insomnia, ADHD delayed phase fragment sleep |
| “Fatigue = depression only” | Medical, sleep, endocrine, and occupational causes are common |
| “Burnout and ME/CFS are the same” | Burnout is occupational (ICD-11 QD85); ME/CFS has specific criteria (post-exertional malaise) |
| “ADHD fatigue = laziness” | Executive load, sleep disorders, circadian delay, medication rebound |
| “More coffee fixes tiredness” | Masks sleep debt; worsens anxiety/insomnia cycle |
| “Detox / adrenal fatigue supplements” | “Adrenal fatigue” not recognized in endocrine guidelines |
| “Always tired = need testosterone” | Low T is one piece; requires symptoms + confirmed morning labs |
| “If I exercise more I’ll have energy” | Overtraining worsens fatigue; sleep apnea limits recovery |

## C. Frequently missed diagnoses

1. **Obstructive sleep apnea** — ~80% undiagnosed (AASM indicator report); fatigue without classic snoring in some patients  
2. **Iron deficiency without anemia** — ferritin &lt;50 µg/L + fatigue responds in RCTs (CMAJ 2012; meta-analysis PMID 29626044)  
3. **Vitamin B12 deficiency** — unexplained fatigue, neuropathy, cognitive fog (NICE NG239 2024)  
4. **Hypothyroidism** — especially subclinical; TSH context matters  
5. **Insulin resistance / prediabetes** — post-meal slump, normal A1C early  
6. **ADHD + sleep disorder overlap** — insomnia, delayed phase, OSA prevalence  
7. **Depression / anxiety** — anergia, insomnia, hyperarousal  
8. **Medication effects** — antihistamines, beta-blockers, SSRIs, gabapentinoids, sedating meds  
9. **Low testosterone (men)** — nonspecific symptoms; overlap sleep apnea  
10. **ME/CFS** — if post-exertional malaise ≥24h, unrefreshing sleep, cognitive PEM (IOM/NIH criteria)

---

## D. Conditions commonly mistaken for each other

| Pair | How to separate |
|------|-----------------|
| Sleepiness vs fatigue | Sleepiness = doze risk (ESS); fatigue = low energy despite rest (FSS) |
| Burnout vs depression | Burnout job-linked cynicism/efficacy; depression pervasive anhedonia |
| ADHD vs burnout | ADHD lifelong cross-context; burnout follows chronic workplace stress |
| ADHD vs hypothyroid | Labs + developmental history |
| OSA vs depression | Snoring, witnessed apneas, resistant HTN; sleep study |
| IR vs “sugar crash” | Pattern + metabolic labs, waist trend |
| ME/CFS vs depression | PEM worsens after exertion; orthostatic symptoms |
| Low T vs sleep apnea | Both cause fatigue; screen apnea before TRT |

---

## E. Misleading online advice

- Universal “thyroid panel” without symptoms or exam  
- HOMA-IR DIY diagnosis from one fasting insulin  
- Mega-dose B vitamins without deficiency  
- Ashwagandha/adrenal cocktails for fatigue  
- Skipping sleep study because “I don’t snore loud”  
- Stimulant sourcing without ADHD/sleep evaluation  
- ME/CFS treated with graded exercise alone (historical harm; pacing emphasized now)

## Red flags (when to seek urgent care)

- Sudden severe fatigue with chest pain, shortness of breath, syncope  
- Fatigue + unexplained weight loss, night sweats, fever  
- New severe headache, focal neuro deficits  
- Suicidal ideation with profound fatigue  
- Suspected sleep apnea with drowsy driving
