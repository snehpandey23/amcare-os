## Caption

Started minoxidil and noticing more shedding? That can feel scary — and it doesn't automatically mean treatment isn't working.

Some people experience temporary early shedding as follicles enter a new growth cycle. Experiences vary. Hair growth takes time (often months).

If shedding persists or worsens — or you have scalp irritation, unwanted hair growth, or other concerns — talk to a healthcare professional instead of relying only on social media.
