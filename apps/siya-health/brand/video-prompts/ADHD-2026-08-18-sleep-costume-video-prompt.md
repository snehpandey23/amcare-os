### Spoken script (Siya angle — paraphrased)

```text
You got eight hours. You still can’t think.
That’s not automatically ADHD.
Hours in bed are not the same as restored sleep — and unrestored sleep can wear an ADHD costume: fog, irritability, re-reading the same line.
If a long weekend actually returns your focus, start with sleep — snoring, unrefreshing nights, a crash after nights went bad.
You can have both. Treating sleep first doesn’t mean ADHD isn’t real. It means the order matters.
Educational only. siya.health
```
