## Full script (VO) — paste into HeyGen

It’s okay if you clean or pace when you can’t sit still with a racing mind — restlessness in ADHD doesn’t always look like bouncing off the walls.

It’s okay if you need something on in the background just to open one email — task initiation often needs external activation, not another lecture about discipline.

It’s okay if “five minutes” of scrolling becomes forty while the real task waits — that’s a stuck start meeting time blindness, not not caring.

It’s okay if you only move when the deadline is already on fire — starting early is hard when the urgency hasn’t arrived yet.

It’s okay if a full to-do list makes you do less, not more — when prioritization and working memory overload, everything feels equally loud.

Those aren’t always character flaws. For a lot of people with ADHD — especially women recognized late — they’re how executive function shows up in ordinary life.

We wrote more about this — the link is in the caption. Education, not a diagnosis.
