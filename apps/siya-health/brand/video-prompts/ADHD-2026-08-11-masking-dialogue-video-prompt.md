## Full script (approved)

**A (partner):** Why do you think it took so long to get diagnosed?  
**B:** Probably because I always got good grades.  
**A:** Okay… but what about the deadlines you barely hit? Being late? Losing things?  
**B:** I just got called lazy. Or “not trying hard enough.” That didn’t count, apparently.  
**A:** Or it counted as the wrong story. What if the grades were last-minute panic and adrenaline — not proof everything was fine?  
**B:** …Yeah. I got really good at looking fine.  
**A:** So the outside looked successful.  
**B:** And the inside was exhausted. Masking isn’t fake. It’s expensive.  
**A:** So late diagnosis isn’t you suddenly becoming a problem.  
**B:** It’s people finally seeing the cost.

**Close (B to camera, 5–8s)**  
If this sounds familiar, you’re not alone. A calm next step beats more self-blame — a free Meet & Greet when you’re ready. Not a diagnosis. Not a hard sell.
