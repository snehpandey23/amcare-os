## Guardrails

- No AI avatar / synthetic talking head as the presenter (**Track A**)  
- **One text layer** — Format 2: no auto-captions; Format 1: supers **or** auto-captions, never both  
- **Pronunciation:** “Siya” = “C-ya” / “see ya” (SEE-yah) — not SAI-ya / SIGH-ya / SAH-ya. Spoken/VO/TTS lines use phonetic **See-ya Health**; on-screen stays Siya / siya.health. Prefer URL close when possible.  
- No near-verbatim transcription of research sources (angle ≠ words)  
- No textbook/listicle framing (“5 types of…”) as the spine  
- Public figures: only publicly disclosed facts; never invent quotes  
- No medication guarantees / cure language  
- No DIY dosing or unapproved protocols  
- Match clinical confidence of the paired research (association ≠ causation when relevant)  
- Company voice; no Founder LinkedIn CTA unless requested  
- On-screen text must pass spokenness read-aloud test (complete natural phrasing)  
- Format 2: **every beat has concrete Visual / B-roll** (non-negotiable)
