## Suggested VO
“You can feel lonely even when your calendar is full.

Hug someone. Give a real compliment. Help with one small thing. Play a favorite song. Make someone laugh.  
Pick one habit for seven days. Rate how connected you felt.  
Habits help. They don’t replace care.”
