# TRACK B — Founder AI Avatar Guardrails

```text
Status: LOCKED for draft prompts — 2026-08-13
Production (HeyGen / render / ship): OFF until explicit approval
Parents: video-prompts/README.md · FOUNDER-LINKEDIN-VOICE.md (tone kinship only)
```

Track B is **not** anonymous company voice. It is **explicitly the founder** speaking via a disclosed founder AI avatar, mixed with real B-roll.

If the brief does not say founder avatar / Track B → use **Track A**.

---

## When Track B applies

- Brief explicitly requests **founder AI avatar**
- Content is allowed to be founder-attributed (not company-anonymous)
- Viewer must never wonder “is this Siya company or the founder?”

## When Track B does **not** apply

- Default educational carousels / reels → Track A  
- Anonymous company voice → Track A  
- “Just use HeyGen because it’s faster” without founder disclosure → **forbidden**

---

## Disclosure (required)

Every Track B script and on-screen open must make clear this is the **founder** speaking.

Examples (pick one, plain language):

- “I’m Dr. Sneh — founder of See-ya Health…” *(spoken phonetic)*  
- On-screen chip early: `Dr. Sneh · Founder`  

Ambiguous open = fail.

**One text layer:** If using designed on-screen supers, turn **off** HeyGen Captions / editor auto-captions / platform auto-captions. Do not ship two caption styles at once.

---

## Clarity first (hard rule — reviewer fail mode)

**Topic must be stated plainly in the first 2 spoken lines.**

Fail example (real review): a late-ADHD-in-women video that opened on hormones/cycle language and was heard as **PMS**.

Pass pattern:

```text
Line 1: State the topic in plain words (who + what).
Line 2: One clarifying clause so it cannot be heard as a different topic.
Then: recognition / story beats.
```

No clever, delayed, or metaphor-first opens that hide the subject.

---

## Pacing (hard rule — “feels AI” tell)

Reviewers flagged pacing as the #1 AI tell.

Required in every Track B draft:

- **Natural pauses** between sentences (not rushed, not metronome-even)
- **Mixed sentence length** — short + long alternating; no uniform cadence
- **No long unbroken monologue** — break into **short spoken beats** with **B-roll cuts** between them (same beat discipline as Track A Format 2)
- Script laid out as numbered beats with `[PAUSE]` / cut cues where helpful

---

## Expressions / visuals (hard rule — avatar tell)

- Prefer **lower intensity / more natural micro-expressions** if the avatar tool allows tuning (avoid over-animated “presenter mode”)
- Prefer **short avatar-only segments** interspersed with **real B-roll cutaways** — not long continuous avatar talking-head shots
- Mix: avatar → B-roll → avatar → B-roll (hides avatar tells; matches “mix real footage + avatar”)
- No Ken Burns of the carousel as the whole reel

---

## Tone — not an ad (hard rule)

Reviewers: “reads as an ad.”

- Avoid polished sales transitions (“Here’s the thing…” / “That’s why we built…”)
- Avoid stacking soft pitch language before the close
- CTA = **genuine aside**, soft and brief — same TOF standard as Track A (blog / learn more — not hard Meet & Greet by default)
- Educational first; commercial ask earned later-funnel only

---

## Pronunciation

Same as Track A:

| Surface | Write |
|---------|--------|
| On-screen | Siya Health / siya.health |
| Spoken / TTS / avatar VO | **See-ya Health** (phonetic) |

Wrong: SAI-ya / SIGH-ya / SAH-ya.

---

## Clinical / brand guardrails (carry over)

- Educational only — does not diagnose the viewer  
- No invented stats · no medication promises / DIY dosing  
- Angle from research ≠ verbatim source words  
- Public figures: only publicly known facts  
- Match clinical confidence (association ≠ causation when relevant)  
- Spokenness: lines must sound like something a person would say aloud
