## Why not the 14-slide ChatGPT trial script

| ChatGPT | Siya cut |
|---------|---------|
| 14 slides | Retention dies after ~7 |
| Full courtroom chrome | Cute once; then lecture |
| Wi-Fi / Human Genome jokes every frame | Keep **one** punch line max |
| Separate FDA + pharmacy + WADA + verdict + wishlist | Compress into 2 slides |

Keep the **best** ChatGPT insights:

- Tension = interesting preclinical + thin human evidence  
- “Two people” as a visual moment  
- Possibility vs proof  
- Avoid scam / cancer / illegal overclaims

## Recommended: single 7-slide pack

### Slide 1 — Hook (emotion)
**Copy:** You don’t want a peptide.  
**Support:** You want your body back.

### Slide 2 — Temptation
**Copy:** The internet sold you Wolverine.  
**Support:** Tendons · muscles · gut · joints — one vial, every specialty.

### Slide 3 — Respect the science
**Copy:** The biology isn’t nothing.  
**Support:** Animal studies link BPC-157 to blood-vessel and tissue-repair pathways. Interesting ≠ proven.

### Slide 4 — Species problem
**Copy:** Most of the wow is still preclinical.  
**Support:** Rats · mice · cells. Not large human trials with reliable long-term outcomes.

### Slide 5 — Human evidence (punch)
**Copy:** How big is the human file?  
**Support:** A handful of tiny, uncontrolled pilots. One safety report: **two people.** Useful start. Not proof of Wolverine.

### Slide 6 — Fair verdict + hard facts
**Copy:** Promising. Experimental. Not proven.  
**Support:** Not an FDA-approved drug. WADA S0 — prohibited for tested athletes. Clinic menu ≠ clinical proof.

### Slide 7 — Practical change
**Copy:** Three questions before you buy.  
**Support:** Proven in humans for *my* problem? Approved product or gray supply? Athlete / service rules?  
Talk to a Clinician · Health Guide
