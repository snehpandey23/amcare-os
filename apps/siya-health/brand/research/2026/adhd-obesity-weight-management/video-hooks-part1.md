**Pack:** ADHD & weight — cues / hyperfocus / effort (A02–A04)  
**Track:** **A — Company** · calm · unscripted-feeling  
**Format:** **2 — Narration-free** (designed text + B-roll) ← default  
**Platform:** Instagram / general — 2.5–4s steady cuts  
**Canvas:** Reel / Short · 9:16 · 1080×1920 · **10–15s each**  
**Framing lock:** ADHD can make weight management **harder** — **not** “ADHD causes obesity”  
**Excluded:** dopamine-mechanism · BED prevalence · “until medicated” · BMI / scale / before-after  
**Status:** Part 1 — produce & review openers only  

**Part 2 gate:** Do **not** write middle + soft close / blog CTA until one of these 10–15s cuts looks good in chat.

**One text layer:** Designed beat cards only. **No auto-captions** (CapCut / IG / TikTok / export).

**Banned:** AI avatar · Ken Burns of carousel slides · Founder LinkedIn CTA
