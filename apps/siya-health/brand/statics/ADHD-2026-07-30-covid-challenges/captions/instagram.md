COVID made ADHD harder.

Pandemic stress hit focus, emotions, and routines — especially when the scaffolding of normal life disappeared. If things still feel harder to hold together, that’s not a personal failure. Understanding is how support starts.

Practical step: Talk with a clinician about what changed during COVID and what still isn’t working — so support matches the real strain.
