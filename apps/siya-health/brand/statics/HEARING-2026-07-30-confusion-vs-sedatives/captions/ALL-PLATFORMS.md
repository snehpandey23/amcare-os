Confused? Maybe it’s hearing.

When someone seems foggy, agitated, or “not following,” families often reach for calming meds. Untreated hearing loss can look a lot like confusion — missed words, wrong answers, rising anxiety — and sedatives only quiet the surface.

Practical step: Before assuming it’s only anxiety or cognitive decline, ask about a hearing evaluation. Clearer sound can change how someone shows up in conversation.
