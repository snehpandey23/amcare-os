## Caption

3 peptides that influence your brain?

Semax, Selank, and DSIP show up in research conversations about focus, calm, and sleep. Tiny molecules. Loud claims. Uneven evidence.

Hope isn’t proof — peptides need evidence. DSIP is not FDA-approved. “Peptides” isn’t one proven therapy.

**One practical step:** Before trying any named peptide, ask a clinician for evidence grade — what outcome, in whom, and what’s the approval status? Avoid unverified vendors.

Talk to a Clinician if you’re curious about peptide therapy in a medical context.
