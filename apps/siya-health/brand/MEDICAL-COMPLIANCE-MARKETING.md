## Content standards (fail closed)

### Claims
- **Substantiated** — peer-reviewed / labeled evidence. No forum anecdote or unverified stat on-frame or in caption as fact.
- **Balanced** — no comparative medication claims (onset, efficacy, side effects) without Medical Director **+** Legal sign-off.
- **Current** — FDA labeling where applicable.
- **Physician-reviewed** before publish for clinical claims.

### Before / after
- **Prohibited** for prescription-medication content.
- Cosmetic/procedure results require **individual results may vary** + timeline/protocol disclosure.

### Testimonials (v2.0 — Legal-corrected)
- Compensation / **any material connection MUST be disclosed** (FTC). No exemption for patient testimonials.
- No atypical/best-case as “typical.”
- Medical-supervision disclaimer where applicable.
- Verify treatment completion before use.

### Always on Knowledge frames
- Compositor footer: `siya.health · All content for educational purposes only` (do not replace with custom “i | Learn more” treatments).
- Captions: educational · not a diagnosis · social media ≠ clinician visit.

## Claim-risk framework (research → copy)

Tag every clinical/statistical claim **LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH** in the research or `copy/` notes.

| Tag | Meaning | Agent rule |
|-----|---------|------------|
| LOW | Soft recognition, no stat/mechanism | May use after checklist Pass |
| MEDIUM | Clinical framing, monitoring language | Prefer Medical Director eyes before Phase 3 |
| HIGH | Specific stat, “research shows”, medical mechanism, safety/efficacy | **STOP** — route Medical Director before use in copy/slides. Also Legal if §7.3 |

“Parked” without an owner is **not** compliance. HIGH = named stop for Medical Director.

## §7.2 Marketing-content compliance checklist

Run for **every** piece before Phase 1 approval and again before Phase 3 ship.  
Any **Flag** → do not compose / do not ship until Medical Director (and Legal if §7.3) clears it.

Write results into the pack as `compliance/CHECKLIST.md` (required at Phase 3).

| # | Check | Pass / Flag |
|---|--------|-------------|
| 1 | No individualized medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendation | ☐ |
| 2 | No comparative medication claims without Medical Director + Legal sign-off | ☐ |
| 3 | Every clinical/stat claim tagged LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH; no HIGH used without Medical Director review | ☐ |
| 4 | No before/after imagery for prescription medication content | ☐ |
| 5 | Cosmetic/procedure results include individual-results-may-vary disclaimer | ☐ / N/A |
| 6 | Any testimonial discloses compensation / material connection if one exists | ☐ / N/A |
| 7 | Testimonial = typical result + treatment completion verified | ☐ / N/A |
| 8 | Educational disclaimer present and legible (compositor footer + caption) | ☐ |
| 9 | Content does not imply diagnosis from a social media interaction | ☐ |
| 10 | Brand Style Lock + visual approval gate followed | ☐ |

---

## §7.3 Legal Review — required triggers

Legal Review is **required** (not optional judgment) when any apply:

1. Any claim tagged **HIGH**
2. Any comparative medication or treatment claim
3. Any testimonial or endorsement
4. Any before/after or outcome-result content
5. Anyone on the chain is unsure how to classify

Research / mining
