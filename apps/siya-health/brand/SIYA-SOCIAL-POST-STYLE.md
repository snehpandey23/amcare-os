Use this system for **graphic educational posts** (carousels + statics) — the look Siya Health already used for Minoxidil and similar education creatives.

---

## Feel

Premium medical education graphic — clean, modern, trustworthy.  
**Not** a lifestyle photo collage. **Not** a product ad splash.  
Feels like a high-end digital health brand deck slide.

---

## Canvas

- **Aspect:** Instagram 1:1 (1080×1080)
- **Base:** Clean off-white / warm white (`#FAFAFA`–`#F7F5FB`)
- **Atmosphere:** Soft lavender flowing curves / wave lines in the background (subtle, never loud)
- **Corners:** Decorative magenta → purple gradient wave shapes in bottom corners (signature motif)
- **Whitespace:** Generous. Do not crowd.

---

## Color system

| Role | Colors | Use |
|------|--------|-----|
| Navy | `#1B2A4A` / deep navy | Primary body type |
| Purple | `#6B4EFF` → `#7B61FF` | Gradient accents, icons |
| Magenta / Pink | `#E83E8C` → `#C44DFF` | Keyword highlights, waves, slide pills |
| Soft lavender | `#E8DEFF` / `#D4C4FF` | Background curves |
| Off-white | `#FAFAFA` | Canvas |

**Keyword emphasis:** Highlight 1–3 key words with a **pink → purple gradient fill** on otherwise navy type.  
**Dividers:** Short horizontal gradient line (pink → purple) under headlines when useful.

---

## Typography

- Modern, clean **geometric sans-serif** (not serif editorial)
- Strong hierarchy: large headline → short support
- Left-weighted or balanced left/center — never dense paragraphs on image when avoidable
- Carousel body copy may use short stacked sentences; keep simple English

---

## Imagery

- High-quality **3D medical-style illustrations** (hair follicle cross-section, dropper bottle, timeline nodes, icons)
- Soft studio lighting on 3D objects; slight glow OK on medical objects only
- Product bottles only as **generic educational props** (e.g. “Minoxidil Topical Solution”) — not retail ads, not brand SKU selling
- Prefer illustration over photoreal worried-patient stock for this system
- No before/after miracle grids

---

## Logo

- **Top-left corner only** — real Siya Health® mark
- **Never** a large background watermark / submerged center logo
- Leave clear space around the mark

---

## Carousel chrome

- Slide pill bottom-left: rounded capsule, pink→purple gradient fill, white type `1/5`, `2/5`, …
- Consistent margins across all slides in a set
- Same background wave language on every slide

---

## Static variant (opinions / clarity posts)

Same color, type, waves, and 3D medical prop language.  
For two-line clarity posts:

1. Line A (navy + optional gradient on a key word)  
2. **Large vertical gap**  
3. Line B (navy + optional gradient on a key word)  
4. Quiet footer if needed: `siya.health · Educational only`

---

## Do / Don't

**Do**
- Education-first framing
- Gradient keyword highlights sparingly
- 3D follicle / clinical icons for hair posts
- Corner logo + slide pills on carousels

**Don't**
- Photoreal cream-clinic photo backgrounds for this graphic system (those belong to video / photo SOTs)
- Purple full-bleed dark mode
- Loud multi-layer card stacks
- Guaranteed outcomes copy
- Background logo watermarks
