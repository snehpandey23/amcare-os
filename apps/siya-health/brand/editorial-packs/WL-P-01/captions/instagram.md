## Caption

Phentermine — safety basics

Appetite suppression with stimulant-like tradeoffs.

People ask because it’s familiar and can quiet appetite quickly — and because side effects worry them. It can raise heart rate and blood pressure. History matters before the prescription.

**One practical step:** Ask cardiac and blood-pressure screening questions before stimulant-like weight-loss meds. Decide fit with your clinician.

Educational only. Not prescribing advice.
