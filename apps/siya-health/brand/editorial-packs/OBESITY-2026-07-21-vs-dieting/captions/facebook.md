## Post

Medical weight loss vs dieting — what actually changes?

Crash diets often restart the restrict–rebound–shame loop. Biology adapts. Shame doesn’t fix hormones.

Medical evaluation looks at history, comorbidities, mental health, and appropriate tools — which may or may not include medication.

**Practical step:** If you’re in yo-yo cycles, ask about medical evaluation — not another crash diet.
