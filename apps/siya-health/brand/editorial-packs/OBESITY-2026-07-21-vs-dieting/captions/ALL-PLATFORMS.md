## Instagram
Dieting vs medical weight loss

If willpower were enough, yo-yo cycles wouldn’t be so common.

The loop is familiar: restrict → lose → rebound → shame → harsher plan. Biology pushes back through appetite hormones, muscle loss, and metabolic adaptation — that’s not moral failure.

Medical weight loss asks different questions: history, comorbidities, medications, mental health, sustainable tools — not another 7-day detox.

**One practical step:** If you’re yo-yoing, ask about a medical evaluation — not another crash diet.

Educational only. Not prescribing advice.
