## Post

Ambien and sleep medications — risks and benefits.

They can help some people short-term. They are not casual. Alcohol combinations are dangerous. Next-day impairment matters for driving and work.

**Practical step:** Never mix with alcohol. Discuss impairment and use boundaries with your clinician.
