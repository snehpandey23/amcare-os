## Script

**Hook (0–3s)**

**Beat 1 (3–12s)**  
You care. You meant to start.  
Then the evening arrives and the only thing finished is the self-critique.

**Beat 2 (12–20s)**  
That weight isn’t proof you’re careless.  
For many people, the hardest part isn’t the missed task — it’s what they say to themselves after.

**Beat 3 (20–26s)**  
**Try this:** rename the insult once — “I’m stuck on starting.”  
Then one ugly 2-minute step.

**Close (26–30s)**  
If this pattern is lifelong, a validated screener + a clinician conversation beats another night of blame.
