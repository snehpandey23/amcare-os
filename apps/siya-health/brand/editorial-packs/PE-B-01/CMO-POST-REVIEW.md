# CMO post review — PE-B-01 (Alpana brief)

## Verdict
Ship as **evidence-literacy**, not peptide promotion. Alpana structure (cover → Semax → Selank → DSIP → close) is strong; nickname claims (“The Focus/Calm/Sleep Peptide”) and “may help reduce anxiety” were softened to “studied for …” + research-context language.

## Practical change (required)
Before trying any named peptide, ask a clinician for **evidence grade** (outcome · population · FDA status) — never DIY unverified vendor stacks.

## CTA
**Talk to a Clinician** only (closing slide). No Founder LinkedIn. No Start Here / Meet & Greet / Waitlist.
