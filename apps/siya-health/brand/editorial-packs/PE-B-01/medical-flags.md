# Medical flags — PE-B-01

**Publish organic?** YES with educational disclaimer  
**Paid?** Prefer clinical stamp; high woo-adjacent risk (named research peptides)

## Safe
- Tiny signaling molecules are researched for cognition / anxiety / sleep *contexts* — not proven consumer therapies
- Evidence grade varies widely by compound, dose, route, and population
- Tagline locked: “Hope isn’t proof — peptides need evidence.”
- DSIP called out as not FDA-approved
- CTA: Talk to a Clinician · educational only
- Link to evidence-literacy peptides blog; avoid unverified vendors

## Do not add
- Guarantees of focus, calm, sleep, or “mental clarity”
- DIY dosing, stacks, nasal/injection protocols, or sourcing tips
- Vendor / research-chemical recommendations
- Implying Semax / Selank / DSIP are FDA-approved treatments
- Framing as Siya-prescribed peptide therapy products
- Founder LinkedIn CTA (not requested)
- Banned CTAs: Start Here · Meet & Greet · Join Waitlist · Book with {Name} · Siya Circle as primary
