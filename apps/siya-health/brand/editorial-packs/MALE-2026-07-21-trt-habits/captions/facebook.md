On testosterone therapy (TRT)?

The prescription helps when it’s medically indicated — but how you train, eat, sleep, and follow up often decides how you feel day to day.

Five fundamentals we share with patients (educational, not a personal protocol):

1. **Lift** — resistance training 2–4× / week. TRT doesn’t build muscle by itself.  
2. **Protein** — a palm-sized protein source at each meal, especially on training days.  
3. **Sleep** — protect 7+ hours. Poor sleep blunts recovery and mood.  
4. **Vitamin D** — fix it if it’s low. Ask your clinician to check a level before megadosing.  
5. **Labs** — keep follow-ups (hematocrit, levels, safety checks). That’s what makes TRT medical care — not a shortcut.
