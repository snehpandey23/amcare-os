## Post (copy-paste)

TRT is a medical therapy — not a lifestyle brand.

When testosterone replacement is appropriate, outcomes still depend on fundamentals most feeds skip:

→ Resistance training 2–4× / week  
→ Adequate protein across meals  
→ Consistent sleep (7+ hours)  
→ Correcting vitamin D deficiency *after* checking a level  
→ Keeping monitoring labs (hematocrit, levels, safety)

Skipping follow-up is how “optimization” becomes risk.

We published a short educational carousel on these five habits for men already on therapy — and a deeper guide on when TRT is appropriate in the first place.
