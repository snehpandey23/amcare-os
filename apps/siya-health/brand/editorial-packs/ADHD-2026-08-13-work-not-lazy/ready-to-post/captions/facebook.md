At work, “you’re just lazy” lands harder than a missed deadline — especially when you’ve been trying.

What others see is unfinished work. What they miss is the effort. That misread can become a story you tell yourself, and it drains more than it helps.

Laziness is a choice story. Executive dysfunction is a regulation story.
