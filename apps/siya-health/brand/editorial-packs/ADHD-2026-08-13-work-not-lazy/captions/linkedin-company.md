In knowledge work, unfinished output is visible. Effort often isn’t.

Adults living with ADHD-related executive strain frequently hear — or start saying — “I’m just lazy.” That framing is a choice story. Regulation difficulty is a different story: initiation, attention, and the gap between what someone knows they can do and what gets done under workplace load.
