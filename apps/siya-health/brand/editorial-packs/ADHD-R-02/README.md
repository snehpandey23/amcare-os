# ADHD-R-02

Is it ADHD... or addiction? — a Siya Health question-and-answer reel.

**Angle:** Synthesized from the ADHD-vs-addiction research thread (dopamine seeking, tolerance/withdrawal, comorbidity, self-medicating undiagnosed ADHD). Structured as tension-building Q&A: the video ends on an open question, then **Siya Health answers** with the practical next step (get evaluated, don't guess).

**Practical change:** If you can't tell whether it's ADHD or addiction, don't self-diagnose either way — get a proper clinical evaluation.  
**Format shipped:** Avatar-free reel (script-matched B-roll) + HeyGen scene prompt  
**Funnel:** Awareness → consideration → action (evaluation)  
**Audience:** Adults 20–45 with ADHD traits or concerned partners/family
