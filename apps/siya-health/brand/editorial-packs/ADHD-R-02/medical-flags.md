# Medical flags — ADHD-R-02

**Publish organic?** YES with educational disclaimer — **higher sensitivity than ADHD-R-01** (addiction/substance topic).

## Safe
- Framing ADHD-driven dopamine seeking vs. addiction markers (tolerance, withdrawal, compulsive prioritization) as *patterns to notice*, not a self-diagnosis tool
- Citing that ADHD and substance use disorder have documented overlap in the literature (brief, non-numeric — no invented statistics)
- CTA to seek professional evaluation rather than self-diagnose
- Non-judgmental, non-shaming tone toward people experiencing either condition

## Avoid
- Diagnosing addiction or ADHD in the video itself ("you have an addiction" / "you have ADHD")
- Implying medication is a guaranteed fix for cravings or substance use
- Encouraging anyone to start, stop, or change medication or substance use without a clinician
- Glorifying or normalizing substance use — **no alcohol, drug, or gambling imagery on screen**
- Inventing statistics or citing a specific study/number not verified
- Stigmatizing language about addiction (no "junkie," "just willpower," etc.)
- Suggesting ADHD treatment replaces addiction treatment, or vice versa

## Required
- Disclaimer on closing card: "Educational only. Not a diagnosis of ADHD or addiction. Please consult a licensed clinician."
- If either topic needs a resource line, prefer directing to a licensed clinician / evaluation, not a hotline number we haven't verified is current.
