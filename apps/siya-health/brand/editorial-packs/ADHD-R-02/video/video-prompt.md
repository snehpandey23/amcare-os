# ADHD-R-02 — HeyGen video script (avatar-free)

**Insight ID:** ADHD-R-02  
**Topic:** Is it ADHD… or addiction?  
**Format:** Short-form reel · 9:16 · **~50–55 seconds**  
**Mode:** **NO AVATAR** — voiceover + branded, script-matched background scenes  
**Tone:** Professional · non-judgmental · clarifying · hopeful  
**Audience:** Adults 20–45 with ADHD traits, or people worried about a partner/family member  
**Funnel:** Awareness → consideration → action (clinical evaluation)  
**Structure:** Builds tension between two explanations → **ends on an open question** → **Siya Health answers it** → CTA

**Clinical anchor (brief, on-script):** Research literature documents meaningful overlap between ADHD and substance use disorders; untreated ADHD is a known risk factor for self-medicating.  
**Do not claim:** That any behavior is definitely addiction or definitely ADHD, that medication cures cravings, or that this replaces a clinical evaluation.

**Disclaimer (closing card):** Educational only. Not a diagnosis of ADHD or addiction. Please consult a licensed clinician.

## Full narration (paste into HeyGen as voice-only)

```text
Your phone buzzes. You told yourself five minutes — that was an hour ago.
Is that ADHD? Or is it addiction?

People with ADHD often chase dopamine — scrolling, snacking, spending — because their brain's reward system runs low.
That can look like impulsivity, not dependence.

Addiction tends to look different: your body needs more each time, and stopping feels like withdrawal.

Here's the twist — the two overlap a lot.
Research shows a real link between ADHD and substance use, especially when ADHD goes untreated.
Many people are self-medicating undiagnosed ADHD without realizing it.

So when you can't stop — is it the addiction talking, or untreated ADHD chasing the dopamine it's missing?

Siya Health's answer: you don't guess — you get evaluated.
The right diagnosis is what makes the right treatment possible.

Talk to a clinician who can tell the difference — Siya Health can help you start.
```

## Research synthesis (fresh angle — not copied from source notes)

| Source angle | Fresh reel beat |
|--------------|-----------------|
| Reddit: dopamine-seeking, "want to not be me for a while" | Opening hook: phone scroll that runs long |
| Tolerance/withdrawal/compulsive prioritization = addiction markers | Named plainly as a contrast, not a checklist |
| High comorbidity + untreated ADHD fuels self-medicating | "Many are self-medicating undiagnosed ADHD" |
| Ambiguity in the research itself ("when both may coexist") | Turned into the video's central open question |
| Medication reduces craving for some (not universal) | Kept out of the script to avoid overclaiming; redirected to "get evaluated" instead |
