## Caption

Is it ADHD — or addiction? The honest answer: they overlap more than most people realize.

ADHD often drives dopamine-seeking behavior — scrolling, snacking, impulsive spending — because the brain's reward system runs low. That can look like impulsivity rather than dependence. Addiction typically shows a different pattern: needing more over time, and withdrawal when you stop.

Research documents a real link between ADHD and substance use, particularly when ADHD goes undiagnosed and untreated. Many people are, in effect, self-medicating a condition they don't yet know they have.

The answer isn't to guess. It's to get evaluated — because the right diagnosis is what makes the right treatment possible.
