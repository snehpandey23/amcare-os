# Instagram — ADHD-R-02 (reel)

## Caption

Is that ADHD… or is it addiction?

ADHD brains often chase dopamine — scrolling, snacking, spending — because the reward system runs low. That can look like impulsivity, not dependence.

Addiction looks different: needing more each time, and withdrawal when you stop.

The two overlap more than people think — research links ADHD and substance use, especially when ADHD goes untreated.

Siya Health's answer: don't guess. Get evaluated. The right diagnosis is what makes the right treatment possible.

Educational only. Not a diagnosis of ADHD or addiction. Please consult a licensed clinician.
