If ADHD and insulin resistance both feel true, you don’t have to pick a side.

Better questions for a clinician visit:
• Have focus problems been longstanding — or mainly recent?
• Should we look at metabolic health and energy around meals?
• What about sleep, cycle/PCOS, mood, and stress?
• When focus worsens, what else shifts?

Whole-person evaluation connects the dots. It doesn’t force one label to explain everything.
