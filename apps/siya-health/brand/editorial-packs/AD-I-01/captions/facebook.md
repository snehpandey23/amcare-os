## Post

ADHD fog… or low iron?

They can look alike — and they can stack.

People living with ADHD (and clinicians) sometimes miss a reversible contributor: low iron stores. Ferritin can be low even when a basic CBC hemoglobin looks “normal enough.”

That matters if you also have heavy periods, restless legs, deep fatigue, or the sense that focus tools aren’t landing like they used to.

Iron doesn’t “cause ADHD.” ADHD is a neurodevelopmental pattern. But iron supports pathways involved in attention — so deficiency can make focus and energy feel harder.

**Practical step:** Ask your clinician for ferritin / iron studies before self-supplementing high-dose iron.
