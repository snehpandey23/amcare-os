## Draft A

I’ve sat with patients who were sure their ADHD meds “stopped working.”

Sometimes the story was sleep.  
Sometimes mood.  
Sometimes hormones.  
Sometimes ferritin so low the CBC still looked politely normal.

Iron deficiency doesn’t cause ADHD.  
But attention doesn’t happen in a vacuum — and iron is part of the chemistry that focus depends on.

The clinical habit I trust: when fog + fatigue + heavy periods stack, I don’t only escalate the ADHD narrative. I ask what we haven’t measured.

Curious how other clinicians sequence that differential without making patients feel dismissed.

Educational reflection only. Not advice for any individual.
