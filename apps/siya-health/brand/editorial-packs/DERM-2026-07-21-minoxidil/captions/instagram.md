## Caption

Does minoxidil work?

Often — if you give it time and consistency.

For many with pattern hair loss, it can support density. Results vary. Early shedding can happen and feel scary; it isn’t automatic proof the product is “ruining” your hair.

**One practical step:** Use consistently for 3–6 months before judging. Ask a clinician if irritation or doubts show up.
