## Script

**Hook (0–3s)**
On-screen: **I used to think I was just bad at life.**
Visual: figure surrounded by small everyday "failures" — unpaid bill, missed call, unfolded laundry — drawn softly, not chaotic/shameful.

**Beat 1 (3–10s)**
On-screen: **Everyone else seemed to manage. So it had to be me, right?**
Visual: figure watching others move smoothly through their day, feeling out of step.

**Beat 2 (10–17s)**
On-screen: **Turns out "bad at life" had a name — and it wasn't a character flaw.**
Visual: soft shift in lighting, figure standing a little taller.

**Close (17–22s)**
On-screen: **If this sounds familiar, it might be worth a real conversation — not another label you gave yourself.**
