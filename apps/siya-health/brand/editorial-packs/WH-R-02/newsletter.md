## Body

Opening:
If you’re in your 40s and the strategies that used to hold work and family together feel unreliable, you’re not imagining it—and you’re not alone.

Main teach:
We often name one piece at a time: perimenopause, sleep, mood, ADHD. Midlife frequently involves several systems moving together. Understanding the connections is usually more useful than forcing a single label too early.
