Many women in their 40s describe the same shift:

Coping strategies that worked for years suddenly stop working.
Focus frays. Sleep breaks. The mental load that used to be manageable… isn’t.

Medicine often files this under one label at a time—
perimenopause, anxiety, ADHD, “just stress”—
as if the human arrives in departments.

Midlife rarely works that way.

Fog, sleep, iron, mood, hormones, and lifelong attention patterns can move together.
Understanding the connections is often more useful than forcing a single explanation too early.

We built Women’s Midlife Health to teach those intersections—brain, hormones, and metabolism—without turning every symptom into a slogan.
