- Systems may interact (sleep, iron, mood, hormones, attention)
- 14-day symptom log (sleep, bleeding, fog timing) — practical self-observation
- Asking clinician to evaluate iron/ferritin, thyroid, sleep, mood (common reversible contributors to midlife brain fog; not a diagnosis protocol)
