## Caption

Coping strategies that worked for 20 years…
suddenly don’t.

If you’re in your 40s and the systems that used to hold your life together feel unreliable—you’re not “bad at adulting.” Midlife fog often sits at an intersection: sleep, iron, mood, hormones, attention.

**One practical step:** For 14 days, jot sleep hours, heavy bleeding (Y/N), and when fog is worst. Bring that log to a clinician and ask to evaluate iron/ferritin, thyroid, sleep, and mood — not “stress” alone.
