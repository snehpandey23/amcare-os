## Hook (0–3s)

“My coping used to work.”

## Beat 1 (3–12s)

fog, forgotten rooms, systems that suddenly fail.

## Beat 2 (12–22s)
We often file it under perimenopause.
Sometimes that’s fair.

But what if sleep, iron, mood, hormones, and lifelong attention patterns are all moving at once?

## Beat 3 (22–28s)
Medicine is great at departments.
Midlife isn’t.

## Close (28–30s)

Link in bio. Educational only.
