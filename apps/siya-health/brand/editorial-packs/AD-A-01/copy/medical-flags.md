# Medical / legal flags — AD-A-01

**Publish organic?** YES after copy approval + educational + **not legal advice** disclaimer in captions  
**Clinical stamp:** Preferred before paid; required if any ADA “rights” language is sharpened

## Safe

- Lived workplace friction: focus, deadlines, task juggling, surviving on extra hours  
- Educational examples of supports people **discuss** (headphones, written tasks, quieter space, schedule blocks, remote/lower distraction, task breakdown, short breaks)  
- Clinician can document findings/limitations when clinically supported  
- Employer / school / testing board decides what is granted  
- Screening → evaluation → ask about documentation needs  
- Cross-link ADHD vs burnout without diagnosing the viewer

## Avoid / blocked on creative

- “You are entitled to X under the ADA” / guaranteed approval lists  
- Invented approval rates or “most employers…”  
- Presenting school extra time as automatic workplace policy  
- Medication listed as an ADA accommodation  
- Legal strategy, disclosure coaching, or “how to win your HR case”  
- Fear/shame framing that pressures disclosure  
- Self-diagnosis from missing deadlines alone

## Disclaimer (captions)

Educational only — not medical or legal advice. Accommodation outcomes depend on employment law, employer policy, and your situation. Siya Health can provide clinical documentation when appropriate; legal strategy belongs with HR, an employment attorney, or your school’s disability office.
