## 1) Story spine (do not change without re-approval)

Direct-address hook ("before you talk to HR") → name your actual friction → ask a clinician about documentation → follow your employer's process → disclaimer. This is a **process/checklist reel** for people already close to taking action — plainer and more instructional than Script H's mood-led story.

**Practical change carried in this reel:** Identify your specific friction (focus / noise / meetings / deadlines) → ask a clinician what documentation they can provide if evaluation supports it → then follow your employer's accommodation process.

**Claim ladder:** Process framing only — clinician documents, employer decides. No ADA guarantees, no approval %, no legal strategy, no meds-as-accommodation.

## 3) Full VO script (read straight through)

> "Before you talk to HR — know what actually breaks your day. Is it focus, noise, meetings, or deadlines?
> Ask a clinician what documentation they can provide if evaluation supports it.
> Then follow your employer's accommodation process.
> Educational only — not legal advice."

**Word count:** ~50 words · **Pace:** ~130–140 wpm → fits ~21–23s of speech inside the 28–30s runtime (leaves headroom for the end card hold and breathing room between steps).

**VO delivery notes:**
- Direct and practical, not clinical-sounding — like a knowledgeable friend giving you the actual game plan before a hard conversation.
- Slight upward energy on "Is it focus, noise, meetings, or deadlines?" — it should read like a quick self-check, not a quiz show.
- Keep "if evaluation supports it" and "follow your employer's process" neutral/matter-of-fact — no urgency or pressure tone that could read as legal promise.
- Land the disclaimer calmly, not as a rushed legal mumble at the end.

## 6) HeyGen build steps (no-avatar / stock-footage workflow)

1. Create new project → **Text-to-Video (stock footage)** mode — do **not** select an Avatar/Presenter template.
2. Paste the VO script (Section 3) into the script box, or upload as scene-by-scene text if HeyGen paces per-scene.
3. For each scene, either:
   - Use HeyGen's built-in stock library and search the **B-roll keywords** in the table above, or
   - Upload your own licensed/generated footage per scene (see Section 7) and assign it to that scene slot.
4. For Scene 3, split the 6-second block into 3–4 short sub-clips (1.5–2s each) so "Focus? Noise? Meetings? Deadlines?" lands as a quick rhythmic sequence rather than one static shot.
5. Add burned captions per Section 4 — disable HeyGen's default caption style and manually set font/color to match brand tokens.
6. Set aspect ratio **9:16**, duration target **28–30s**.
7. Add bed music per Section 5; duck under VO.
8. Insert the end card as a **static image** (built via `compose_*.py` compositor per Brand Style Lock — not a HeyGen auto-generated closing frame).
9. Export MP4, no built-in watermark, review against the Section 8 checklist before posting.

## 7) Footage sourcing notes (real video, not illustration)

- Prefer **real people in real offices/homes/telehealth-style settings** — warm, naturalistic lighting; avoid stock-photo "corporate handshake" energy or overly polished HR-training-video vibes.
- Diversity across gender/age within the 22–55 adult range across scenes; no single continuity actor required for this format.
- Scene 5's "office door / hallway" shot should read neutral-to-hopeful, not ominous or confrontational — this is not a "dreading HR" horror beat.
- **Reject:** actors miming exaggerated stress, dramatic slow-motion walks toward a manager's office, courtroom/legal-document close-ups, staged "diverse stock team meeting" clips, canned laughter-track office clips.
- If no suitable stock exists for a scene, an acceptable substitute is a 2–5s handheld/tripod clip in a real office/home/telehealth setup matching the description — steady, natural light, no dialogue needed (VO carries the line).
