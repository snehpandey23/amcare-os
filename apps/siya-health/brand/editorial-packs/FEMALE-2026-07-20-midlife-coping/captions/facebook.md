If you’re in your 40s and thinking:

“I used to hold everything in my head—what happened?”

You’re not imagining it. And you’re probably not looking for a single buzzword.

Midlife often brings overlapping changes: sleep, hormones, mood, energy, focus.
Sometimes lifelong patterns (including ADHD) that coping used to hide become harder to manage.

Medicine is good at naming pieces.
It’s less good at owning the whole person.

We wrote about this in Women’s Midlife Health—and specifically in Perimenopause & Brain Fog—with careful language about what we know, what we’re still learning, and when it’s worth talking to a clinician.
