# Instagram — AD-ED-01

Frozen at your laptop.

Hours can pass. The work still hasn’t started.
Your body is still — your brain is busy listing everything you “should” be doing.
Tasks live in your head, not on the calendar. Knowing the list isn’t the same as beginning.

That gap has a name: task initiation struggle — one piece of executive dysfunction.
(Not a diagnosis from a post.)

Practical step:
2 minutes — open the file and type one ugly sentence. Try it for 7 days.
If the freeze is lifelong across settings, take a validated ADHD screener — then talk to a clinician.
