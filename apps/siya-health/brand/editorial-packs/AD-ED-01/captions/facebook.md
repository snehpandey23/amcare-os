# Facebook — AD-ED-01

Frozen at your laptop — hours pass, and the work still hasn’t started.

Sometimes the list is loud in your head while nothing moves on the screen. That gap isn’t proof you don’t care. It’s often what task initiation struggle feels like — a piece of executive dysfunction (not a diagnosis from social media).

Try this: 2 minutes — open the file, type one ugly sentence. Do it for 7 days. If the freeze is lifelong, a validated screener + a clinician conversation is a clearer next step than more self-blame.
