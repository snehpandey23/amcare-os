# LinkedIn Company — AD-ED-01

“Frozen at your laptop” is a workday scene many high-performing adults recognize: the body still, the mental list loud, the start never arriving.

That gap is often discussed as task initiation — part of executive dysfunction — not a character lecture. (A post isn’t a diagnosis.)

Practical change: two minutes — open the file and type one imperfect sentence. If the pattern is lifelong across settings, a validated screener and a clinician conversation beat another week of silent blame.
