# Medical flags — ADHD-R-01

**Publish organic?** YES with educational disclaimer

## Safe
- Educational framing: ADHD traits can strain relationships; many couples improve with communication, systems, and shared responsibility
- Brief clinical validation mention (adult ADHD & relationships guides / literature) without overstating
- Hopeful, non-shaming tone for both ADHD and non-ADHD partners
- CTA toward conversation + clinician if stuck

## Avoid
- “ADHD relationships never work” or “always work”
- Framing ADHD partners as toxic / doomed / incapable of love
- Self-diagnosis as final; medication as a cure-all for relationship problems
- Advice to leave (or stay) a partner as blanket guidance
- Equating ADHD with addiction, autism, or “not caring”
- Clinical decision-making, couples therapy scripts presented as treatment
- Fear marketing or shame-based hooks
