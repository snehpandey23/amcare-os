## Caption

Do relationships with an ADHD partner ever work?

They can — when you stop treating symptoms like character flaws.

Missed texts and zoning out get read as “you don’t care.”
Criticism gets heard as “you’re failing.”
That’s the resentment ↔ shame cycle many couples get stuck in.

ADHD doesn’t doom love. Unmanaged patterns do.
Pause. Build systems together. Own your part.

Educational only. Not a diagnosis or therapy substitute.
If you’re stuck, talk with a clinician who understands ADHD.
