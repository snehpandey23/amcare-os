## Caption

For many adults, ADHD shows up less as “attention” and more as relationship strain.

Distractibility, forgetfulness, impulsivity, and emotional intensity can be misread as not caring — while feedback lands as shame. Clinical relationship guidance describes this resentment–shame cycle clearly.

What helps couples move forward isn’t perfection. It’s teamwork: pause before reacting, separate symptoms from character, and build shared systems for the everyday load.

ADHD doesn’t doom love. Unmanaged patterns do.
