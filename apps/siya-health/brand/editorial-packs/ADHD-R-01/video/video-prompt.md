## Full narration (paste into HeyGen)

```text
Here's the question I keep seeing about ADHD and love.
Do relationships with an ADHD partner ever actually work?

Yes — they can. But not by pretending the hard parts aren't real.

ADHD can make love feel intense and genuine…
and still make follow-through inconsistent.

A missed text. A forgotten chore. Zoning out mid-conversation.
Partners often hear: "You don't care."
The ADHD partner often hears: "You're failing."
That's how resentment and shame start trading places.

Clinical guides on adult ADHD and relationships describe this cycle clearly.
Distractibility, forgetfulness, impulsivity, big emotions — they can strain a partnership.
But symptoms aren't character flaws.

Couples who last treat ADHD as a team problem, not a verdict.
They pause before reacting.
They build simple systems — shared lists, fairer chores, cooler repairs after conflict.
And both people own their part.

ADHD doesn't doom love. Unmanaged patterns do.
If this is your relationship, start with one honest conversation this week.
Follow Siya Health for clarity-first education — and talk with a clinician if you're stuck.
```

**Approx. word count:** ~165 · **Target delivery:** slightly slower than casual (~145 wpm) → ~50–55s. Trim lines 6 or 15 if you need ≤45s.
