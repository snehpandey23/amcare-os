## How to build in HeyGen (click path)

1. **New video** → **Avatar** (or Instant Avatar if Siya-approved likeness) → **9:16**.
2. Paste **entire** script from `script-v1-comorbidity.txt` into Script.
3. Split into **5 scenes** at the `---` markers.
4. Add **Text overlay** per scene (table below). Max ~6 words/line.
5. Enable **Captions**. Style: high-contrast cream/white on navy bar — not emoji-heavy.
6. Optional B-roll (scenes 2–3 only ≤40% runtime): soft window light, water glass untouched, headphones, calendar with empty blocks. Keep avatar primary.
7. End card (scene 5): logo top-left if available + disclaimer line.
8. Export **MP4 1080×1920**, post with `../captions/instagram.md`.
