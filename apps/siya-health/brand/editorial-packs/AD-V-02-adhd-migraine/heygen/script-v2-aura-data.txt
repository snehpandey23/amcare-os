The ADHD–migraine link is a little stronger when migraine comes with visual aura.

---

Aura can look like flashes, zig-zags, or temporary visual changes before the pain. Research in adults found migraine and ADHD symptoms co-occur more often than chance — especially with those visual disturbances.

---

That is not a diagnosis from a video. It’s a reason to tell your clinician the full story: attention patterns and migraine features, not just “bad headaches.”

---

If this resonates, track aura days separately for two weeks, then ask whether both deserve a real workup. Educational only — not medical advice.
