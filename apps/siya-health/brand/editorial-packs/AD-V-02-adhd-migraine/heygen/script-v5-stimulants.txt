ADHD meds and migraines: both stories are real.

---

Some adults say stimulants were one of the most helpful changes for their head pain. Others say the same meds triggered migraines — sometimes through appetite loss, sleep disruption, or skipped meals.

---

Migraines also show up in people with ADHD who never take stimulants. So this isn’t a simple “meds good” or “meds bad” reel.

---

If you’re on ADHD medication and tracking headaches, bring timing, meals, sleep, and migraine days to your prescribing clinician. Do not start, stop, or change doses from social media. Educational only — not medical advice.
