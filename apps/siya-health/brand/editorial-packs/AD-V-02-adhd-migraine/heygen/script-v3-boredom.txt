My migraine trigger wasn’t cheese. It was boredom.

---

For some ADHD brains, understimulation isn’t restful — it’s stressful. The afternoon goes empty, the nervous system hunts for input… and then light and sound suddenly feel unbearable.

---

I’m not saying boredom “causes” migraine for everyone. I’m saying if your migraine days line up with restless, under-stimulated hours, that’s a pattern worth naming.

---

For one week, mark days as overloaded, understimulated, or okay — next to migraine yes or no. Bring it to a clinician. Educational content only — not medical advice.
