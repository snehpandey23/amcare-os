Is this a genetic thing?

---

When migraines, ADHD, and other neurodivergence show up across grandparents, parents, siblings, and kids, it’s fair to wonder about family clustering. Both migraine and ADHD can run in families.

---

That doesn’t mean destiny. Genes, environment, sleep, hormones, and stress all play. A Reel can’t map your inheritance.

---

If your family story is loud, bring it to a clinician as history — not as a self-diagnosis. Educational only — not medical advice.
