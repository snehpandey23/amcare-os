## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5s)

**Spoken**  
What does ADHD have to do with migraine? More than most people are told.

**Text overlay**  
`ADHD… and migraine?`

**Avatar**  
Medium close-up, direct to camera. Slight pause after the question.

**Visual**  
Soft cream or calm office. No B-roll yet.

## Scene 2 — Reframe / data (5–16s)

**Spoken**  
In large adult studies, migraine and ADHD symptoms show up together more often than chance — and the signal is a little stronger when migraine comes with visual aura.

**Text overlay**  
`They co-occur more often`

**Avatar**  
Calm authority — no scare energy on “studies.”

---

## Scene 3 — Stack (16–28s)

**Spoken**  
That doesn’t mean ADHD “causes” your migraine. It means the same nervous system can be sensitive — and an ADHD day of skipped meals, bad sleep, sensory overload, and stress can stack into a migraine day.

**Text overlay**  
`Triggers can stack`

**Optional B-roll**  
Untouched water glass / bright window / headphones — cut back to avatar for last line.

---

## Scene 4 — Practical (28–38s)

**Spoken**  
For seven days, log both: migraine yes or no, plus sleep, food, water, meds timing, sensory load, and where your ADHD energy crashed.

**Text overlay**  
`Log both for 7 days`

---

## Scene 5 — CTA + disclaimer (38–45s)

**Spoken**  
Bring that pattern to a clinician who can hold both. This is educational content only — not medical advice, and not a diagnosis.

**Text overlay**
