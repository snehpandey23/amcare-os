My migraines weren’t random. They were a stack.

---

Guilt. Procrastination. Skipping food. Forgetting water. Too little sleep. Too much sound. Harsh sun. Weather shifts. Sometimes even certain cheeses. Alone, each one is “whatever.” Together, it’s a migraine day.

---

When ADHD is in the mix, that stack isn’t a character flaw — it’s a regulation problem. The brain drops the basics, then the migraine brain pays the bill.

---

Circle your top three stack items for seven days. Protect those first. Then talk with a clinician about both the migraines and the attention pattern. Educational only — not a diagnosis.
