## 1) Master video prompt (paste into Look / scene generator)

```text
Create a 9:16 vertical educational short (1080×1920, 38–45 seconds) for Siya Health.

Avatar: calm adult clinician-educator, warm and trustworthy, speaking directly to camera. Thoughtful physician energy — not salesy, not wellness-influencer, not shouty. Minimal hand gestures; open palms only on contrast lines. Steady eye contact on the hook and the closing CTA.

Wardrobe: navy tailored blazer (#001878 feel), soft cream or off-white blouse/shirt, no loud patterns, no logos, no lab-coat scare energy, no purple accents.

Background: soft cream clinic / calm consulting room with gentle window light. Subtle depth, quiet atmosphere. No purple SaaS glow, no neon, no busy stock office.

Motion: medium conversational pace; slight pause after the opening question; calm emphasis on the words “co-occur” and “stack.”

On-screen text (large, few words, mute-first):
1) ADHD… and migraine?
2) They co-occur more often
3) Triggers can stack
4) Log both for 7 days
5) Educational · Not medical advice

Captions: auto captions ON — cream/white on deep navy bar, no emoji.

Tone: educational comorbidity explanation. Never claim ADHD causes migraine. No medication dosing. End with a soft clinician invite + educational disclaimer.
```
