I thought I needed more Tylenol. I needed a fuller picture.

---

ADHD isn’t only a focus problem. It’s a regulation condition — attention, sleep, appetite, stress response, sensory thresholds. For some people, that whole-body pattern sits right next to headaches or migraine days.

---

That is not permission to self-medicate with stimulants for pain. It is permission to stop treating your symptoms like separate mysteries.

---

If caffeine painkillers have been your daily strategy for years, ask a clinician for a broader workup — including whether ADHD belongs in the conversation. Educational only — not medical advice.
