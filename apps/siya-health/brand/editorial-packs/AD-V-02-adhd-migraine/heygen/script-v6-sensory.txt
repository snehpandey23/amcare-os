Light. Perfume. Loud EarPods. Soft singers only. That’s not drama — that’s a sensitive nervous system.

---

A lot of people with lifelong migraines and later ADHD describe the same thing: sensory load that others shrug off feels like a threat. Sound, smell, light, even textures.

---

Migraine as a “brain type” doesn’t diagnose you. It does explain why your thresholds are different — and why ADHD sensory overwhelm and migraine sensitivity can share a house.

---

This week, note your top sensory load before a migraine day. Then ask a clinician who understands both. Educational content only — not medical advice.
