## V1 — What does ADHD have to do with migraine? ★ HERO

**Recognition:** Treated as two separate problems for years.  
**One concept:** They co-occur more often than chance; triggers stack through an ADHD day.  
**Hooks (pick one):**
- **A Curiosity:** “What does ADHD have to do with migraine?”
- **B Callout:** “ADHD… and migraine?”
- **C Comment language:** “Migraine as a brain type puts this in a different light.”
**Format:** HeyGen avatar, 5 scenes, cream/navy, no purple glow.  
**Practical change:** 7-day dual log (pain + sleep/meals/meds/sensory/ADHD crash).  
**Paste:** `heygen/script-v1-comorbidity.txt`

## V2 — Aura / visual disturbance (data beat)

**Recognition:** Flashes, zig-zags, then head pain — and ADHD fog in the same life.  
**Hooks:**
- **A:** “The ADHD link is a little stronger with migraine aura.”
- **B:** “Visual disturbances + migraine… researchers watch this carefully.”

## V3 — Boredom / understimulation

**Recognition:** Empty afternoon → restless → then migraine day.

## V4 — Lifestyle stack (undiagnosed ADHD cascade)

**Recognition:** Guilt, procrastination, skipped food/water, sleep debt, sound, sun, weather — then blame “random migraines.”  
**Hooks:**
- **A:** “My migraines weren’t random. They were a stack.”
- **B:** “Guilt. Skip lunch. No water. Bad sleep. Then the migraine.”
**Format:** Avatar + short stacked text overlays (max 6 words/line).  
**Practical:** Circle the top 3 stack items for 7 days.  
**Paste:** `heygen/script-v4-lifestyle-stack.txt`

## V5 — Stimulants double-edged

**Recognition:** One person says Adderall saved their head; another says stimulants started their migraines.  
**Hooks:**
- **A:** “ADHD meds and migraines: both stories are real.”
- **B:** “Did stimulants help your migraines — or start them?”
**Format:** Avatar balanced; no brand/dose; clinician only.  
**Practical:** Track headache days vs med timing / meals / sleep with your prescriber.  
**Paste:** `heygen/script-v5-stimulants.txt`
