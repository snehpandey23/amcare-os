## V1 — What does ADHD have to do with migraine? ★ HERO

**Primary keyword:** ADHD and migraine  
**Secondary keyword:** migraine ADHD adults  
**Hook archetype:** Myth-Busting (co-occurrence ≠ causation) + Pattern Interrupt  
**Visual strategy:** Talking head + B-roll  

**Recognition:** Treated as two separate problems for years.  
**One concept:** They co-occur more often than chance; triggers stack through an ADHD day.  

**Hooks (pick one):**
- **A Curiosity / Pattern Interrupt:** “What does ADHD have to do with migraine?”
- **B Callout:** “ADHD… and migraine?”
- **C Relatable comment language:** “Migraine as a brain type puts this in a different light.”
- **D Myth-Busting:** “No — ADHD doesn’t ‘cause’ migraine. Here’s the real link.”

**Beat map (auditory + visual):**
| Time | Spoken | On-screen / B-roll |
|------|--------|--------------------|
| 0–3s | Hook A or B | Large text: `ADHD… and migraine?` |
| 3–16s | Co-occur more than chance; stronger with visual aura | Soft B-roll: overlapping calm shapes / clinic cream |
| 16–28s | Not causation — ADHD day can stack into migraine day | B-roll: water glass, headphones, soft light |
| 28–38s | 7-day dual log | Text: `Log both for 7 days` |
| 38–45s | Clinician + disclaimer | End card + disclaimer |

**Practical change:** 7-day dual log (pain + sleep/meals/meds/sensory/ADHD crash).
