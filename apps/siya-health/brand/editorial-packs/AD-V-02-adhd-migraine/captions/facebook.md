What does ADHD have to do with migraine?

Adult studies find migraine and ADHD symptoms show up together more often than chance. Triggers can also stack through an ADHD day — meals, sleep, sensory load, stress.

For 7 days, log both the migraine and the basics. Then talk with a clinician.
