## Instagram

I thought I needed more Tylenol. I needed a fuller picture.

ADHD whole body regulation isn’t only a focus problem — attention, sleep, appetite, stress response, sensory thresholds. For some people, that pattern sits right next to headaches or migraine days. ADHD headaches dopamine conversations are interesting — and easy to oversimplify online.

That is not permission to self-medicate with stimulants for pain. It is permission to stop treating your symptoms like separate mysteries.

If caffeine painkillers have been your daily strategy for years, ask a clinician for a broader workup — including whether ADHD belongs in the conversation.
