## Instagram

Light. Perfume. Loud EarPods. Soft singers only. That’s not drama — that’s a sensitive nervous system.

A lot of people with lifelong migraines and later ADHD describe the same thing: migraine sensory sensitivity that others shrug off. Sound, smell, light, even textures. ADHD light sensitivity migraine days are real lived experience — not “being difficult.”

Migraine as a “brain type” doesn’t diagnose you. It does explain why your thresholds are different — and why ADHD sensory overwhelm and migraine sensitivity can share a house.

This week, note your top sensory load before a migraine day. Then ask a clinician who understands both.
