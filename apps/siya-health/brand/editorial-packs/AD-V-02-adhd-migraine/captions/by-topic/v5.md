## Instagram

ADHD meds and migraines: both stories are real.

Some adults say stimulants were one of the most helpful changes for their head pain. Others say the same meds triggered migraines — sometimes through appetite loss, sleep disruption, or skipped meals.

Migraines also show up in people with ADHD who never take stimulants. So the ADHD medication migraine conversation isn’t a simple “meds good” or “meds bad” Reel.

If you’re tracking stimulants and migraines, bring timing, meals, sleep, and migraine days to your prescribing clinician. Do not start, stop, or change doses from social media.
