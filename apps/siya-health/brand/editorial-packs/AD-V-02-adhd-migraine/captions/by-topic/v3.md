## Instagram

My migraine trigger wasn’t cheese. It was boredom.

For some ADHD brains, understimulation isn’t restful — it’s stressful. The afternoon goes empty, the nervous system hunts for input… and then light and sound suddenly feel unbearable.

An ADHD boredom migraine pattern isn’t proven for everyone. But if your understimulation migraine trigger days line up with restless, empty hours, that’s worth naming.

For one week, mark days as overloaded, understimulated, or okay — next to migraine yes or no. Bring it to a clinician.
