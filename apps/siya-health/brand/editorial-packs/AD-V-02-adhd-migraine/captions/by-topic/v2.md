## Instagram

The ADHD–migraine link is a little stronger when migraine comes with visual aura.

Aura can look like flashes, zig-zags, or temporary visual changes before the pain. Research in adults found migraine and ADHD symptoms co-occur more often than chance — especially with those visual disturbances.

That is not a diagnosis from a Reel. If visual aura migraine ADHD patterns show up in your life, track aura days separately for two weeks, then tell a clinician the full story: attention patterns and migraine features — not just “bad headaches.”

Educational only. Not medical advice. Not a diagnosis.
