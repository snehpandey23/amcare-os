Someone asked about hemiplegic migraine. Here’s the careful version.

Hemiplegic migraine is a rare migraine subtype that can include temporary weakness on one side of the body — and it can look frighteningly like a stroke.

Social media cannot tell you which one you have. Sudden weakness, facial droop, trouble speaking, or new neurological symptoms need urgent medical evaluation — same day, emergency pathways when in doubt. Do not self-sort hemiplegic migraine symptoms from a Reel.

If you’ve been labeled with complex or hemiplegic migraine, that care belongs with specialists who know your history — not social media.
