I’ve been thinking about this clinically:

“What does ADHD have to do with migraine?” is a fair question. Population data says they co-occur more than chance — especially migraine with visual aura. Lived experience says the ADHD day (skipped meals, sensory load, sleep debt, guilt-procrastination loops) often *is* the migraine setup.

The honest frame isn’t “ADHD causes migraines.” It’s: one nervous system, two labels, one stack of triggers — and care that can hold both.

Educational reflection only. Not advice for any individual.
