What does ADHD have to do with migraine?

In a large adult cohort study, migraine and ADHD symptoms were comorbid (about 1.8× more often together), with a stronger signal for migraine with visual disturbances. For migraine ADHD adults, the useful move isn’t a slogan — it’s treating attention regulation and migraine triggers as one nervous-system story.

Practical change: a 7-day dual log (pain + sleep/meals/meds/sensory load/ADHD crash) before the next clinician visit.
