## Instagram (V1 ★)

What does ADHD have to do with migraine?

More than most people are told. In adult research, ADHD and migraine symptoms co-occur more often than chance — especially when migraine includes visual aura. That doesn’t mean ADHD “causes” migraine. It means the same sensitive nervous system can stack triggers: skipped meals, bad sleep, sensory overload, stress.

If you’re one of the many migraine ADHD adults juggling both labels, try this: for 7 days, log migraine Y/N + sleep, food, water, meds timing, sensory load, and where your ADHD energy crashed. Bring the pattern to a clinician who can hold both.

Educational only. Not medical advice. Not a diagnosis.
