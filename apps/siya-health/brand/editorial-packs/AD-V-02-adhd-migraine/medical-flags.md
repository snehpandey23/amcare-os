# Medical flags — AD-V-02 (ADHD × Migraine)

**Publish organic?** YES for V1–V4, V6–V7 with spoken + on-screen educational disclaimer.  
**Prefer clinical stamp before paid / before V5 (stimulants) and V8 (hemiplegic).**

## Safe
- Bidirectional comorbidity language (“more likely to co-occur”) — cite population association, not personal diagnosis
- Migraine with aura / visual disturbance association with ADHD symptoms (Hansen et al., BMC Neurology 2018; OR ≈ 1.8 overall, higher with visual aura)
- Shared pathways framed cautiously: dopamine, genetics, stress, sleep, sensory sensitivity — as *possible contributors*, not proven single cause
- Stimulants: **mixed** — some people report fewer migraines, some report triggers (appetite/sleep/skipped meals) — clinician only
- ADHD lifestyle cascade as *common triggers that stack* (missed meals, dehydration, sleep debt, sensory overload, stress/guilt) — not “ADHD causes migraine”
- Sensory sensitivity as lived experience (light, sound, smell, texture)
- Family clustering: migraines / neurodivergence can run together — not destiny, not DIY genetics advice
- Hemiplegic migraine: rare subtype; sudden one-sided weakness needs urgent medical evaluation — educational naming only
- Soft invite to clinician conversation + pattern log

## Avoid
- “ADHD causes chronic migraine” as a hard claim
- “Your stimulants are causing / curing your migraines”
- Stop / start / dose ADHD or migraine meds from a Reel
- Self-diagnosis of ADHD, migraine with aura, or hemiplegic migraine from the video
- Fear-mongering stroke language without clear “seek urgent care” framing for hemiplegic symptoms
- Guaranteed genetic inheritance claims
- Founder LinkedIn hard CTA (Company captions OK with one soft link)
- Naming patient Reddit / IG commenters
