## Auditory framework (spoken script)

Every cut follows the same ear pattern:

1. **Hook (0–3s)** — archetype line (interrupt / relate / bust)  
2. **Proof or mirror (3–15s)** — one research soft cite **or** one VOC mirror  
3. **Reframe (15–28s)** — careful truth + hard ban stated once  
4. **Practical change (28–38s)** — log / top-3 / clinician ask  
5. **Close (38–45s)** — soft CTA + disclaimer  

Voice: calm educator, medium pace, no fear spike, no “you probably have…”.
