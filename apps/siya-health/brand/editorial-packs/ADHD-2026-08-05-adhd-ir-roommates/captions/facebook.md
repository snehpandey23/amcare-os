ADHD and insulin resistance can coexist. PCOS may be in the mix for some people.

The link is real enough to take seriously — not settled enough to oversimplify as cause-and-effect.

A careful evaluation looks at attention history, metabolic health, sleep, hormones/cycle when relevant, and mood — the whole person.

Educational only. Not a diagnosis. Speak with a qualified clinician if symptoms affect daily life.
