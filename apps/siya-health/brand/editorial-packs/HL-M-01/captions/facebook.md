## Post

Does minoxidil work for hair loss?

For many people with androgenetic (pattern) hair loss, yes — with consistent use. Results vary. An early shed phase can occur.

**Practical step:** Consistent use for 3–6 months before judging response. Check in with a clinician about irritation or uncertainty.
