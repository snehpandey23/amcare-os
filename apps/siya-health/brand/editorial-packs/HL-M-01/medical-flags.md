# Medical flags — HL-M-01

**Publish organic?** YES with educational disclaimer  
**Paid?** OK organic educational

## Safe
- Minoxidil can help many with pattern hair loss
- Consistency over weeks/months required
- Early shedding can occur and may be temporary
- Judge response over 3–6 months typically
- Not a guarantee of full restoration

## Do not add
- Guaranteed celebrity hair results
- DIY oral minoxidil dosing from a carousel
- Claiming it cures all alopecia types
- Shame for genetic hair loss
- Telling people to ignore scalp irritation indefinitely
