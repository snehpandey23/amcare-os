Achievement is an outcome. It does not show the effort required to produce it.

An adult may have graduated, built a career, and appeared organized while privately relying on deadline pressure, overwork, or extensive external systems.

That pattern does not diagnose ADHD. It does show why a responsible adult evaluation should consider more than grades or job titles: developmental history, impairment across settings, sleep, mood, and alternative explanations all matter.

A useful preparation step is to build a short timeline with examples from school, work, relationships, and daily life.
