Doing well in school does not tell the whole story.

Some adults remember good grades alongside all-night work sessions, chronic procrastination, lost belongings, or needing far more structure than their peers. Those experiences do not establish ADHD, but achievement alone should not end the conversation either.

A comprehensive evaluation considers childhood history, current impairment across settings, sleep, mood, and conditions that can overlap with or resemble ADHD.

Practical next step: write down examples from school, work, relationships, and home life, then bring that timeline to a qualified clinician.
