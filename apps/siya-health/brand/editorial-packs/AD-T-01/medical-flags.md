# Medical flags — AD-T-01

**Publish organic?** YES with educational disclaimer  
**Paid?** Prefer after clinical stamp (ADHD + TRT overlap claims)

## Safe
- Symptom overlap between low testosterone and ADHD-like presentation
- Either/or framing is a myth — both can be present
- TRT outcomes for ADHD symptoms are mixed in lived experience
- Ask clinician to evaluate attention history and hormone status
- Do not stop ADHD medication without clinical guidance

## Do not add
- “TRT fixes ADHD” / “TRT cures brain fog”
- Advice to quit or cut stimulants after starting TRT
- “Your ADHD was never real — it was low T”
- DIY dosing, cycle, or lab cutoffs as Siya protocol
- Unvalidated dopamine → prolactin → low T causal chains as brand science
- FTM/trans hormone-therapy anecdotes as primary proof for cis men’s creative
