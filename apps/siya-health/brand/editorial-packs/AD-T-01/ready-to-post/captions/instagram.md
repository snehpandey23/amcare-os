## Caption (copy-paste)

Low T can look like ADHD.

That doesn’t make ADHD fake.

Brain fog, low drive, poor focus, irritability, flat mood — men report these for both. The internet loves an either/or. Biology often stacks more than one layer.

TRT isn’t an ADHD cure. Some men feel clearer. Some feel worse. Some still need ADHD care.

**Practical step:** Ask a clinician to evaluate your attention history and hormone status — before you change meds or chase a comment-section diagnosis.
