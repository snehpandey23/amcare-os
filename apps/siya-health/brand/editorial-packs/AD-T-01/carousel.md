**Insight:** Low T can look like ADHD — that doesn’t make ADHD fake  
**Audience:** Men ~25–70 (TOFU–MOFU); fog, low drive, poor focus; some on ADHD meds or wondering “is it hormones?”  
**Product:** ADHD Care · Men’s Performance & Hormonal Health  
**Spokes:** https://siya.health/adhd-care · https://siya.health/mens-health-longevity · https://siya.health/blog/when-is-testosterone-therapy-appropriate  
**Voice:** Company only (no founder LinkedIn)  
**Format:** 4:5 · 1080×1350 · 6 slides · `INSTAGRAM-STATIC.md`

## Slide 1 — Hook

## Slide 2 — Recognition
**Copy:**
The symptoms overlap for a reason.

**Support:**
Brain fog · low drive · poor focus ·

---

## Slide 3 — Myth
**Copy:**
It’s not either/or.

**Support:**
The internet loves a single villain.  
Biology often stacks more than one layer.

---

## Slide 4 — Reality check
**Copy:**
TRT isn’t an ADHD cure.

**Support:**
Some men feel clearer on TRT.  
Some feel worse.  
Some still need ADHD care.  
Lived experience is mixed — and that matters.

---

## Slide 5 — Practical change
**Copy:**
“Get checked” ≠ “quit your meds.”

**Support:**
Ask a clinician to evaluate attention history  
and hormone status — before you change  
anything on your own.

---

## Slide 6 — Path
**Copy:**
Both can be true.

**Support:**
ADHD can be real. Low T can be real.  
The move is evaluation —  
not a comment-section diagnosis.
