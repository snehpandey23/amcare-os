Low testosterone and ADHD can share a symptom language: fog, low drive, poor focus, irritability, flat mood.

That overlap is clinically useful — and commercially oversimplified.

Two myths we see online:
1. It’s either ADHD or low T  
2. TRT will fix ADHD (or prove you never had it)

Neither holds. Both conditions can be present. TRT outcomes for attention symptoms are mixed. Stopping ADHD medication without guidance is not a content strategy — it’s a safety risk.

**Practical change:** Evaluate attention history and hormone status with a clinician before DIY treatment changes.
