Low testosterone can look a lot like ADHD — brain fog, low drive, poor focus, irritability, flat mood.

That overlap is real. It does **not** mean ADHD isn’t real, and it does **not** mean TRT is an ADHD cure.

Some men feel clearer on TRT. Some feel worse. Some still need ADHD care.

**One practical step:** Ask a clinician to evaluate your attention history and hormone status before you change medications on your own.
