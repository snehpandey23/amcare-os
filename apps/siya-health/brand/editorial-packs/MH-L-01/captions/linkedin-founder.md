## Draft A (copy-paste)

I keep noticing the same pattern with men on testosterone therapy.

They want the prescription to do the work that sleep, training, and follow-up labs were supposed to do.

TRT can be appropriate care.  
It is not a substitute for resistance training.  
It is not a substitute for protein and recovery.  
It is not a substitute for checking hematocrit and levels on schedule.

And vitamin D? Fix deficiency — after you measure it. Blind megadosing is not “optimization.”

The unsexy truth: medical therapy works best when the basics are treated like part of the treatment.

Curious what other clinicians tell patients on day one of TRT — beyond the script.

Educational reflection only. Not medical advice for any individual.
