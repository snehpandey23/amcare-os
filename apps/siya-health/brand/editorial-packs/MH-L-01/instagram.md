## Caption

On TRT? The prescription isn’t the whole program.

Five fundamentals that matter in practice:
1. Resistance train 2–4× / week  
2. Hit protein at each meal  
3. Protect 7+ hours of sleep  
4. Check vitamin D — don’t megadose blind  
5. Keep follow-up labs (hematocrit, levels, safety)

Medical TRT without lifestyle + monitoring is incomplete.

Educational only. Not a diagnosis or a dosing guide. Individual plans require your clinician.
