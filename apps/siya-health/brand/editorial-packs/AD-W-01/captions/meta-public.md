ADHD in women gets missed.

Some attention and mood patterns look alike on the surface — impulsivity, rejection sensitivity, mood swings, relationship friction. BPD and ADHD can share overlapping symptoms. They can also co-occur. Neither pattern is “just the other one mislabeled.”

What helps is history: When did this start? Has it shown up across years and settings — or mainly in certain relationship crises? A clinician uses that timeline to differentiate; a checklist in a comment section can’t.

If you’re seeking care: bring a lifelong timeline and share your full history with your provider. Ask what evaluation fits *your* story — including whether ADHD screening is appropriate, and without dropping any BPD-specific care that already helps (for example, structured therapies such as DBT when that’s part of the plan).
