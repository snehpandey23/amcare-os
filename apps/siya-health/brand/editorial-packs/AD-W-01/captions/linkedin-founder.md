In clinic, I still see women whose attention story was never fully taken.

Not because someone “got the wrong label on purpose.” Because impulsivity, rejection sensitivity, mood swings, and relationship friction can show up in more than one condition. BPD and ADHD can overlap. They can also both be true.

What I trust more than a checklist is the timeline.

When did this start?  
Across school, work, relationships — or mainly in certain crises?

That history is how we differentiate. It is not how we erase a diagnosis that already fits, or walk someone away from care that is helping — including structured therapies like DBT when BPD is part of the picture.

Sometimes ADHD evaluation belongs in the conversation.  
Sometimes the work is holding more than one truth at once.
