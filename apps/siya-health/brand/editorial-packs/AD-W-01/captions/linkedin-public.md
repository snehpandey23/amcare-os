ADHD in women is still frequently missed.

Attention and mood-related presentations can look similar on the surface — impulsivity, rejection sensitivity, mood instability, and relationship friction appear across more than one condition. BPD and ADHD can have overlapping symptoms and can co-occur. Overlap does not mean one diagnosis is routinely a misread of the other.

Clinicians separate these presentations with history: When did this start? Has the pattern been present across settings and years — or does it cluster mainly around certain relationship crises? Timeline and context matter more than a social-media checklist.

A practical next step in care: bring a lifelong history to the visit. Ask what evaluation fits that history — including whether ADHD screening is appropriate — without discontinuing BPD-specific treatment that is working (for example, DBT when it is part of the care plan).
