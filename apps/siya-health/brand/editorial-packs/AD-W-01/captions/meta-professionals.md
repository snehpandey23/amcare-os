ADHD in women gets missed at work, too.

Patterns that show up as inconsistency under pressure, rejection sensitivity after feedback, or friction in teams can overlap with more than one condition. BPD and ADHD can share symptoms. They can also co-occur. Overlap is not a shortcut to swap one diagnosis for another.

Clinicians don’t start from the latest hard week at work. They ask: When did this start? Has this been present across school, early career, and now — or does it cluster mainly around certain relationship or workplace crises?

If you’re seeking care: bring a lifelong timeline (school → career → now) to share with your provider. Ask what evaluation fits your history — including whether ADHD screening belongs in that conversation — while continuing any BPD-specific care that is helping (such as DBT when that’s already part of the plan).
