ADHD in women is still often missed in professional settings.

Work stress can amplify patterns — inconsistency under load, sensitivity to feedback, interpersonal friction — that also appear in other conditions. BPD and ADHD can share overlapping symptoms and can co-occur. Symptom overlap is not diagnostic, and it is not a reason to treat one label as the “real” explanation for the other.

Clinicians differentiate with longitudinal history, not the latest quarterly cycle: When did this start? Has the pattern been present across school, early career, and current roles — or does it appear mainly in relationship or workplace crises?

Practical next step: bring a lifelong timeline to share with a clinician and ask what evaluation fits that history — including whether ADHD screening is appropriate — while continuing any BPD-specific care that helps (such as DBT when already indicated). Shared evaluation — not self-diagnosis from social media or a single performance review.
