# Medical flags — AD-W-01

**Publish organic?** YES with educational disclaimer

## Safe
- Educational framing tied to existing spoke
- Overlap + co-occurrence of BPD and ADHD (both can be true)
- Clinician differentiates via lifelong timeline / history
- Practical step: share full history; ask what evaluation fits — ADHD screening only when appropriate to *that* history
- Affirm continuing BPD-specific care that helps (e.g. DBT when indicated)

## Avoid
- Internet diagnosis of BPD or ADHD
- Stigma; “too emotional” / “personality” as dismissed labels next to the BPD comparison
- Implying BPD is commonly just missed ADHD
- Steering anyone away from appropriate BPD-specific care toward ADHD screening as the “correct” path
- Claiming all misdiagnosed women have ADHD
