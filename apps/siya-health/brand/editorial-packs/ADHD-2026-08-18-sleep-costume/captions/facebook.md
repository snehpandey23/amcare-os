Eight hours in bed can still leave you unable to think.

That overlap with ADHD is real — and it isn’t a diagnosis by itself. Unrefreshing sleep, snoring, or a focus crash after nights went bad belong in the conversation with a clinician *before* anyone assumes a stimulant is the first move.

Many adults have both a sleep problem and ADHD. Treating sleep first doesn’t erase a lifelong pattern. It keeps the order honest.
