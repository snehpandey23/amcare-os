### Spoken script (Siya angle — paraphrased)

```text
People talk a lot about physical signs of ADHD.

Fidgeting. Restless legs. Trouble sitting still.

Those cues are common to notice.
They are not enough to diagnose.

Many adults suppress movement in meetings and clinics.
Restlessness can be quiet — or feel internal.
Anxiety and sleep problems can look similar too.

Diagnosis needs history, impairment, and pattern over time —
not one habit on a list.

Notice the body. Don’t diagnose from it.

Full guide at siya.health. Educational only.
```
