## Instagram

Physical signs of ADHD — like fidgeting, restless legs, or trouble sitting still — are common to notice.

They are not enough to diagnose.

Many adults suppress movement in meetings and clinics. Restlessness can be quiet or internal. Anxiety and sleep problems can look similar too.

Diagnosis needs history, impairment, and pattern over time — not one habit on a list.

This is education, not a diagnosis. Social media cannot replace a clinician.

This content is for education only and should not replace professional medical advice.
