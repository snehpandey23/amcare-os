ADHD can make weight management feel harder for some people — not because of a character flaw, and not because “ADHD causes obesity.”

Missed hunger/fullness cues, hyperfocus that skips meals, then a rush to catch up, plus the effort wall of “just prep it” — these patterns stack.

It’s not just willpower. Fuller guide (food noise + next steps): https://siya.health/blog/adhd-and-binge-eating
