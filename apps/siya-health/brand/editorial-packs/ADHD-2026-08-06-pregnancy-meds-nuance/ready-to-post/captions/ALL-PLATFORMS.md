Pregnant and on ADHD medication — or planning to be?

The loudest advice online is usually the least careful: quit everything, or shrug it off. What newer research points toward is a careful middle — still individual, still incomplete.

For many prescribed ADHD medicines, recent observational studies often don’t show a huge harm signal. That isn’t a guarantee. Untreated ADHD has risks too.

One medicine — modafinil — stands apart with a clearer malformation signal. That conversation belongs with your clinicians, not a comment section.

General education only — not personal medical advice. Decide with your prescribing clinician and pregnancy care team.
