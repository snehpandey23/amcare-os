# LinkedIn Company — ADHD-2026-08-06-pregnancy-meds-nuance

Public conversation about ADHD medication in pregnancy often collapses into “always stop” or “always fine.”

A 2025 systematic review of recent observational cohorts supports a more careful middle: many analyses do not show a large harm signal for continuing many prescribed ADHD medicines, while untreated ADHD also carries risks — and modafinil stands out with a clearer malformation signal requiring clinician-led planning.

Educational content for a general audience. Not a substitute for individualized medical advice. Patients should decide with their prescribing clinician and obstetric team.
