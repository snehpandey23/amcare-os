### Spoken script (Siya angle — paraphrased)

```text
Pregnant and on ADHD medication — or planning to be?

Online, the loudest advice is usually the least careful.
One side says quit everything. The other shrugs it off.

What newer research actually points toward is a middle lane —
still cautious, still individual, not a TikTok yes-or-no.

For many prescribed ADHD medicines, recent observational studies
often don’t show a huge harm signal — and the evidence still isn’t perfect.
Leaving ADHD untreated isn’t risk-free either.

One medicine — modafinil — is different.
It has a clearer malformation signal, so that conversation belongs
with your clinicians, not a comment thread.

This is education, not personal medical advice.
Decide with the people who know your history —
your prescribing clinician and your pregnancy care team.
```

### Short cut (≤30s)

```text
ADHD meds in pregnancy aren’t a simple yes or no.

Education only. Decide with your care team.
```
