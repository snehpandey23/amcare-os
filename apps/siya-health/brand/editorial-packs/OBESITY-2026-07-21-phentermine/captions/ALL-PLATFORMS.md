## Caption

Phentermine isn’t “just appetite help.”

It’s stimulant-like — heart rate and blood pressure belong in the conversation before the prescription.

People ask because it can quiet appetite quickly, it’s familiar, and side effects worry them. Screening isn’t optional theater: cardiac history, BP, anxiety/sleep, and other stimulants matter.

**One practical step:** Ask cardiac and blood-pressure screening questions before stimulant-like weight-loss meds — then decide fit with your clinician.

Educational only. Not prescribing advice.
