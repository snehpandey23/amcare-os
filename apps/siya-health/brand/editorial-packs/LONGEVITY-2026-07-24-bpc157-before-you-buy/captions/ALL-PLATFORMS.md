## Caption

Before you buy the “Wolverine” vial —

BPC-157 is not an FDA-approved drug. Being on a clinic menu is not the same as being proven in clinical trials.

Even if the molecule in papers eventually earns stronger human evidence, the product in a vial can still differ: concentration, purity, storage, labelling. The peptide and the product are not always the same question.

Competitive athletes: BPC-157 is prohibited by WADA under S0 (non-approved substances). Experimental and permissible are not synonyms.

**Practical step — three questions before you buy:**
1. What human outcome is proven for *my* problem?
2. Is this an approved product pathway — or a gray / research-labeled supply?
3. Do athlete, league, or service rules ban it?

Then Talk to a Clinician. Curiosity belongs in medicine. So does intellectual honesty.

Educational only. Not individual medical advice. Not a recommendation to use or avoid BPC-157.
