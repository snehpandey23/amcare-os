## Caption

You don’t want a peptide.

You want your body back.

That’s the real product being sold when the internet calls BPC-157 the “Wolverine peptide” — tendons, muscles, gut, joints, recovery, all in one vial.

Here’s the honest tension:

The preclinical biology is genuinely interesting. Animal and lab work has explored blood-vessel and tissue-repair pathways. Interesting science is not the same as proven treatment.

Most of the wow is still rats, mice, and cells.

The human file? Tiny uncontrolled pilots. One widely discussed safety report involved two people. Useful as a starting point. Not proof of Wolverine.

**Practical step:** Pause long enough to ask what is proven in humans for *your* problem — then Talk to a Clinician before buying anything pitched as healing in a vial.
