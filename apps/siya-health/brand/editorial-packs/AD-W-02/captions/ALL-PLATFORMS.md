## Caption

ADHD in women often looks quiet.

Not the stereotypical hyperactivity — more often daydreaming, disorganization, emotional intensity, and a lifelong sense of “why is everything harder for me?”

Hormonal shifts, social expectations, and misattribution to anxiety or mood can delay recognition for years.

**Screening is not diagnosis.** A structured evaluation with a licensed clinician explores childhood history and look-alikes — not an online quiz alone. Evaluation does not guarantee medication.

Practical next step: read the Health Guide, then Talk to a Clinician if it fits your history.
