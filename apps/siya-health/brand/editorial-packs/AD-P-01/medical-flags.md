# Medical flags — AD-P-01

**Publish organic?** YES with educational disclaimer  
**Clinical stamp:** Required before paid; preferred before organic if differential language is tightened further

## Safe
- Physical signs as *possible* adult hyperactivity/restlessness expressions
- Adult hyperactivity often quieter / internalized
- Inattentive ADHD may lack overt fidgeting
- Overlap with anxiety, sleep problems, caffeine, habit
- Screener + clinician path; educational only
- “Common ≠ conclusive” / not enough to diagnose

## Avoid
- Physical sign = you have ADHD
- RLS, hypermobility, sleep apnea framed as ADHD itself
- Self-diagnosis as final answer
- Founder LinkedIn unless explicitly requested
- Medication dosing / treatment protocols in this pack
