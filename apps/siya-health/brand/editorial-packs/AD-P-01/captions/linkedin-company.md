Physical signs that may appear with ADHD — and why they're not enough to diagnose.

In adults, hyperactivity often looks quieter: leg bounce, fidgeting as focus regulation, inner restlessness, restless sleep. Useful to notice. Not definitive on its own.

The same movements show up with anxiety, sleep disruption, caffeine, and habit. Inattentive ADHD may show little overt movement at all.

Practical step: if the pattern is lifelong and impairing, take a validated screener — then talk to a clinician. Don't diagnose from a fidget list.
