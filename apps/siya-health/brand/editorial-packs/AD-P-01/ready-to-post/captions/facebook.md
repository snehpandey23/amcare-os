# Facebook — AD-P-01

Physical signs may show up with ADHD — fidgeting, restlessness, restless sleep — but they are not enough to diagnose.

Same movements can appear with anxiety, boredom, caffeine, sleep problems, and habit. And you can have ADHD without looking “hyper.”

If the pattern is lifelong and impairing, take a validated screener / talk to a clinician. Don't diagnose from a fidget list.
