## Caption

Physical signs that may appear with ADHD — and why they're not enough to diagnose.

Leg bounce. Restless sleep. Fidgeting that helps you focus. The body often shows up first — but common isn't conclusive.

If the pattern is lifelong and getting in the way, take a validated screener — then talk to a clinician. Don't diagnose from a fidget list.
