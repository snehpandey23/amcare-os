## Caption

Still foggy on ADHD treatment?

Women: low iron may be what you’re missing.

This isn’t “just anemia.” Iron status can affect brain function — including pathways tied to dopamine and focus.

Your hemoglobin can look “normal” while ferritin (iron stores) is still low.

**Practical step:** Ask your clinician for ferritin / iron studies — especially with fog, fatigue, restless legs, or heavy periods. Don’t megadose iron on your own.

Educational only. Associations ≠ “iron causes ADHD.” Iron doesn’t cure ADHD.

# LinkedIn Company — ADHD-2026-07-21-iron-fog v3

ADHD treatment conversations often stop at attention meds.

For women, a quieter variable is iron status. Hemoglobin can look “normal” while ferritin (iron stores) is low — and low iron is linked with fatigue, brain fog, and worse attention symptoms in some people, including ADHD populations.

Educational note: associations ≠ causation. Iron does not cause or cure ADHD.

**Practical step:** Ask for ferritin / iron studies — especially with fog, fatigue, restless legs, or heavy periods. Don’t megadose iron without clinical guidance.
