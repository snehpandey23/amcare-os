Still foggy on ADHD treatment?

For many women, low iron is part of the fog story — and it’s easy to miss when hemoglobin looks “normal.”

Iron isn’t just about anemia. It matters for brain function too, including pathways tied to dopamine and focus.

**Practical step:** Ask your clinician for ferritin / iron studies if you have fog, fatigue, restless legs, or heavy periods. Don’t megadose iron on your own.

Educational only — associations aren’t the same as “iron causes ADHD.”
