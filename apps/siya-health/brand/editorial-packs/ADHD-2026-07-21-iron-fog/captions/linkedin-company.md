ADHD treatment conversations often stop at attention meds.

For women, a quieter variable is iron status. Hemoglobin can look “normal” while ferritin (iron stores) is low — and low iron is linked with fatigue, brain fog, and worse attention symptoms in some people, including ADHD populations.

Educational note: associations ≠ causation. Iron does not cause or cure ADHD.

**Practical step:** Ask for ferritin / iron studies — especially with fog, fatigue, restless legs, or heavy periods. Don’t megadose iron without clinical guidance.
