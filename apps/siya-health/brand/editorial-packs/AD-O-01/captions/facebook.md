## Post

Is online ADHD diagnosis legitimate?

It can be — when standards match real clinical care: licensed clinician, thorough history, validated tools as support, and follow-up.

Quiz-only paths and “guaranteed Rx” marketing are red flags. Telehealth itself isn’t the problem; shortcuts are.

**Practical step:** Checklist before paying — licensed clinician, history, validated tools, follow-up. Not quiz-only.
