# Facebook — AD-V-01

I start strong… then I vanish mid-task.

A lot of adults hear “lazy” for a pattern that may actually be lifelong attention / follow-through struggle.

Try this for 7 days: log 3 starts and note where momentum drops. If it’s been lifelong, a validated ADHD screener + a clinician conversation is a clearer next step than more self-blame.

More here: https://siya.health/blog/youre-not-lazy-signs-undiagnosed-adult-adhd
