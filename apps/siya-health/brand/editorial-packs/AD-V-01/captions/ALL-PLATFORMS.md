# ALL PLATFORMS — AD-V-01

## Core
I start strong… then I vanish mid-task.
Often called laziness — sometimes a lifelong attention / follow-through pattern.
Practical: 7-day log of 3 starts + where momentum drops → if lifelong, validated ADHD screener → clinician.
