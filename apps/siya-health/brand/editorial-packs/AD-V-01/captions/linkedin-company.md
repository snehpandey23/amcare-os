# LinkedIn Company — AD-V-01

“I start strong… then I vanish.”

At work, that often gets moralized as laziness or poor discipline.
Clinically, a lifelong follow-through pattern can be a clue worth evaluating — not a LinkedIn personality verdict.

Practical change: for 7 days, log three tasks you start with energy and note where momentum drops. If the pattern is lifelong, take a validated ADHD screener and discuss it with a clinician.
