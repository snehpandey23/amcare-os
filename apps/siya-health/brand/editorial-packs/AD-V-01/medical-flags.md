# Medical flags — AD-V-01

**Publish organic?** YES with educational disclaimer

## Safe
- Recognition: start-strong / fade pattern as a *clue*, not a diagnosis
- Lifelong pattern → screener → clinician
- Link to existing educational spoke

## Avoid
- “This means you have ADHD”
- Guaranteed diagnosis from a reel
- Med names, doses, “stop your meds”
- Fake urgency / limited-time booking pressure
