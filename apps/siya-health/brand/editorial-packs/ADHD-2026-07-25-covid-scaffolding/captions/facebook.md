# Facebook — ADHD-2026-07-25-covid-scaffolding (Part 1)

COVID didn’t give you ADHD — it took away the scaffolding.

Remote life removed routines and raised self-management demands overnight. For many adults, hidden patterns got loud — not a brand-new personality for everyone.

**Practical step:** Rebuild 3 scaffolds for 14 days (fixed wake · protected work/school block · accountability check-in). If lifelong + impairing → Talk to a Clinician / Book ADHD Evaluation.
