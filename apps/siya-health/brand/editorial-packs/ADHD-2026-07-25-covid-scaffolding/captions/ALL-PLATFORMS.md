## Caption

COVID didn’t give you ADHD.

It took away the scaffolding.

For a lot of adults, “fine” suddenly stopped working — not because a new personality appeared overnight, but because the rails disappeared: routines, nearby people, built-in start times, places that cue the next step.

ADHD leans hard on external structure. Lockdowns and remote life removed those rails while exploding self-management demands. Commute gone. Desk optional. School and work became a browser tab.

This isn’t “everyone got ADHD.” For many people it was a stress test — hidden patterns got loud when scaffolding vanished.

**Practical step (start now; full how-to in Part 2):** Rebuild three external scaffolds for 14 days — fixed wake time · one protected work/school block · one accountability check-in. Note what gets easier. If the pattern is lifelong and impairing, Talk to a Clinician or Book an ADHD Evaluation.

Educational only. Not a diagnosis. Screening and education ≠ clinical evaluation. Infection / Long COVID fog can be a separate conversation with your clinician — it is not the only explanation for what many people felt.
