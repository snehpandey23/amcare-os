# LinkedIn Company — ADHD-2026-07-25-covid-scaffolding (Part 1)

COVID didn’t invent ADHD for most adults.

It stress-tested executive function by removing external scaffolding — while remote school and work assumed multi-platform self-management.

That framing matters for employers, schools, and clinicians: diagnosis surges and “new” struggles often reflect context collapse, not a single infection storyline.

**Practical step:** Rebuild three scaffolds for 14 days (fixed wake · one protected work/school block · one accountability check-in). If the pattern is lifelong and impairing, Talk to a Clinician / Book ADHD Evaluation.

Part 2 covers school, remote work, and the rebuild in detail.
