# Medical flags — ADHD × COVID scaffolding series (Parts 1–2)

**Publish organic?** YES with educational disclaimer in captions

## Safe
- ADHD tightly linked to executive function; many people rely on external structure
- Lockdowns / remote transition removed scaffolds and raised self-management demands
- Online school and remote work assumed multi-platform self-start
- Not “everyone got ADHD”; for many adults it unmasked or amplified existing patterns
- One U.S. working-adult ASRS estimate rose during the pandemic (caption caveat only — not global prevalence doubling)
- Some people benefited when environments were adapted
- Practical: rebuild 3 scaffolds × 14 days → Talk to a Clinician / Book ADHD Evaluation if lifelong + impairing
- Infection / Long COVID fog is a separate emerging story — not the Part 1 spine

## Avoid
- “COVID caused lifelong adult ADHD”
- “Everyone got ADHD after COVID”
- Framing 4.4%→9.3% as world prevalence fact without study context
- DIY stimulant protocols for Long COVID or “new ADHD”
- Infection as the only explanation for diagnosis surge
- Founder LinkedIn unless requested
- Meet & Greet as primary CTA on educational packs
