# Medical flags — AD-V-02

**Publish organic?** YES with educational disclaimer (spoken + on-screen)

## Safe
- Late *recognition* / late diagnosis language (not adult-onset ADHD)
- Masking, people-pleasing, perfectionism, overachievement as lived patterns — not diagnostic criteria alone
- Inattentive presentation often missed vs hyperactive stereotype
- Anxiety / depression can co-occur or be prior labels — don’t claim all were “wrong”
- Hormones: “Many women notice ADHD symptoms shift with cycle, postpartum, or perimenopause” (association / clinical pattern)
- Estrogen interacts with pathways involved in attention (plain language, no “hormones cause ADHD”)
- Soft CTA: lifelong history with a licensed clinician; screening ≠ diagnosis

## Avoid
- Self-diagnosis from a Reel / “you definitely have ADHD”
- Internet BPD vs ADHD diagnosis
- “Hormones cause ADHD” / HRT as ADHD treatment
- DIY stimulant dose changes around cycle / pregnancy
- “ADHD in women is a trend”
- Guaranteed medication or “meds free” promises
- Scare-based motherhood / menopause fear marketing
- Absolute % claims (e.g. unverified “80% chance of…”) without primary source in brief

## Claim ladder (from women-hormones-adhd NUANCES)
```text
SAFE:   Many women notice ADHD symptoms shift with their cycle or perimenopause.
SAFE:   Late diagnosis in the 30s–40s is common when coping strategies stop working.
SAFE:   Girls/women may present with inattention + internalization more than visible hyperactivity.
CAUTION: Some clinicians explore cycle-aware care — only with your doctor.
AVOID:  Hormones cause ADHD. / Increase your dose before your period. / HRT treats ADHD.
```
