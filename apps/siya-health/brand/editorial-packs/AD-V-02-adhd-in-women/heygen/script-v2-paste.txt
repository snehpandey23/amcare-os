ADHD doesn’t always look like a hyper little boy.

---

In many women, it looks like inattention, disorganization, emotional intensity, and a mind that won’t quiet down — while the outside stays composed.

---

Girls often mask. They people-please. They overwork. They’re called anxious or “too sensitive” long before anyone asks about ADHD.

---

That’s one reason ADHD symptoms in women — especially inattentive ADHD in women — get recognized later. Late recognition isn’t a trend. It’s a presentation that didn’t match the stereotype.

---
