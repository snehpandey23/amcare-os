You’re not “suddenly getting worse” in your thirties or forties.

---

Many women with undiagnosed or under-recognized ADHD notice symptoms intensify when hormones shift — after pregnancy, across the cycle, or in perimenopause.

---

Estrogen interacts with brain systems involved in attention. When that buffer changes, masking gets harder: fog, overwhelm, emotional volatility, less capacity to push through.

---

That doesn’t mean hormones cause ADHD. It means biology can reveal a pattern that was already there.

---

What helps practically: track what you notice, reduce cognitive load, protect sleep, and get properly assessed — don’t sit in self-blame. Educational only — not medical advice. Individual care belongs with a licensed clinician.
