# Research bank — ADHD in women (compressed)

## Brief answers

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Topic | ADHD in women |
| Why now | Often overlooked / late diagnosed; high social volume |
| Audience | Women 25–50 · late recognition · hormonal impact on focus · masking exhaustion |
| Business goal | Consideration + action |

## Recurring VOC struggles (Reddit + comments)

1. **Good grades hid it** — called lazy for deadlines; overachievement via last-minute panic  
2. **Masking / people-pleasing** — cooperative “good girl”; exhaustion when mask fails  
3. **Mislabel path** — anxiety, depression, bipolar first; ADHD later  
4. **Child’s diagnosis → own recognition**  
5. **Postpartum / perimenopause crash** — coping buffer gone  
6. **Internal hyperactivity** — busy mind, not bouncing off walls  
7. **Emotional intensity / RSD** — “too sensitive,” fear of disappointing people  
8. **Success outside, distress inside**  
9. **Relief after language** — “I’m not broken, just different”  
10. **Want options** — including non-med paths (handle carefully: don’t promise)

## Format winners (what to steal structurally)

| Format | Why it worked | Siya adaptation |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| Couple conversation (IG) | Real dialogue, dual POV | Scripted dialogue OR interviewer + patient-style FAQ |
| Psychiatrist talking head | Authority + calm | Clinician avatar / founder tone, no hype |
| Therapist roleplay | Shows masking under questioning | Optional V2b — keep clinical, not theatrical |
| Text overlay on lifestyle B-roll | Hormones + motherhood story | Text-led + soft B-roll; careful claims |
| Podcast clip / book extract | Rapid “spot it” lists | Use sparingly; end with clinician CTA |
| Silent gestures + text (YT) | Soft, saveable, no VO fatigue | Strong for V5 |
| “It’s okay…” reframes | Safety + validation | Brand-aligned recognition |
| Sign → everyday example | Clear teaching rhythm | Use for V2 / V5 |
