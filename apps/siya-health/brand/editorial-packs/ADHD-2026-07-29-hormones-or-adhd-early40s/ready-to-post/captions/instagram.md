Early-40s brain fog… hormones or ADHD?

That either/or is usually too small. Some women notice focus and overwhelm get harder around cycle shifts, postpartum, or menopause — and researchers are still studying those links. ADHD patterns often stretch back years, not only this decade.

Don’t coin-flip from a reel. Bring lifelong attention history and mood or hormone context to a clinician.
