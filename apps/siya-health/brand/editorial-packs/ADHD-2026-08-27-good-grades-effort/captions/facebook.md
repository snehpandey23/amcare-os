Good grades don't show what things actually cost behind closed doors.

Many adults who receive an ADHD diagnosis in their 30s, 40s, or 50s were told they were "fine" in school simply because their report cards looked good. What wasn't visible was the panic-fueled all-nighter, the disorganized backpack, and the exhausting effort required to stay afloat.

Intelligence and coping systems can compensate for executive dysfunction for years — until life demands increase.
