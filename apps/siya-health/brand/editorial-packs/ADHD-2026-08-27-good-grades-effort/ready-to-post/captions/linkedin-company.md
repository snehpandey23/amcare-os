In academic and professional environments, performance is evaluated on deliverables, not the internal friction required to produce them.

For many high-achieving adults with undiagnosed ADHD, intellectual ability and urgency often serve as compensatory mechanisms. Outward success can obscure chronic executive strain, leading to delayed recognition and unnecessary burnout.
