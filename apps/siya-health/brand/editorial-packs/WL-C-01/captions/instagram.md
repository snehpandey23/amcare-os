Compounded vs branded GLP-1

Same buzzword. Not always the same product story.

Shortages, cost, and “same thing cheaper” ads made this confusing. Branded products are FDA-approved. Compounded versions follow different rules and quality systems — which means questions are appropriate.

**One practical step:** Before you pay or inject, ask pharmacy/source, FDA status, and what you’re actually receiving.

Educational only. Not prescribing or pharmacy advice.
