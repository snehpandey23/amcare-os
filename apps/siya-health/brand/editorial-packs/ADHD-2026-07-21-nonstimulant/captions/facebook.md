## Post

Stimulants aren’t the only ADHD medication class.

Non-stimulants can be appropriate for some people — anxiety sensitivity, sleep problems, substance-use history, or preference for non-controlled options are common reasons the conversation comes up.

They are not identical to stimulants in onset or side-effect profile. Fit is individual.

**Practical step:** Bring side-effect, substance-use, and anxiety history to discuss non-stimulant options with your clinician.
