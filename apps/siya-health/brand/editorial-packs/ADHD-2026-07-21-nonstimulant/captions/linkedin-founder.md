## Draft A

Patients often arrive sure stimulants are “the ADHD med” — or sure they can’t take them.

Non-stimulants sit in that middle space: different pathways, different tradeoffs, sometimes the better fit when anxiety, sleep, or substance history reshapes the risk–benefit math.

I don’t crown a winner on social media. I ask what they’ve tried, what they feared, and what their body already told us.

Curious how other clinicians introduce non-stimulant options without making patients feel like they’re getting the “lesser” treatment.

Educational reflection only. Not advice for any individual.
