## Caption

Food noise is real.

Weight loss isn’t only calories — it’s mental load: constant planning, bargaining, cravings that don’t match hunger, shame after every “slip.”

Mood and attention shape appetite. Depression, anxiety, and ADHD can change reward, impulsivity, and follow-through — that’s systems medicine, not character judgment.

GLP-1s may quiet food noise for some people. They don’t replace mood or ADHD care when those need attention.

**One practical step:** Name food noise out loud. Ask whether ADHD or mood needs concurrent care alongside weight treatment.

Educational only. Not a diagnosis.
