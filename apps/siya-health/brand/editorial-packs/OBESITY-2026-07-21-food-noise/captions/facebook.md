## Post

Food noise is real — and it affects weight-loss outcomes.

Mental health and ADHD can shape impulsivity, reward, and follow-through. That’s not laziness.

GLP-1 medications may quiet food noise for some people; they don’t replace concurrent mental-health care when it’s needed.

**Practical step:** Name food noise. Ask if ADHD or mood needs care alongside weight treatment.
