---

## Claim ladder

**Safe:**
- Oxytocin is involved in social bonding, trust, empathy, and connection.
- Released during warm touch, positive social interaction, caregiving, laughter.
- Daily habits can create more opportunities for bonding.
- Some foods support overall mood/nutrition; evidence for direct oxytocin “boosting” is limited/popular-science level.

**Avoid:**
- “Cure loneliness / depression / anxiety with oxytocin.”
- Guaranteed hormone increases from specific foods.
- Medical dosing, supplements, or “hack your hormones” language.
- Using this as ADHD, hormone, or psychiatric treatment advice.

---

## Why we cut the list to 5

Your draft had 8+ ways. For carousel retention, keep **5 strongest, most doable** actions:

1. Hug someone you care about (friend / parent / partner)  
2. Give a real compliment  
3. Help someone with something small  
4. Listen to a favorite song (or do a hobby you love)  
5. Make someone laugh (or play with children if that fits your life)

Deferred to caption / Story / second static:
- Giving gifts (overlaps with helping + complimenting)
- Food list as a separate “supportive foods” slide (kept soft)
