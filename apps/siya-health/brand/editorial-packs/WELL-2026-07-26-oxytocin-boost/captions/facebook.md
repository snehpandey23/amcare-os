## Instagram

You can feel lonely even when your calendar is full.

Oxytocin is often called the “love hormone.” It plays a role in bonding, trust, empathy, and emotional connection — and it tends to show up during warm touch, kindness, caregiving, and shared laughter.

5 simple ways to create more connection this week:
1. Hug someone you love
2. Give a real compliment
3. Help with one small thing
4. Play your favorite song / do your hobby
5. Make someone laugh (or play with children if that fits)

Some nourishing foods can support mood too — dark chocolate, banana, nuts, oranges, yogurt, egg yolks, seeds, unsweetened cocoa. They’re support for a nourished body, not a hormone prescription.

Practical change: pick ONE habit for 7 days. Each night, rate how connected you felt (1–5).

If loneliness or low mood stays stuck, talk with a clinician. Habits help. They don’t replace care.

Save this for a soft week.
