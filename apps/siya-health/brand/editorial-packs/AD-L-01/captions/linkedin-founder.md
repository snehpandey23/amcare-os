## Draft A

Patients ask me: “If I start this, am I signing up forever — and is forever safe?”

I don’t answer with a meme. I answer with monitoring: blood pressure, weight, sleep, mood, and whether the benefit still outweighs the burden.

Long-term use is common. Unmonitored use is the problem I worry about more than a calendar year count.

Curious how other clinicians talk about “forever” without trapping people in fear or false guarantees.

Educational reflection only. Not advice for any individual.
