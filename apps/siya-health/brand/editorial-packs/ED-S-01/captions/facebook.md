## Post

Sildenafil for erectile dysfunction — what to expect.

It helps erectile response with stimulation; it isn’t an automatic libido switch. Timing varies. Nitrate interactions are critical to rule out.

**Practical step:** Nitrate/cardiac med review + timing expectations with your clinician.
