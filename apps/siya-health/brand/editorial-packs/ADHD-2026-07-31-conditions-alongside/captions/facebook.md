ADHD and other mental health conditions often travel together.

Symptoms of depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, PTSD, and binge eating can overlap
with ADHD — or exist alongside it. Getting a clear picture helps treatment address the
whole person, not just one part of the story.

This post is for education. It is not a diagnosis and should not replace professional
medical advice. If symptoms are affecting your daily life, speak with a qualified
healthcare professional.
