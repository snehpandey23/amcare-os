ADHD rarely shows up alone.

Depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, PTSD, and binge eating disorder can all occur
alongside ADHD — and they can also look like each other.

That overlap is why a thorough evaluation matters. Treating one condition while missing
another can leave symptoms unfinished.

This carousel is education, not a diagnosis. Social media cannot tell you which
conditions you do or don't have.

If your symptoms don't fit neatly into one box and they're affecting work,
relationships, or daily life — talking with a qualified clinician is the right next step.

This content is for education only and should not replace professional medical advice.
