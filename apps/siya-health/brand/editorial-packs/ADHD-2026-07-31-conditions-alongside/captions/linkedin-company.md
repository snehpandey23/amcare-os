ADHD in adults is frequently accompanied by other conditions — mood, anxiety, trauma-
related, and eating disorders among them.

Symptoms overlap. Conditions can coexist. One diagnosis can mask another. A
comprehensive assessment that looks at attention, mood, anxiety, trauma history, sleep,
and eating patterns helps clinicians design care that fits the whole person.

Educational content only. Not a substitute for professional medical advice. Do not
self-diagnose from social media.
