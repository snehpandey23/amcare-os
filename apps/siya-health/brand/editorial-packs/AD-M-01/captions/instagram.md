## Caption

Stimulants aren’t the only option.

Non-stimulant ADHD medications can make sense for some people — especially with anxiety flares on stimulants, sleep issues, substance-use history, or a preference for non-controlled options.

They work through different pathways. Onset and side effects aren’t identical to stimulants. “Better” depends on your map — not a TikTok ranking.

**One practical step:** Bring your side-effect, substance-use, and anxiety history to your clinician and ask whether a non-stimulant option belongs in the conversation.

Educational only. Not prescribing advice.
