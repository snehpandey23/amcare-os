## Caption

How to safely get prescriptions online

Convenience is fine. Shortcuts that skip medicine aren’t.

Red flags: no exam/visit, guaranteed controlled substances, pressure to pay first, vague clinician identity. Safe usually looks boring — licensed clinician, real visit, verifiable pharmacy, follow-up.

**One practical step:** Use the red-flag checklist before you pay. No exam or guaranteed controlled Rx? Walk away.
