## Script

**Hook (0–3s)**
On-screen: **Phone in my hand. Nothing gets done.**
Visual: hand holding phone, task list glowing untouched in background.

**Beat 1 (3–10s)**
VO/caption: “I want to start. I just… can’t.”
Visual: same pose held longer — time-lapse light change (afternoon → evening) to show hours passing.

**Beat 2 (10–18s)**
On-screen: **Sometimes it feels physical — like something’s holding the switch down.**
Visual: soft illustrated “weight” metaphor over the hand/chest, not literal medical imagery.

**Beat 3 (18–25s)**
On-screen: **“I match most of these signs… but I’m also up all night with bad sleep. What now?”**
Caption/VO: Poor sleep and low mood can look a lot like this too — that’s exactly what an evaluation sorts out.

**Close (25–32s)**
On-screen: **Write down 3 examples. Bring them to a clinician — not just a checklist.**
