## Draft A

Patients ask me whether online ADHD diagnosis is “real” or a loophole.

I ask what the process looked like. Licensed clinician? Real history? Validated tools used as tools — not as the diagnosis? Follow-up?

Telehealth can meet that bar. A quiz that sells certainty cannot.

Curious how other clinicians help patients spot the difference without painting all digital care as unsafe.

Educational reflection only. Not advice for any individual.
