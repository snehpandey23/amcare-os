## Instagram
Is online ADHD diagnosis legit?

It can be — when the process looks like real medicine.

You’ve seen the other version: 5-minute quizzes, guaranteed Rx, no history, no follow-up. That’s not evaluation.

Telehealth isn’t the problem. Skipping clinical standards is. A legitimate path still needs a licensed clinician, full history, validated tools as support (not the whole diagnosis), and follow-up.

**One practical step:** Use this checklist before you pay — licensed clinician, history, validated tools, follow-up. Skip quiz-only paths.

Educational only. Not a diagnosis.
