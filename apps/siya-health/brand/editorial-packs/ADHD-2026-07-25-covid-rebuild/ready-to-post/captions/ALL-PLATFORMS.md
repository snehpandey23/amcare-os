## Caption

Remote school. Remote work.

That was the executive-function stress test.

Online school assumed a manager — tabs, deadlines, cameras, self-start without hallway cues. ADHD teens often needed more rails, not fewer.

Work-from-home isn’t automatically easier focus. No commute boundary. Meeting pile-ups. For many adults, executive function *became* the job.

Some people thrived when environments were adapted. Many didn’t — and the struggle looked brand new when every external prompt disappeared.

**Practical step:** For 14 days, rebuild three scaffolds:
1. Fixed wake time  
2. One protected work / school block  
3. One accountability check-in  

Note what gets easier. If the pattern is lifelong and impairing, Talk to a Clinician or Book an ADHD Evaluation.

Educational only. Not a diagnosis. Structure helps many people — it does not replace a clinical evaluation when ADHD is on the table.
