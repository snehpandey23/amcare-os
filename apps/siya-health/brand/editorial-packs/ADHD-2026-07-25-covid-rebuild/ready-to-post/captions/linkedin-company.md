# LinkedIn Company — ADHD-2026-07-25-covid-rebuild (Part 2)

Remote school and remote work didn’t only change location — they changed the executive-function load.

Online learning assumed multi-platform self-management. Hybrid work removed commute boundaries and hallway cues. Some people thrived in adapted environments; many experienced a “new” struggle that was partly a scaffolding collapse.

**Practical step for teams and individuals:** For 14 days rebuild three scaffolds — fixed wake time, one protected deep-work/school block, one accountability check-in — and notice what improves.

If the pattern is lifelong and impairing, Talk to a Clinician / Book ADHD Evaluation.
