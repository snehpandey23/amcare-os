## Caption teaching beats (not on-frame — draft for later)

- The “tomorrow will be different” vow is recognition language, not a moral failure.
- Self-blame often shows up before a useful label does.
- Practical change: if this is longstanding and impairing, learn the domains — then talk with a clinician. Social media is not a diagnosis.
