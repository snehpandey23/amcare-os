Many high-performing adults describe the same private pattern: sitting at the laptop with a clear list — and a start that won’t come.

The next-day vow (“tomorrow will be different”) and the self-blame that follows are often read as character. Clinically, they can map to executive dysfunction — inconsistent activation and regulation, not absence of effort or intelligence.
