## Post

Compounded vs branded GLP-1 medications — what’s the difference?

Branded products are FDA-approved. Compounded versions are prepared under different frameworks. That doesn’t automatically make compounding “bad” — and it doesn’t make it identical.

**Practical step:** Ask pharmacy/source, FDA status, and what you’re actually receiving before paying or injecting.
