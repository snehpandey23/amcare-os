## Caption

Ambien isn’t harmless sleep candy

Useful for some. Risky when treated casually.

It can help sleep onset for some people — with tradeoffs like impairment, next-day fog, and rare complex sleep behaviors. Alcohol mixing is a hard no. Next-day judgment can still be off.

**One practical step:** Never mix with alcohol. Discuss next-day impairment and safer-use boundaries with your clinician.

Educational only. Not prescribing advice.
