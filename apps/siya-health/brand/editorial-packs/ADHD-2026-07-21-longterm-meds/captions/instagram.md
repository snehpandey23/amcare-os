## Caption

“Is this safe forever?”

The honest answer needs monitoring — not a slogan.

People worry about heart, appetite, sleep, mood, and whether they’ll need medication forever. Long-term ADHD medication use is common under clinical care — but it isn’t “set and forget.”

Risk isn’t one internet number. It depends on dose, health history, co-meds, and how you’re followed.

**One practical step:** Schedule monitoring (blood pressure, weight, sleep, mood) with your prescriber. Don’t stop abruptly without a plan.

Educational only. Not prescribing advice.
