## Post

“Is ADHD medication safe long-term?”

Many adults take ADHD medication for years under clinical care. Safety isn’t a yes/no slogan — it’s monitoring plus individual risk–benefit.

People reasonably ask about heart, appetite, sleep, and mood. Internet absolutes skip dose, history, and follow-up.

**Practical step:** Schedule monitoring (BP, weight, sleep, mood) with your prescriber. Don’t stop abruptly without a clinician plan.
