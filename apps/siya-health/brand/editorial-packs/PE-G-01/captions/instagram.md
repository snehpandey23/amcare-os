## Caption

Glutathione & peptides — what’s real?

Hype is loud. Evidence is uneven.

“Peptides” isn’t one proven therapy — each compound has different evidence strength. Marketing promises glow, recovery, fat loss, longevity certainty. Vendor quality is part of the risk.

**One practical step:** Ask for evidence grade (what outcome, in whom?). Avoid unverified peptide vendors. Stay curious about FDA-approval status without turning 2026 into a hype headline.

Educational only. Not prescribing or supplement advice.
