# Medical flags — PE-G-01

**Publish organic?** YES with educational disclaimer  
**Paid?** Prefer clinical stamp; high woo-adjacent risk

## Safe
- Evidence grade varies by specific compound and claim
- Many marketing claims outrun clinical evidence
- Avoid unverified peptide vendors / research-chemical gray markets
- Ask what is FDA-approved vs wellness-marketed — carefully, without hype
- Educational skepticism + curiosity

## Do not add
- Miracle longevity/glow guarantees
- DIY peptide dosing stacks
- Vendor recommendations for unverified sources
- Declaring broad FDA-approval wins for 2026 as fact hype
- Implying glutathione IV cures disease
