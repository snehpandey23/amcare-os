# Medical flags — ADHD-C-01

**Publish organic?** YES with educational disclaimer

## Safe
- Time blindness, RSD (rejection sensitive dysphoria), hyperfocus-then-distance, and limerence described as commonly reported adult-ADHD experience patterns — not presented as standalone diagnoses
- Reframing "symptoms vs. character" without excusing harmful behavior
- Practical, low-risk systems (pause, shared lists, fair task split, naming patterns aloud)
- CTA to a licensed clinician for a full evaluation

## Avoid
- Diagnosing the viewer or a specific partner
- Suggesting limerence/intense crushes justify acting on them, cheating, or breaking commitments — feelings are common; actions and consent are a separate, personal responsibility
- Presenting RSD, limerence, hyperfocus-then-distance as official clinical diagnoses (community/clinical-descriptive terms, not DSM categories)
- Guaranteeing relationship outcomes ("this will fix your relationship")
- Medication claims or dosing language
- Shaming either the ADHD partner or the non-ADHD partner

## Required
- Disclaimer: "Educational only. Not a diagnosis or a substitute for therapy or medical care."
