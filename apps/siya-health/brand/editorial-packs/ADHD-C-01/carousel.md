**Practical change:** Name one pattern out loud with your partner this week, then pick one system to try together.

## Claim ladder

**Safe:** Naming time blindness, RSD, hyperfocus-then-distance, and limerence as documented, common adult-ADHD experience patterns (community + clinical literature); reframing symptoms vs. character; practical systems (pause, shared lists, task split); CTA to professional support.

**Avoid:**
- Diagnosing the viewer or their partner
- Framing limerence/intense crushes as an excuse to act on infidelity — feelings are common and not a moral failing, but choices and consent still matter; do not blur that line
- Guaranteeing a relationship outcome
- Presenting RSD or hyperfocus as clinical diagnoses on their own (they are described patterns, not standalone DSM diagnoses)
- Medication or treatment claims
