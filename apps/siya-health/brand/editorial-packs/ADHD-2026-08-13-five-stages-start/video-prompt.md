## B — Real human on camera (Format 1 — optional alt / upgrade)

Only if Format 2 performs and talent is booked. Same spine as beats 0–7, delivered as a knowing list to camera — joke on the **pattern**, never on the viewer. Soft blog CTA. No AI avatar.

### Spoken spine (upgrade only — draft)

```text
The five stages of “I’m starting now.”

One: open the laptop. This time, for real.
Two: open a dozen tabs that have nothing to do with the task.
Three: suddenly the desk needs cleaning.
Four: clear the evening so you can focus — and somehow still not start.
Five: swear tomorrow will be different. Repeat from one.

It’s funny because it’s true. It’s usually not laziness —
it’s often executive dysfunction.

Full guide at siya.health. Educational only.
```
