## Instagram

The 5 stages of “I’m starting now.”

1. Open the laptop. This time, for real.  
2. Open twelve tabs that have nothing to do with the task.  
3. Suddenly the desk needs cleaning.  
4. Clear the evening so you can focus… and somehow still not start.  
5. Tell yourself tomorrow will be different. Repeat from 1.

Funny because it’s true. Usually not laziness — often executive dysfunction.
