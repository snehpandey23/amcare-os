# Series brief — Real Comments. Real Questions.

**Parent insight:** `ADHD-2026-07-31-didnt-add-up`
**Status:** Pilot approved conceptually · first five posts mapped · produce one at a time
**Rule:** Internet supplies the question. Existing Knowledge Hub supplies the answer. We do not invent topics when a live guide already covers them.

## Format lock (every post)

1. **Hook** — a real-feeling comment or question (composite or anonymized public phrasing — never an identifiable person's words without permission).
2. **Recognition** — one sentence that makes the reader feel seen.
3. **Clarification** — what the experience may relate to (never "you have X").
4. **Soft path** — link to the matching `/answers` or `/blog` guide + Meet & Greet.
