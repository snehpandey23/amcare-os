3. **Tree-brain beat (12–20s)** — Quote: "My brain has always felt like a tree." Woman at desk, gaze drifting. Branching-thought visual metaphor (subtle — no cartoon trees). Super: *Recognition isn't diagnosis.*
4. **Second-opinion beat (20–28s)** — Quote: "I was told it wasn't ADHD." Man mid-60s at table. Super: *A second opinion is about accuracy — in either direction.* (compliance lock — keep this line).
5. **Thesis (28–34s)** — "The goal isn't to collect diagnoses. / It's to understand what's causing your symptoms."
6. **Close (34–40s)** — "Something still doesn't add up." · Meet & Greet CTA · `siya.health · Educational only`.
