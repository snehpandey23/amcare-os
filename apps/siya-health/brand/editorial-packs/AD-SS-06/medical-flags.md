# Medical flags — AD-SS-06

**Publish organic?** YES with educational disclaimer

## Safe
- Hyperfocus as lived, common self-report — framed as "some people," not universal
- Practical change (external alarm) is concrete and non-medical

## Avoid
- "Hyperfocus = ADHD superpower" glamorization without the cost side
- Claiming hyperfocus alone is diagnostic
- Any neurochemistry explanation (dopamine, etc. — PART 3 not cleared)
