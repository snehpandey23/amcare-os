Is ADHD a superpower?

The phrase helps some people name strengths. Others find it dismisses real impairment — unfinished work, emotional overload, interest-based attention that looks like “laziness” from the outside.

Clinically honest frame: some traits can become strengths with support, and the struggles still deserve care — not a slogan.
