I’ve been thinking about this clinically:

“Is ADHD a superpower?” is the wrong binary. Some adults describe real silver linings — creativity, energy, hyperfocus. Many also live with impairment that a slogan can’t fix.

The useful question isn’t whether ADHD is a gift. It’s whether someone has the support and clinical care to work with their brain without shame.
