**Publish organic?** YES with educational disclaimer (spoken + on-screen)

## Safe
- “Superpower” as contested cultural framing, not clinical fact
- Adults can report strengths / “silver linings” (creativity, energy, hyperfocus) without calling ADHD a gift
- Interest-based attention / conditional hyperfocus as lived experience language
- Support and clinical care matter; struggles are valid
- Soft invite to clinician conversation

## Avoid
- “ADHD is always a superpower”
- “ADHD is never a strength”
- Cure / hack / stop-the-feeling promises
- Self-diagnosis or guaranteed Rx from the video
- Stimulant marketing or “meds fast”
- Mocking either side of the Reddit debate
- Founder LinkedIn hard CTA (Company captions OK with one soft link)
