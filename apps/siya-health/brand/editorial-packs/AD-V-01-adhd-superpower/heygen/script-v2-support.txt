ADHD feels like a superpower… until you don’t have support. Then it just feels like drowning.

---

With the right people, the right systems, and the right care — creativity and drive can show up. Without that? Trial and error. Overwhelm. That emotional rollercoaster people keep talking about.

---

So if the superpower posts make you feel left out — you’re not failing at ADHD. You’re describing what happens when a real condition meets a world that wasn’t built for your brain.

---

Support isn’t a vibe. It’s structure, skills, and clinical care when you need it. This is educational content only — not medical advice. If you’re stuck, a licensed clinician can help you sort the next step.
