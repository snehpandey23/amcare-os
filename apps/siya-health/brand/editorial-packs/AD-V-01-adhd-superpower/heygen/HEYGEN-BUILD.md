## How to build in HeyGen (click path)

1. **New video** → **Avatar** (or Instant Avatar if using a Siya-approved likeness) → **9:16**.
2. Paste **entire** script from `script-v1-paste.txt` into Script.
3. Split into **5 scenes** at the `---` markers (or create 5 scenes and paste each block).
4. Add **Text overlay** per scene (table below). Keep overlays short — max ~6 words/line.
5. Enable **Captions** (burned-in or export SRT). Style: high-contrast white/cream on dark navy bar, or clean white with soft shadow — not emoji-heavy.
6. Optional B-roll / stock (scenes 2–3 only): half-finished to-do list, browser tabs, cloudy-then-clear sky. Keep avatar primary; B-roll ≤40% of runtime.
7. End card (scene 5): logo top-left if available + disclaimer line.
8. Export **MP4 1080×1920**, then post with `../captions/instagram.md`.
