# Scene-by-scene — HeyGen Video 1

Use with `script-v1-paste.txt`. Each `---` in the paste file = new HeyGen scene.

## Scene 1 — Hook (0–5s)

**Spoken**  
Is ADHD a superpower? The internet says yes. A lot of people living it say… it’s more complicated.

**Text overlay**  
`Is ADHD a superpower?`

**Avatar**  
Medium close-up, direct to camera. Slight pause before “more complicated.”

**Visual**  
Solid soft cream or calm office. No B-roll yet.

---

## Scene 2 — Recognition (5–14s)

**Spoken**  
Because for many adults, that so-called superpower only shows up when everything goes perfectly — the right task, the right pressure, zero friction. One small inconvenience… and the interest is gone.

**Text overlay**  
`Only when everything’s perfect…`

**Optional B-roll**  
Half-checked to-do list / laptop with many tabs (stock OK). Keep avatar picture-in-picture or cut back to avatar for last line.

---

## Scene 3 — Clarity (14–26s)

**Spoken**  
Creativity, hyperfocus, energy — those can be real. Some adults even describe real silver linings. But ADHD is still a neurodevelopmental condition. Strengths don’t cancel the hard parts: unfinished work, emotional overload, that one voice saying six things at once.

**Text overlays** (two beats if HeyGen allows timed text)  
1. `Strengths can be real`  
2. `Struggles still count`

**Avatar**  
Calm, even tone on “neurodevelopmental condition” — no scare energy.

---

## Scene 4 — Reframe (26–34s)

**Spoken**  
So the honest line isn’t “ADHD is a superpower.” It’s this: with the right support, some traits can become strengths — and the struggles still deserve care, not a slogan.

**Text overlay**  
`Not a slogan. A condition.`

---

## Scene 5 — CTA + disclaimer (34–40s)

**Spoken**  
If this feels familiar, you’re not imagining it. This is educational content only — not medical advice. A licensed clinician can help you figure out a clearer next step.

**Text overlay**  
`Educational · Not medical advice`

**End card (optional)**  
Siya Health wordmark (top-left) · soft line: “Talk with a clinician if this resonates.”
