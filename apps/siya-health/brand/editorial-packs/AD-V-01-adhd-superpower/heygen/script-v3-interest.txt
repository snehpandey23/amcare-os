It’s not laziness. It’s interest-based attention.

---

A boring, necessary task can feel impossible. A weird, high-stakes, fascinating problem? Suddenly you’re locked in. Same brain. Different fuel.

---

That’s why “ADHD is a superpower” posts miss people. Hyperfocus on the exciting problem looks like a gift. Avoiding the boring paperwork looks like a character flaw. It’s often the same wiring.

---

Naming it doesn’t fix it — but it can stop the shame spiral. Strategies and clinical care help you work with that brain, not against it. Educational content only — not medical advice.
