## Caption

"Nothing halfway" is how a lot of adults with ADHD describe their emotional experience — full overload, or total shutdown, with very little in between.

That pattern gets misread constantly as a lack of willpower. It isn't. It's emotional dysregulation — a well-documented, often-overlooked side of adult ADHD that shows up as hard-to-steer feelings, not a character issue. Research on adult ADHD links this kind of emotional intensity to real difficulty at work and in relationships.

One practical first step: track it for two weeks — what triggered it, how long it lasted, how much sleep you had — and bring that log into a conversation with a clinician.
