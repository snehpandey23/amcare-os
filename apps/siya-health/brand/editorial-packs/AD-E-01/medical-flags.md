# Medical flags — AD-E-01

**Publish organic?** YES with educational disclaimer

## Safe
- Lived intensity/freeze language ("nothing halfway," overloaded or frozen)
- Willpower myth correction (emotional dysregulation ≠ lack of willpower)
- Soft ADHD–emotion link ("ADHD can show up in emotion too," "one of the most overlooked sides of ADHD")
- 2-week trigger/duration/sleep log as a practical, non-clinical first step
- Naming emotional dysregulation as a real, common pattern **without** presenting it as a standalone DSM diagnosis
- Screening framed explicitly as screening, not diagnosis
- CTA driving to free screening / evaluation as the primary action

## Blocked — do not use in this pack
- **Stimulant/medication claims** (e.g. "medication reduces irritability," emotional blunting effects) — clinical-only, not for this creative
- **Bipolar misdiagnosis statistics** or "often misdiagnosed as bipolar" framed as fact — not sourced, not usable
- **RSD framed as a formal DSM diagnosis** — it is a described pattern, not a standalone diagnosis
- **ADHD vs. PTSD (or vs. bipolar) comparison** content — deferred, not in this pack
- **Self-diagnosis language** — never imply a screener or this video can confirm ADHD
- **Partner-blaming "eggshells" framing** — do not center the non-ADHD partner's frustration or imply the ADHD partner is at fault for others' fear/anxiety
- **Any invented statistic** ("1 in 3...", specific prevalence %, etc.) — do not use unless sourced and verified

## Required
- Disclaimer: "Educational. Not medical advice or a diagnosis."
- Crisis line: if referencing hospitalization/NSSI-adjacent lived experience from source Reddit threads, do **not** dramatize it on-screen; redirect instead to "if you're in crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line" as a general safety note — do not use specific personal crisis anecdotes from the research as video content.
