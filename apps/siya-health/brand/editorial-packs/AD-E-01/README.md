# AD-E-01

ADHD & Emotional Dysregulation — "Nothing halfway" (evaluation-focused reel)

**Insight:** ADHD emotional dysregulation — "nothing halfway." Feelings arrive at full volume or shut the system down entirely; it's one of the most overlooked sides of ADHD.
**Audience:** 18–55, all genders — overload/freeze, self-blame/guilt loops, work–relationship strain, fear of stigma or evaluation.
**Funnel:** Awareness → Consideration → **Evaluation** (this pack's video objective is explicitly to drive screening/evaluation, not just inform).
**Formats:** Reel (avatar-free, HeyGen-ready) + carousel text/visual brief (from source research report, ready for design).
**Practical change:** 2-week emotion + trigger log → bring it to a clinician; free screening as the low-friction first step.
**Spokes:** `/blog/adhd-symptoms-overlooked` · `/answers/rejection-sensitivity-adhd` · `/adhd-care` · `/adhd-screening`

**Sources:** APA Monitor (2024) · Reddit r/ADHD (ntrhqy, 1bdcoyj), r/ADHD_partners (1sma3jh), r/adhd_anxiety (1d9tv05) · Tiny Therapy Collective · PubMed 37747111, 34759846, 37470198

## Objective (important — read before editing)

This video's job is **evaluation, not just information.** Every beat should build toward "find out for yourself" rather than stopping at "here's what emotional dysregulation is." The CTA is the free screening — a low-friction, low-stigma first action — not a generic "talk to someone" line.

## What's deliberately excluded (per medical flags)

Stimulant/medication effects, bipolar misdiagnosis stats, RSD-as-DSM-diagnosis framing, ADHD-vs-PTSD comparison, self-diagnosis language, partner-blaming "eggshells" framing, and any invented statistic. See `medical-flags.md`.
