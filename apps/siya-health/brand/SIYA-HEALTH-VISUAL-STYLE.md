**Graphic social posts (carousels / illustrated statics):** follow `SIYA-SOCIAL-POST-STYLE.md`  
(navy type · pink→purple keyword gradients · off-white + lavender curves · 3D medical illustrations · corner logo · `n/5` pills). That is the restored historical Siya social system.

Create a premium, modern healthcare visual identity (for photo/video).

The overall aesthetic should feel **clean, trustworthy, calming, and evidence-based**.

The visual style should resemble a **high-end medical practice** rather than a social media advertisement.

---

## Inspiration

Think modern private healthcare clinic.

- Apple-level minimalism
- Mayo Clinic professionalism
- Calm documentary storytelling
- Premium telehealth experience

**Never feel like a commercial.**

---

## Color palette

### Primary

| Name | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| Soft Purple | `#7B61FF` | Accents, logo gradient anchor, key UI |
| Lavender | `#CDB8FF` | Soft fills, secondary accent |
| Warm White | `#FAFAFA` | Primary canvas |
| Cream | `#F8F5F2` | Type bands, paper fields |
| Light Beige | `#EFE8E3` | Secondary surfaces |

### Secondary

| Name | Hex | Use |
|------|-----|-----|
| Soft Blue | `#EAF4FF` | Calm secondary fields |
| Light Grey | `#F2F4F7` | Dividers, quiet chrome |
| Muted Sage | `#DCE8E1` | Soft nature accents |

**Rules:** Plenty of white space. Avoid saturated colors. Avoid bright reds, oranges, or neon.

---

## Lighting

- Natural daylight
- Soft window lighting
- Warm morning light
- No harsh shadows
- No dramatic lighting
- Bright but calming
- Premium healthcare atmosphere

---

## Camera style (video)

- Slow cinematic push-ins
- Eye-level camera
- Natural handheld movement
- Medium shots
- Close-up detail shots
- Shallow depth of field
- Clean compositions
- No shaky camera
- No quick zooms
- No TikTok-style editing

---

## Background

- Modern medical clinic
- Private consultation room
- Minimal office
- Soft wood textures
- White walls
- Indoor plants
- Large windows
- Minimal décor
- Premium healthcare environment

---

## People

- Diverse adults of different ages and backgrounds
- Patients natural and authentic
- Providers approachable, professional, compassionate
- Natural smiles
- Relaxed body language
- Avoid exaggerated emotions
- Avoid overly posed stock-photo expressions

---

## Graphics

- Minimal medical illustrations
- Simple brain icons
- Hair follicle illustrations (when relevant)
- Subtle line animations
- Clean medical diagrams
- Minimal labels
- Elegant typography
- No cluttered infographics

---

## Text style

- Minimal on-screen text
- One message at a time
- Large typography
- Short sentences
- Plenty of spacing
- Avoid paragraphs

On Instagram statics/carousels, also follow `INSTAGRAM-STATIC.md`:

- Submerged corner logo (once) — no background watermark stamp
- Quiet footer: `siya.health  ·  All content for educational purposes only`
- Company posts need ≥1 practical change (`EDITORIAL-TEST.md`)

---

## Music (video)

- Soft piano
- Gentle ambient music
- Warm acoustic textures
- Hopeful · relaxing · minimal
- Never dramatic
- Never suspenseful

---

## Transitions (video)

- Soft dissolve
- Fade
- Cross dissolve
- Gentle slide
- No flashy transitions
- No glitch effects
- No spinning animations

---

## Overall feeling

The viewer should immediately feel:

- Safe
- Understood
- Calm
- Supported
- Professionally cared for

Communicate **medical expertise, compassion, and trust** while maintaining a premium healthcare brand identity consistent across all Siya Health® educational content.
