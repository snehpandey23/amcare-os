## Design statement (locked from samples)

**Siya Health social creatives should feel like a trusted clinician who also understands real life.**

Human warmth first. Clear teaching second. Brand + contact close the frame.

Not a cold clinic brochure. Not a 3D product deck by default. Not shame, fear, or hard-sell.

---

## Shared design DNA (present in ≥4 of 6)

1. **Human-first imagery** — real lifestyle photos *or* friendly illustrated people (not anatomy-only by default)
2. **Navy + magenta emphasis** — navy for authority type; magenta/plum for 1–3 key words
3. **Soft lavender / cream / light-blue fields** — airy healthcare feel, not dark mode
4. **Scannable teaching blocks** — icon rows, checklists, short callout boxes
5. **Soft personality accents** — small hearts, botanical line art, occasional script word, circular magenta “feeling” badge
6. **Brand lockup** — Siya mark visible; awareness/education covers often use a **footer bar** with phone `(215) 445-1244` + `www.siya.health` + optional tagline *Care. Compassion. Connection.*
7. **Empathy over alarm** — hopeful faces, warm light, “you’re not alone” framing

**Outlier note:** Sample 6 (“Meeting Genius”) is full-bleed lifestyle + handwritten overlay with **weak brand lockup**. Keep its *empathy* and *paradox framing*; do **not** treat missing logo/footer as the standard.

## What this is NOT (vs earlier lock)
