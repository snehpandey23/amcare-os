**Start here every morning:** [`DAILY-PLAN.md`](./DAILY-PLAN.md)

This folder is the **operating system for the marketing team** — daily rhythm, objectives, tracking, and the full workflow from research through distribution and ads.

It sits on top of Brand OS (voice, creative system, editorial test) and connects to the existing Content Tracker in `04-Content-Tracker/`.

## The loop (one sentence)

```text
Research → Insight → Editorial Pack → Human feedback → AI polish → Publish → Cross-post → Engage → Measure → Improve Knowledge Product
```

Never ask: *What should we post today?*  
Always ask: *What is the most important thing we can teach — and which Knowledge Product does it improve?*
