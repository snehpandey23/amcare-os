# Weekly Marketing Rhythm

```text
Editorial desk, not campaign calendar — but the week still needs structure.
```

## Monday — Plan & research

| Focus | Actions |
|-------|---------|
| **Week kickoff** | Review OPERATING.md, OBJECTIVES.md, tracker statuses |
| **Research deep dive** | Advance 2–3 Research Backlog items; SERP + patient questions |
| **AI briefs** | Queue 1–2 packs for drafting/polish in Cursor |
| **Ads** | Budget check, weekend performance, week pacing plan |

**Output:** Week priorities written in Daily Log (Monday row)

---

## Tuesday — Create & review

| Focus | Actions |
|-------|---------|
| **Feedback loop** | Review AI drafts; write specific feedback |
| **Design** | Carousel/static production for Ready queue |
| **SEO** | One spoke: outline, link map, or draft start |
| **Editorial Test** | Pre-check all assets moving to Ready |

**Output:** ≥1 asset → Ready or Scheduled

---

## Wednesday — Publish primary

| Focus | Actions |
|-------|---------|
| **Instagram** | Publish 1 carousel or reel from Ready bank |
| **Cross-post** | LI Company, FB, X, Pinterest same day or next |
| **Engagement** | Heavy comment reply day on fresh post |
| **Ads** | Mid-week optimization; creative tests |

**Output:** Distribution Tracker fully logged for publish day

---

## Thursday — Distribute & engage

| Focus | Actions |
|-------|---------|
| **Secondary channels** | Catch-up cross-posts; IG Story |
| **Founder LinkedIn** | Only if pack includes founder draft AND explicitly scheduled |
| **Community** | Outbound comments on peer content (3–5) |
| **SEO** | Draft progress or internal linking pass |
| **Newsletter** | Siya Circle draft if monthly/biweekly cadence due |

**Output:** Engagement metrics logged; newsletter draft if on schedule

---

## Friday — Measure & improve

| Focus | Actions |
|-------|---------|
| **Metrics review** | Top/bottom posts: saves, shares, link taps |
| **FEEDBACK.md** | Update design-lab learnings from week's posts |
| **Tracker hygiene** | All Published rows have URLs; no orphan Ready items |
| **Research** | Log what worked → next insight ideas |
| **Ads** | Weekly CPL report; pause losers |
| **Sync** | Confirm WorkDrive matches git tracker |

**Output:** Friday Daily Log with week summary + next week #1 priority

---

## Monthly anchors (first week of month)

| Task | Owner |
|------|-------|
| Knowledge Product health check (hub → spoke → care) | Content lead |
| OBJECTIVES.md progress review | Team lead |
| Ads monthly report (spend, CPL, by campaign) | Ads |
| SEO: Search Console review + priority shift | SEO |
| Compliance: re-check paid creative library | Approver |
| Creative Registry: add seeds from top performers | Research |

---

## Capacity guide (realistic)

**Solo marketer (40 hrs/week):**
- 2 publishes/week max (quality > quantity)
- 1 SEO spoke every 2 weeks
- Ads: monitor daily, optimize 2×/week
- Research: 4–6 hrs/week

**Small team (2–3 people):**
- 3–4 publishes/week
- 1 SEO spoke/week
- Dedicated engagement 30 min/day
- Parallel: design while research runs next pack

**Current constraint:** Pause at 10 — prioritize **finishing feedback on existing bank** over new production until loop closes.
