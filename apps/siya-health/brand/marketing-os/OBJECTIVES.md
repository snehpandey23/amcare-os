## What we intentionally do NOT optimize for

- Viral hooks that sacrifice trust
- Follower count without save/share quality
- Posting daily with weak insights
- Meet & Greet as primary CTA (shifting to screening / eval / Talk to a Clinician)
- Product #2–6 before Product #1 measured
- TikTok (not in current strategy)
