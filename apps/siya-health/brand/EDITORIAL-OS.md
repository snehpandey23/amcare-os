# Siya Editorial OS

```text
Status: Locked operating system — 2026-07-22
Amended: 2026-08-12 — research = angle; video format lock; no verbatim / no AI avatar
Amended: 2026-08-12 — existing blog as research anchor; CTA by funnel stage
Replaces: “content generation system” mindset
Unit of work: Editorial Pack (not a post)
North star: One memorable realization per piece
```

Feedback from production is **rarely about writing**. It is about **thinking**:

- Is slide 1 a hook?
- What is the story? What does the reader expect next?
- Is this practical?
- Does the image match the message?
- Why would someone keep swiping?

Those are editorial principles. This OS exists to force that thinking **before** copy is written.

---

## Research deliverable (locked 2026-08-12)

Research produces an **articulated angle**, not a transcript dump.

| Weak “research” (not enough) | Required output |
|------------------------------|-----------------|
| Link + pasted comments | **Your read:** what this means for Siya |
| Near-verbatim VO from a viral Reel | **Angle** + why it works + Siya paraphrase direction |
| “Here’s 5 Reddit threads” | Recurring pain · natural phrasing · **one recommended angle** |

Raw links and quotes are **supporting material**. The deliverable is: *here’s the angle I’d pursue, and why.*

**Hard rule — no near-verbatim transcription:** Captions and video prompts must never closely mirror a source video’s narration line-for-line. If caption and source read “same to same,” that is a **process failure**, not an acceptable output.

**Style:** Prefer insight / emotional recognition over textbook or listicle spines (“5 types of ADHD”). Viral research already shows that divide.

### Primary content workflow — existing blog as anchor (locked 2026-08-12)

**Default:** Do **not** start from a fresh topic search. Pick a **published Siya blog** (spoke / Knowledge Product) as the anchor, then research Reddit / Instagram / Reels / forums **against that page’s topic**.

| Step | What you do |
|------|-------------|
| 1. Anchor | Name the live URL (e.g. `/blog/executive-dysfunction-adhd`) |
| 2. Research | Mine audience language, keywords, and recurring pain **for that topic** |
| 3. Split output | **(a)** New social / carousel / reel packs that **link back to that blog**, using the audience phrasing found in research · **(b)** A **flagged list** of real audience questions the blog does **not** yet address |
| 4. Gaps | Gap list is **not** an auto-edit ticket — it feeds a **separate deliberate decision** on whether/when to update that page |
| 5. Cap | Roughly **1–2 new social pieces per existing blog**, then move to the **next** blog anchor. Do not unlimited-generate from one topic |

Fresh-topic research is the exception (new Knowledge Product / new spoke), not the default factory path.

### Working cadence (still-forming phase)

- **Tight check-ins** every **10–30 minutes** on judgment work (angle selection, hook framing).
- Pure execution (tracker rows, already-approved prompt fill) can run longer without interruption.
- **Thinking stays with founder/lead** until the angle/hook is locked.
- **Execution is handed off only when fully specified** (locked prompt + clear target) — do not assign “figure out the angle” to someone still building that judgment.

### Video formats

Allowed reel formats and bans (including **no AI avatar**): see [`video-prompts/README.md`](./video-prompts/README.md).

## Stage outputs (required artifacts)

### 1. Research Brief
**Primary path:** name the **existing published blog URL** as anchor → research against it → articulated angle + why → output split **(a) social that links back** / **(b) flagged unanswered audience questions**. Cap **~1–2 packs per blog**, then switch anchors. See **Primary content workflow** above.

Already-done research: point to extract / spoke / Knowledge Product; do not re-research by default.

When new research *is* done (Reddit / Reels / papers): the brief must include an **articulated angle + why**, not only links and pasted comments. See **Research deliverable** above.

### 2. Editorial Insight (ONE sentence)
The article is **not** the insight.

| Topic | Weak | Insight |
|-------|------|---------|
| Iron | Iron causes fatigue | Iron deficiency can quietly affect attention, memory, and executive function |
| Long COVID | They’re just tired | Persistent cognitive symptoms can resemble ADHD |
| Sleep apnea | Sleep apnea is common | Sometimes poor focus begins with poor sleep, not poor motivation |

### 3. Human Recognition
Who feels **seen** — not who learns something.  
This becomes the opening.

Examples: “I can’t think clearly anymore.” · “My brain never came back.” · “I slept 8 hours. Why am I exhausted?”

### 4. Creative Angle Library
25–50 metaphors/angles. Not posts. Choose **one**.

### 5. Hook Selection
20 hooks. Rank. Pick one.  
❌ Iron deficiency causes fatigue.  
✅ Still tired… or just can’t think clearly?

**Public disclosure hooks (allowed):** When a real public figure has **openly** discussed their own diagnosis, that may be a hook — only from what is actually publicly known. Never fabricate quotes or invent claims. Details: `video-prompts/README.md`.

❌ “5 types of ADHD you need to know” (textbook/listicle)  
✅ One lived recognition or reframe that earns the swipe

### 6. Story Architecture
Every piece answers, in order:

1. Why should I care?  
2. Why is this happening?  
3. What does research say?  
4. What should I do?  
5. Soft next step by funnel stage (company only — see **CTA by funnel stage**)

Not: definition → symptoms → diagnosis → treatment (textbook).  
Not: numbered clinical type lists as the main spine.  
Not: defaulting every first-touch piece to “book a free screening.”

### 7. Visual Direction
Movie scene / lived metaphor. Not illustration cliché.

### 8. Minimal Graphic
**Never teach on the image.** Image = curiosity. Caption = understanding.  
One sentence / one realization on the frame when possible.

### 9. Caption
Explain, research, sources, nuance, CTA (company) — **matched to funnel stage**.

### 10. CTA by funnel stage (locked 2026-08-12)

Company channels only. Founder LinkedIn stays no-CTA unless explicitly requested.

| Funnel stage | Typical piece | Close with (softer → harder) |
|--------------|---------------|------------------------------|
| **Top / awareness** (first touch, recognition, “it’s okay if…”) | Carousel / reel that introduces a realization | **Read the in-depth blog** (anchor spoke) · **join the newsletter** / Siya Circle — *not* screening or Meet & Greet as the default |
| **Mid / consideration** | Comparison, “what evaluation includes,” medication education | Blog + optional soft care path (Meet & Greet *or* screening — one ask, not both stacked) |
| **Later / conversion** | Pricing, same-week eval, “ready when you are” | Screening · Meet & Greet · book — harder conversion ask is **earned** here |

**Rule:** Save the harder conversion ask for later-funnel content. Awareness posts exist to earn trust and send people to the **written spoke**, not to convert on first contact.

### 11. Website expansion + repurpose
Default is **social → existing blog**, not research → new blog every time. New blog / FAQ outline only when the flagged gap list is deliberately approved for a page update. Email · Pinterest · newsletter · short video · threads still link the same spoke.

## Ten Creative Principles (codified)

1. **Recognition before education.**  
2. **One idea per post.**  
3. **People remember stories, not statistics.**  
4. **The graphic creates curiosity. The caption creates understanding.**  
5. **Never waste Slide 1.**  
6. **Never waste the last slide.**  
7. **Every post answers “So what?”**  
8. **Don’t explain medicine. Explain people’s lives.**  
9. **Replace medical language with lived experience**  
   (e.g. executive dysfunction → “I know exactly what needs to be done… I just can’t start.”)  
10. **Every post creates ONE realization — not ten.**

Also retain: ≥1 evidence-based practical change on company/social (`EDITORIAL-TEST.md`).  
Visual production: [`VISUAL-OS.md`](./VISUAL-OS.md) v1.1 — one job · Rule of Three · 4:5 · Blur Test · never-ship gallery.

## Editorial Pack = unit of work

Template: [`../editorial-packs/_TEMPLATE/`](../editorial-packs/_TEMPLATE/)

Every approved Insight ID produces a standardized package (thinking first, assets second):

```text
01-research-brief.md
02-editorial-insight.md          ← one sentence
03-human-recognition.md
04-creative-angles.md            ← 25–50; chosen one marked
05-hooks-ranked.md               ← 20; winner marked
06-story-architecture.md
07-visual-direction.md           ← metaphors / movie scenes
08-hero-static-concept.md
09-carousel-concept.md
10-reel-concept.md
11-caption-company.md            (+ platform adaptations)
12-cta.md                        ← company only; funnel stage + soft/hard ask noted
13-blog-outline.md               ← only if gap-update deliberately approved; else link existing spoke
14-newsletter-version.md
15-sources.md
16-seo-cluster-and-links.md      ← primary: link back to anchor blog
17-editorial-score.md            ← must be ≥85
ready-to-post/                   ← images + captions (after approval)
```

Pack research brief must name **anchor blog URL**, **(a)/(b) split**, and **cap count** (which # of 1–2 for this spoke).

Do **not** jump to `ready-to-post/` until Stages 2–7 and score ≥85.
