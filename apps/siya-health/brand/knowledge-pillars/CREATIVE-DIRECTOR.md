## Mission

Build a physician-led **consumer medical knowledge platform**.

Not a blog. Not a social calendar. Not isolated posts.

Optimize for compounding authority, trust, SEO, education, and acquisition — in that order of integrity.

---

## What Siya is becoming

**Systems medicine explained simply.**

Core domains: Brain · Hormones · Sleep · Metabolism · Primary Care

Diseases are manifestations. Intersections are the moat:

ADHD × Hormones · Hormones × Sleep · Iron × Fatigue · Sleep Apnea × Testosterone · Food Noise × ADHD · GLP-1 × Muscle · Metabolism × Mood

Never silos.

## Knowledge architecture

```text
Knowledge Product     (canonical, evergreen, versioned)
        ↓
Knowledge Articles    (spokes — one important question each)
        ↓
Creative Registry     (approved insights)
        ↓
Marketing Assets      (distribute; always link back)
        ↓
Analytics             (feed the Product, not vanity)
```

**Never ask:** What should we post today?  
**Always ask:** What is the most important thing we can teach — and which Knowledge Product does it improve?

Loop: Improve Product → Compile insights → Choose best **Insight ID** → Editorial Pack → Publish → Measure → Improve Product

We are a **newsroom / editorial desk**, not a campaign calendar. Insights resurface; weeks don't.

## Every Knowledge Product should contain

Intro · How everything connects · Common symptoms / recognition · Clinical explanation · Evidence ladder (know / think / don't know) · Myths · FAQs · Clinical pearls · Patient journey · Related articles · Internal links · References · CTA

Useful, not encyclopedic.

---

## Evidence ladder (non-negotiable)

Distinguish: established · emerging · clinical observation · expert opinion · unknowns

Never overstate. Never DIY dosing. Never HRT-as-ADHD. Never cure language.

Formal physician review may lag publish; pages must carry educational disclaimer + pending-review status until stamped.

---

## Creative families (compiler output)

Recognition · Explanation · Myth · Research · Physician Perspective · Process · Proof · Action

Each registry insight: title, insight, evidence, confidence, emotion, stage, keywords, links, CTA, status  

**Publication states:** `Draft` → `Clinical Review` → `Approved` → `Designed` → `Published` → `Republished` → `Archived` → `Updated`

Organize by **Insight ID** (e.g. `WH-R-02`), never by “Week 1 / Piece A.”

## SEO

Own **intersections**, not generic disease definitions.

`/blog/` URLs OK for now; `/knowledge/` migration is Phase 2 — do not block deploy.

## Social / Editorial Desk

Distribute knowledge. Do not chase trends. Organize by **Insight ID**, not weeks.

One approved Insight → **Editorial Pack** (not “channel pack” / not “campaign”).

### Founder LinkedIn (Dr. Sneh) — protected

**Never** includes: Book Now, Meet & Greet, Screening, “read our blog,” or any CTA.

Job: make physicians and thoughtful professionals stop scrolling. Profile discovery is enough.

Voice: story → observation → system contradiction → open question. Live with the question.

### Company + other platforms

May educate and gently direct: Recognition → Knowledge Product → Spoke → Meet & Greet / Screening.

### Editorial Pack contains

Insight · Evidence · Claim ladder · Visual direction · Founder version (no CTA) · Company version · Platform adaptations · Tracking · Knowledge Product update note

Example: `women-hormones-adhd/EDITORIAL-PACK-WH-R-02.md`

---

## Editorial gate

Every asset must answer:

1. Who is this for?  
2. What problem?  
3. Which Knowledge Product?  
4. Which spoke?  
5. Journey stage + emotion?  
6. What **practical change** (company/social) — ≥1 evidence-based next step?  
7. What CTA (if any)? — founder LinkedIn answer is always **none**

Also pass [`EDITORIAL-TEST.md`](./EDITORIAL-TEST.md).

If it strengthens nothing — don't make it.  
If company/social content only resonates but teaches no usable next step — don't make it.
