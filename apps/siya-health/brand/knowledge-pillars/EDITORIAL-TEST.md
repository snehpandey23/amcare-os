# Siya Editorial Test

```text
Pin this. Apply before anything ships.
```

Before publishing anything, ask:

1. **Does this improve a Knowledge Product?** (Prefer: does social link back to an **existing published blog** anchor?)
2. **Does this help someone recognize themselves?**
3. **Is this useful — not just resonant?**
4. **Does it include at least one practical change** someone can make (habit, question to ask a clinician, tracking step, or decision rule) grounded in **evidence-based medicine** / accepted clinical guidelines — without DIY dosing or overclaim?
5. **Would we still be proud of this in three years?**
6. **Does this teach something competitors don't?**
7. **Does this build trust instead of attention?**
8. **Is the CTA matched to funnel stage?** Top-of-funnel / first-touch → soft (read the blog, newsletter). Harder asks (screening / Meet & Greet) only on later-funnel pieces — see `EDITORIAL-OS.md` → CTA by funnel stage.

If any answer is no — don't publish.

**Usefulness rule (locked 2026-07-21):**  
Recognition alone is not enough for company/social carousels and educational posts. Every piece must leave the reader with **≥1 concrete next step** they can act on. Vague inspiration (“trust the process,” “listen to your body”) without a specific action fails this test.

Safe practical patterns:
- Track X for N days, then bring it to a clinician  
- Ask for evaluation of Y (labs/screens named carefully)  
- Stop doing Z when A is happening; do W instead  
- **Awareness / first touch:** read the anchor blog · join newsletter (soft)  
- **Later funnel only:** Read spoke → Meet & Greet or screening (company channels only; one hard ask)

Never: DIY stimulant/HRT protocols, “hormones cause ADHD,” guaranteed diagnosis language.  
Never: default every awareness post to “book a free screening.”

---

**Editorial slogan:** Recognition > Expertise  
**Company/social addendum:** Recognition without a useful next step is incomplete.

**Emotional arc (default):**

```text
Recognition → Curiosity → Explanation → Evidence → Practical change → Relationship
```

Founder LinkedIn may stop at Curiosity / open question (no CTA).  
Company / social must include the practical change; Relationship (care / screening) only when funnel stage allows — awareness stops at soft CTA (blog / newsletter).
