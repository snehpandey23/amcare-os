# Siya Editorial Test

```text
Pin this. Apply before anything ships.
```

Before publishing anything, ask:

1. **Does this improve a Knowledge Product?**
2. **Does this help someone recognize themselves?**
3. **Would we still be proud of this in three years?**
4. **Does this teach something competitors don't?**
5. **Does this build trust instead of attention?**

If any answer is no — don't publish.

---

**Editorial slogan:** Recognition > Expertise

**Emotional arc (default):**

```text
Recognition → Curiosity → Explanation → Evidence → Treatment → Relationship
```

Founder LinkedIn may stop at Curiosity / open question.  
Company / social may continue gently toward Relationship (care).
