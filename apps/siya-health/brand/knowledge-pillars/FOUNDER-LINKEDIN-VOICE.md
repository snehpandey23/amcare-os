# Founder LinkedIn voice — craft rules

```text
Locked: 2026-07-21 (from ICU → primary care edit)
Applies: Dr. Sneh founder LinkedIn (and similarly voice-led founder posts)
```

## Sound like

Someone finally putting words to something they’ve carried for years — not a thesis written after an hour with a notebook.

Personal memory → what remained → the shift → quiet realization.

## Do

- Short lines. White space.
- Specific scenes (rooms, conversations, procedures) before conclusions.
- End on a **realization**, not a question (analytics: strongest posts don’t ask for engagement).
- Let systems insight emerge from the story; don’t announce it.

## Don’t

- “Actually saving lives” / ranking which specialty saves lives.
- Crafted slogan lines that sound workshopped (“Primary care is where fewer people have to get there”).
- Soft CTAs, blog links, Meet & Greet (founder channel remains no-CTA).
- Ending with “Curious how others…” unless intentionally testing engagement (default: no).

## Company vs founder

Company packs = audience-specific education + practical change.  
Founder = lived clinical reflection. Produce founder posts only when explicitly requested — not auto-generated with every carousel.
