## The shift in one line

**Old:** Rank for ADHD → expand services.  
**New:** Own **Women's Hormonal Health** as a domain → ADHD, iron, sleep, weight, mood are spokes.

Patients don't organize by ICD codes. They organize by:

- Why am I suddenly exhausted?
- Why can't I concentrate?
- Why am I gaining weight?
- Why is my sleep terrible?
- Why did my coping stop working?

Those are hormonal-health questions. ADHD is one possible chapter.
