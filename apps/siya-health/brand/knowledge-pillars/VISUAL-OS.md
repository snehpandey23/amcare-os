## Dual posting formats (locked)

Pick **one** format per post/carousel from the job. Do not mix inside the same carousel.

### Format A — Educational Text

| | |
|--|--|
| **Job** | Explain one clinical idea (drug, pathway, lab, policy, myth) |
| **Visual** | Cream/beige field · dominant type · simple diagrams · **no people photos** |
| **Tone** | Calm authority · curiosity without drama |
| **When** | Mechanisms, trial explainers, comparisons, “what is X?” |
| **Example** | OBESITY-2026-07-24-retatrutide carousel |
| **Caption** | Carries sources, nuance, practical change, soft CTA |

Whitespace and typography *are* the visual. Apple clarity over Canva collage.

### Format B — Emotional Photo

| | |
|--|--|
| **Job** | One lived realization — recognition before education |
| **Visual** | One human scene or story prop · hook · Siya® · quiet footer |
| **Tone** | Warm, hopeful, candid — not head-in-hands shame stock |
| **When** | Scaffolding lost, fog, paperwork friction, “I thought I was lazy…” |
| **Caption** | Teaches; image only creates the feeling |

### Decision rule

```text
Does the reader need to FEEL seen first?     → Format B (photo)
Does the reader need to UNDERSTAND a fact? → Format A (text)
Both?                                      → Two posts, or carousel that stays in ONE format
```

### Creative ideas by format

**Edu Text:** molecule explainers · trial toplines · Category 2 / grey market · GLP-1 vs dual vs triple · lab literacy · “approved vs in trials” · Minoxidil shed cycle (diagram subset)  
**Emotional Photo:** remote scaffolding · sticky-note fog · cold coffee + tabs · insurance portal unread · zoning out mid-call · “ADHD in women often looks quiet” (AD-W-02)

**Target feel:** warm morning sunlight, soft shadows, creamy whites, subtle beige, natural skin tones.

**Not:** muted gloom · cold grey · **hospital purple stacks** · clinical clipart · staged “healthcare stock” · shame galleries of distress poses.

---

## Every image has ONE job

Not five. Not nine.

| Weak job | Strong job |
|----------|------------|
| Sell a free call *and* tell an empathy story | Make someone think: “Care shouldn’t stop because of paperwork.” |
| Teach nine ADHD symptoms on one poster | Make someone think: “I zone out mid-conversation — that’s ADHD-shaped.” |

If you cannot state the job in **one sentence**, the design is not ready.

**Series rule:** A consistent character or theme may run across **many posts**. Never compress a series into one encyclopedia grid.

---

## Recognition > Information (on the frame)

| Weak (teaches) | Strong (recognizes) |
|----------------|---------------------|
| “Iron deficiency affects executive function” | Sticky note: “What was I doing?” |
| “WHAT DOES ADHD LOOK LIKE (SYMPTOMS)” + 9 panels | “I was looking at you… and I missed the whole sentence.” |
| Woman / couple holding head over paperwork | Unread insurance portal · cold coffee · laptop cursor blinking · grocery list half-written |
| Insurance delays. Approvals. Endless waiting. *(three body grafs)* | One hook that names the friction — teaching moves to caption |

### Harvested recognition patterns (keep)

From audited creatives — **keep the insight, kill the layout:**

1. **System-friction hooks** — paperwork, approvals, waiting rooms that pause care while ADHD does not. Recognition of the *system*, not only the symptom.  
2. **Lived micro-captions** — “all over the place,” “zoning out,” “misplacing keys” as *seed lines* for single posts — never as a 3×3 dump.  
3. **Story props over symptom icons** — table of papers, calendar with one circled date, floating keys — one prop per frame.  
4. **Cream / warm paper fields** — closer to brand than purple CTA slabs (use warm grade, not gloomy desaturation).

---

## Visual hierarchy (locked)

```text
1  HOOK
↓
2  VISUAL
↓
3  BRAND
↓
4  ACTION
```

If someone reads body copy before the hook → hierarchy failed.  
If a purple CTA block or second headline competes with the hook → hierarchy failed.

**Never glue a second poster under the first** (problem half + CTA half). That is two compositions. Split into carousel beats or put the sell in the caption.

---

## Rule of Three

No more than **three** visual elements competing for attention.

Default stack:

1. Headline  
2. Photo / scene  
3. Brand  

Done.

**Explicit fails:** 3×3 symptom grids · stacked ad units (hook + body stack + photo + CTA slab + button + second photo + second logo) · icon charts on the same frame as the hook.

---

## Format (Meta priority)

| Priority | Ratio | Size | Use |
|----------|-------|------|-----|
| **Default** | **4:5** | **1080 × 1350** | Almost everything |
| Secondary | 1:1 | 1080 × 1080 | Cross-posting when needed |

**Avoid 4:3** as a default. Tall “story ad stacks” are not a substitute for 4:5 feed frames.

---

## Color

- Cream · warm beige · soft peach · muted sage  
- Subtle Siya gradient (**warm**, never cold)

**Type accents (from Marketing samples — sparingly):**
- Navy / deep charcoal for authority headlines (Format A/B)
- Plum / magenta on **1–3 keywords max** — emotional punch, not a field color
- Soft green remains the preferred **soft CTA** accent when a path color is needed

**Never:** cold grey · hospital blue · **primary purple medical posters** · muddy desaturated gloom as the field · lavender corner-wave chrome as the brand look

Purple may appear inside the **Siya® mark** and as a quiet keyword tint — never as a full-bleed CTA slab or purple-wave product deck.

---

## Typography

- **One dominant headline**  
- **One supporting sentence** max when needed  
- Prefer **removing text** over enlarging body  
- If body needs +20–30% size → too much body text  
- No all-caps diagnostic poster titles (“WHAT DOES ADHD LOOK LIKE…”) as the social hook

### Word budgets

| Frame | Max |
|-------|-----|
| Slide 1 / hero static | **15–20 words** |
| Educational slides | **30–40 words** |
| Last slide | **Single CTA** (+ quiet footer) |

---

## Whitespace

Use whitespace **intentionally**. Apple, not Canva. Do not fill empty space with extra body lines or a second photo.

---

## Logo — Siya®

- **One brand. One wordmark. No second logo** in a CTA band.  
- **Top left.** Registered mark **always** (`Siya®` / Siya Health® lockup).  
- **Sizing (1080×1350):** wordmark ≈ **80–90px** tall; readable, not washed out.  
- Submerged (no white plate). Never louder than the hook.  
- **Optional ambient mark:** one low-opacity mark (~10–14%) in a *quiet* field — never competing with headline (AD-W-02 pattern). Do **not** add a large background watermark as the brand strategy.  
- **Brand absence fails the Blur Test** — unbranded symptom charts are not Siya posts.

---

## Website / CTA on frame

Footer (quiet, every slide), **bottom center**:

```text
siya.health  ·  All content for educational purposes only
```

- Readable; never louder than the headline  
- **No on-frame buttons** competing with the hook  
- **Banned on-frame / company social CTAs:** “free discovery call,” Meet & Greet, Find the Right Starting Point, Join Waitlist  
- **Allowed soft path (company):** Talk to a Clinician → CarePatron booking (usually in **caption** / last carousel slide — not a loud mid-frame button)  
- **Phone `(215) 445-1244`:** caption and/or **last slide only** — never mid-carousel chrome; never louder than the educational footer on earlier slides  
- **Personality accents (Format B only):** max **one** script/handwritten emotional word; light botanical OK; no emoji stacks on clinical Format A

---

## Carousel swipe indicator

Bottom right · subtle `→` or `↓` · every carousel slide (optional omit on last).

**Deprecated:** bottom-left `n/5` coral pills as primary chrome (cloud Minoxidil Template B). Prefer swipe cue + quiet footer.

| Prefer | Avoid |
|--------|--------|
| Morning light, candid micro-moments | Head-in-hands / forehead-clutch stock |
| One clear prop that tells the story | Paper chaos + stress couple + second stressed woman |
| Quiet confidence after recognition | Nine panels of shame / overwhelm / guilt |
| Warm skin tones | Cool purple-grey grade |

## Pre-publish question (required)

> What emotion should this image create **before** someone reads a single word?

If that answer isn’t obvious → the design isn’t finished.

---

## The 3-Second Blur Test (quality gate)

Squint, or shrink to thumbnail size.

1. **The headline?**  
2. **The emotion?** (one emotion — not “busy chart”)  
3. **That it’s a Siya® post?**

If any answer is no → do not publish.
