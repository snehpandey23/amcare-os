# Staff portal — pilot guide for the team (share in Slack)

**URL:** https://siya-staff-assist.vercel.app/login  
**Pin this link** on desktop and phone (Add to Home Screen).

## Daily flow (everyone)

1. **Sign in** with work email + password from your admin invite.
2. Top right: confirm **Working** (green). Use **Break** / **Focus** / **Back to working** as needed. **Focus** = do not disturb.
3. **My day** (home) = **today’s assigned tasks** and team who’s online.
4. **Team** = full roster + who has what on the board today.
5. **End shift** when you finish (top right) — different from **Sign out** (ends your session only).

If you don’t see Working / Break / Focus: tap **Start shift** (top right) or sign out and sign in again once.

## Department SOP leads

- Purple card on **My day**: your departments → **Open SOP workspace** (draft/update procedures).
- **Billing / onboarding / reimbursement** style work = SOP workspace + tasks on **My day** when assigned.

## Admins

- **Admin** nav → invite team, task board, SOP review.
- Assign department leads in admin; tasks sync to **My day** and **Team** within a few minutes.

## What we removed in the app

- Yellow **Internal Preview** banner (still pilot — just not shown on every screen).

## Support

Wrong password → admin reset on **Admin → Team → Edit**.  
Tasks missing → admin checks **Task board** and department lead assignment.
