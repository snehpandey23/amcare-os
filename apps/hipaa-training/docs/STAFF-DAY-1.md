# Siya Staff Portal — Staff Day-1 (one-pager)

**Audience:** Every staff account (not Admin)  
**URL:** https://siya-staff-assist.vercel.app  
**Status:** Accurate to live product as of 2026-08-24 · replaces older Claude/pilot drafts that skipped Start shift and mis-scoped Ask / feedback  
**Not this doc:** Second FAQ site, or MA hire orientation (Klarity/Spruce Day-1). Chrome how-to: **Ask on My day** or the **Quick My day tour** (spotlight or Assist).

---

## First hour

1. **Sign in** with the invite email + temporary password (admin can reset under Admin → Team → Edit).
2. Complete **onboarding** if prompted (`/onboarding`): preferred name, what to call Assist, training reminders, department / goals / shift rhythm. Change later via **Personalize** under the My day subtitle.
3. Tap **Start shift** (header). Until you do, you will not see Working / Break / Focus.
4. Confirm status shows **Working**. You are on **My day** (`/`) — this is home.
5. Optional **Quick My day tour** (first visit): pick **Show me the buttons** or **Ask Assist to walk me through**. Restart later under **Account → Show My day tour again**.

**Admins** do not get the staff shift bar; this Day-1 pack is for staff. Admins use Admin / Founder Talk surfaces (see Admin pack outline).

---

## Shift (header)

| Control | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Working** | On duty; Team can see you as working |
| **Break** | Away briefly; full-screen break state |
| **Focus** | Quieter My day — priorities + Ask; learning nudges pause. **Back to working** leaves Focus |
| **End shift** | Ends presence and opens **Shift handoff** (optional note; skip allowed). Then you return to Start shift |
| **Sign out** | Ends login only — **not** the same as End shift |

Presence is **self-declared**. It is not a punch clock and not a productivity score.

---

## My day (home)

Two jobs on one screen:

1. **Ask (Assist chat)** — staff help desk. Lives **here**, not as a separate top nav item. Sidebar is **My day · Learn · Memory · Team** (plus Account / Sign out in the header).
2. **Today** — your assigned tasks / checklists (and lead cards if you are a department lead).

### Ask — do this

- Ask policies, SOPs, who to escalate to, “where is Focus / Clear chat / Mic?”, workplace tool links (`open Spruce`, Carepatron, etc.).
- **👍 / 👎** under a reply = “was this reply helpful?” (**yes/no log only**). Does **not** email anyone, change policy, or teach the bot permanent truth.
- **Notify owner** appears when there is **no approved staff guide** for that ask. It queues a knowledge gap for the department lead (or founder). Not for “button broken” or password reset.
- **Copy escalation summary** (when shown) = paste a de-identified handoff yourself (Slack/email). Not a substitute for Notify owner when the gap is missing policy.

### Ask — never do this

- Patient names, DOB, MRN, chart details, or other PHI. Use Spruce / EHR / approved clinical tools for patient work.

### Tasks on Today

- Open assigned items, complete checklist steps, **Mark done**.
- **⚑ Flag** a bad/outdated checklist step when the content is wrong (not for general Ask complaints).
- Linked SOP / builder shortcuts open when the task provides them.

**Approve** (creates an ad-hoc task from an Ask recommendation) is an **admin ops** path, not a Day-1 staff action. Staff do not need it to run a shift.

## Team (`/team`)

| Surface | What it is |
|---------|------------|
| **Team today** | Who is Working / Break / Focus / Off shift |
| **Shift handoffs today** | Notes left at End shift — continuity for the next person |
| **Weekly lead check-ins** (feed) | Visible department pulse when leads have filed |

Read the pulse and handoffs. Do **not** treat Team as a scoreboard.

**Weekly lead check-in** (form: what changed / blockers / founder should know) is for **Marketing, Clinical Operations, and Compliance leads** (and admins) — **not** the staff-wide bug tracker. Ordinary staff report UI/access issues to a lead or admin, not via that form.

---

## Learn

| Path | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Learn → Training** (`/training` and modules) | Required **HIPAA** path: modules → quizzes → final → certificate |
| **Learn → Practice** | Optional drills (culture, typing, English, map, timezones, billing scenarios). Skill practice — **not** the source of approved company policy |

Use **Ask** for live policy/SOP questions; use **Practice** for drills.

---

## Memory (when enabled for your account)

Browse published knowledge / SOPs as your role allows. Drafts are **not** live Ask policy until approved. Writing SOPs = **Department SOPs** / **SOP builder** (AI interview for checklist SOPs; paste-and-review for long policy) — still needs lead/admin review.

---

## Feedback map (use the right channel)

| Channel | Use for | Not for |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Thumbs 👎** | Weak Assist answer | Bugs, PHI incidents, patient QC |
| **Notify owner** | Missing / wrong **approved guide** | Broken button, password |
| **Copy escalation** | Human Slack/email with context | Replacing Notify owner |
| **SOP step ⚑** | Checklist content wrong | General Ask complaints |
| **Weekly lead check-in** | Dept pulse (leads only) | Staff-wide bug tracker |
| **Handoff** | Shift continuity | Long product feedback |
| **Lead / admin** | Access, broken UI, urgent ops | Dumping PHI |

---

## PHI — everywhere

No patient identifiers in **Ask**, **handoffs**, **tasks**, or team chat tools that are not approved for PHI. Patient work stays in **Spruce / EHR / Carepatron** (and other tools on Workplace links).

---

## If something is missing on screen

1. Ask on My day: “where is Focus?”, “what does Notify owner do?”, “open Spruce”.
2. Or replay **Account → Show My day tour again** (spotlight or Ask).
3. Still stuck → your supervisor or admin (access / invites).

Do **not** invent a second FAQ outside Ask for chrome how-to — that content is maintained in Assist meta answers.
