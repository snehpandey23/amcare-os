## Scope of Work - Responsibilities & Deliverables

### A. Patient Coordination & Scheduling

**Responsibilities:**
- Schedule and coordinate patient appointments using designated telehealth platform
- Manage appointment confirmations via email and SMS
- Maintain accurate patient records and scheduling logs
- Handle patient intake questionnaires and pre-visit documentation
- Reschedule cancelled or missed appointments

**Deliverable:** 100% appointment coordination accuracy; zero missed scheduled calls

**System Tasks:**
- Appointment scheduling
- Confirmation management
- Intake form processing
- Rescheduling requests

---

### B. Electronic Health Record (EHR) Management

**Responsibilities:**
- Input and update patient demographics, medical history, and insurance information
- Maintain HIPAA-compliant patient records in designated EHR system
- Ensure data accuracy and timeliness of all entries
- Generate required reports on patient visit frequency and health metrics
- Assist with chart audits to ensure compliance with coding and documentation standards

**Deliverable:** 100% data entry accuracy; <2% audit correction rate

**System Tasks:**
- EHR data entry
- Patient record updates
- Chart audits
- Report generation

---

### C. Pre-Visit Preparation

**Responsibilities:**
- Review patient charts prior to scheduled visits
- Flag any missing information or outstanding items
- Prepare patient files for provider review
- Compile relevant medical history and previous encounter notes
- Ensure all required documents are uploaded to EHR before visit start time

**Deliverable:** 100% of files ready 15 minutes before scheduled appointments

**System Tasks:**
- Pre-charting review
- Document verification
- Chart preparation
- Missing information flags

---

### D. Clinical Documentation Support

**Responsibilities:**
- Transcribe provider notes into patient records post-visit
- Verify accuracy of clinical codes and documentation
- Flag missing or incomplete documentation for provider follow-up
- Maintain organized visit summary records

**Deliverable:** All documentation completed within 4 hours of appointment conclusion

**System Tasks:**
- Note transcription
- Documentation verification
- Note locking
- Visit summary management

---

### E. Patient Communications

**Responsibilities:**
- Respond to routine patient inquiries via secure patient portal
- Provide appointment reminders and pre-visit instructions
- Handle simple informational requests (office hours, locations, general policies)
- Escalate clinical questions to appropriate provider immediately

**Deliverable:** 100% of non-clinical inquiries answered within 24 hours

**System Tasks:**
- Chat review
- Patient inquiry responses
- Appointment reminders
- Message escalation

---

### F. Compliance & Quality Assurance

**Responsibilities:**
- Maintain strict HIPAA compliance at all times
- Complete required HIPAA and data security training modules (quarterly)
- Participate in monthly quality assurance audits
- Report any security incidents or compliance violations immediately
- Maintain audit trail documentation for all patient interactions

**Deliverable:** Zero compliance violations; passing score on quarterly training assessments

**System Tasks:**
- Compliance monitoring
- Audit participation
- Incident reporting
- Training completion tracking

---

### G. Administrative Support

**Responsibilities:**
- Prepare daily patient schedules and provider briefing documents
- Generate daily/weekly appointment utilization reports
- Track no-shows, cancellations, and same-day changes
- Maintain supplies and alert to equipment issues
- Support with billing inquiries and insurance pre-authorization coordination

**Deliverable:** 
- Daily briefing documents by 8:00 AM EST
- Weekly utilization reports by Friday 5:00 PM EST

**System Tasks:**
- Schedule preparation
- Report generation
- Appointment tracking
- Billing support

---

### H. System Management & Troubleshooting

**Responsibilities:**
- Monitor telehealth platform connectivity and perform basic troubleshooting
- Test video/audio conferencing equipment before each patient session
- Restart systems as needed and escalate technical issues to IT
- Maintain backup internet connection availability

**Deliverable:** 99% system uptime adherence; zero patient call drops due to MA-side technical failure

**System Tasks:**
- System monitoring
- Equipment testing
- Technical issue escalation
- Connectivity management
