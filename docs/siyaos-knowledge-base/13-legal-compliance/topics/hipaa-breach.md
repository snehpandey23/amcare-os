## Overview

What staff do when PHI may have been disclosed improperly.

## Why

Breach notification timelines and analysis are regulated — not frontline staff decisions.

## SOP

1. Stop further disclosure.  
2. Report **same day** to Privacy Officer.  
3. Document what happened and what PHI may have been exposed.  
4. Do not notify patients or media unless directed by compliance.

## FAQ

**Someone on a video visit had family in frame — breach?**  
Treat as privacy risk; pause visit; report if PHI may have been heard/seen.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Email sent to wrong patient | Privacy Officer immediately |
| Lost device | IT + Privacy Officer |
