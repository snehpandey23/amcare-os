## Mission

Increase the number of people who **trust Siya Health enough to begin an appropriate clinical relationship** — and keep that trust compounding.

**North Star (not traffic / followers / impressions):**  
Trusted clinical relationship starts per period (qualified → consultation → appropriate care).

---

## Philosophy (v1.0)

- Trust before conversion.
- Systems before campaigns.
- Research feeds every durable content asset.
- Authority is the engine; search is an outcome.
- Prefer durable internal capability over recurring agency dependence when ROI is unclear.
- One excellent process beats ten disconnected campaigns.
- Never exaggerate, fear-market, or make misleading medical claims.
- Brand feel: evidence-based, warm, human, modern, physician-led, transparent, competent, calm.

### Objective order (never invert)

1. Patient trust  
2. Sustainable patient acquisition  
3. Patient lifetime value  
4. Brand equity  
5. Referral generation  
6. Organic growth  
7. Operational scalability  
8. Marketing efficiency  
9. Revenue growth

## Pillars

### Acquisition & authority flywheel

```text
Research → Content → Discovery → Recognition → Trust → Consultation
→ Clinical Experience → Reviews → Authority → Search Demand → Research
```

SEO is downstream of authority. Do not center the company on “SEO as the strategy.”

### Reputation (first-class)

- Google Reviews, care quality signals, NPS  
- Testimonials (compliance-safe)  
- Physician profiles & referring clinicians  
- Employer / community trust  

Ads and SEO **amplify** reputation; they do not replace it.

### Lifecycle (build as capacity allows)

```text
Awareness → Consideration → Consultation → Evaluation → Treatment
→ Retention → Annual care → Referral → Advocacy
```

Acquisition-heavy is correct for now; lifecycle becomes a dashboard later.

## CMO agent prompt (v1.0)

Use this when briefing an AI or human acting CMO — not a channel tactician:

> You are the acting Chief Marketing Officer for Siya Health. Your responsibility is to increase long-term enterprise value through sustainable patient acquisition, brand authority, physician reputation, employer credibility, and scalable marketing systems. Evaluate every recommendation through five lenses: patient trust, clinical integrity, commercial impact, operational scalability, and measurable outcomes. Challenge assumptions before proposing tactics. Prefer systems over campaigns, durable assets over short-term wins, and documented processes over founder memory.
>
> You operate Marketing as a **department of Siya OS**, not a standalone Marketing company. Align with Clinical, Compliance, Patient Operations, and Technology — do not invent a parallel operating philosophy.
