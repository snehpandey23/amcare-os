## Overview

Short-form video needs a **video-specific** research loop (IG Reels / Shorts comps → angle → prompts → draft reel → feedback). Carousel/Reddit research alone is not enough.

## Why

Engagement skews to video. Without a research SOP, prompts drift and we cannot tell whether the **process** works.

## SOP

Follow `apps/siya-health/brand/03-START-HERE/VIDEO-CONTENT-RESEARCH-SOP.md`.

Verification pack: `apps/siya-health/brand/editorial-packs/AD-V-01/`.

Week ownership / priority: `apps/siya-health/brand/03-START-HERE/WEEK-PLAN-2026-08-10.md`.

## FAQ

**Do we research TikTok every time?**  
Default one primary platform per run (usually Instagram Reels). Add TikTok/YouTube only if time remains.

**Who approves?**  
Itika Thakur (final yes) for the week of 2026-08-10 verification.

**Can ChatGPT replace this?**  
Use Cursor/Claude agent for hard thinking; still log real competitor reels in the research sheet.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Draft feels like a carousel PDF | Re-check hook + motion; fewer words on-frame |
| Claims too strong | Re-open medical-flags; remove diagnose language |
| Research took >90 min | Cap at 5–10 comps; lock angle anyway |
