## Overview

How marketing content is created, reviewed, and approved so ads and posts stay medically compliant (SOP **v2.0**).

## Why

Healthcare claims are regulated (FDA/FTC/DEA/state boards). Unreviewed copy creates legal and platform risk. FTC healthcare enforcement attention increased in 2026.

## SOP (agent + staff summary)

Canonical agent lock (full checklist): `apps/siya-health/brand/MEDICAL-COMPLIANCE-MARKETING.md`

1. **Content Creator** runs §7.2 checklist + claim tags (LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH) before submit.
2. **Marketing** confirms brand fit + checklist ran; routes **HIGH** to Medical Director.
3. **Medical Director** required for clinical accuracy, ethics, HIGH-risk claims.
4. **Legal Counsel** required when any §7.3 trigger applies (HIGH, comparative meds, testimonials, before/after, unsure).
5. **Chief Compliance Officer (Sneha)** owns posture; receives monitoring reports.
6. Track approvals; pack must include `compliance/CHECKLIST.md` before ship.

### Medical claims standards

- Substantiated (peer-reviewed / labeled evidence) — not forum anecdotes as fact
- Balanced — no comparative medication claims without Medical Director + Legal
- Current labeling where relevant
- Physician-reviewed before publication for clinical claims

### Before / after

- No before/after photos for prescription medications
- Cosmetic results: individual results may vary + timeline/protocol disclosure

### Testimonials (v2.0 — Legal-corrected)

- **Compensation / any material connection MUST be disclosed** (FTC). No exemption for patient testimonials.
- No atypical results promoted as typical
- Verify treatment completion before use
- Medical-supervision disclaimer where required

*(v1 incorrectly implied disclosure was not required — do not use v1 wording.)*

### Claim-risk

| Tag | Action |
|-----|--------|
| LOW | OK after checklist Pass |
| MEDIUM | Prefer Medical Director before publish |
| HIGH | **Stop** — Medical Director before use; Legal if §7.3 |

### Monitoring

- Monthly: LegitScript / compliance documentation
- Quarterly: broader audit
- Annually: policy review + retraining → CCO

## FAQ

**Can we run a weight-loss before/after ad with GLP-1?**  
No before/after photos for prescription meds. Escalate claim language to Medical Director (+ Legal if outcome content).

**Who approves clinical claims in a carousel?**  
Creator checklist → Marketing → Medical Director for clinical/HIGH. §7.3 → Legal.

**Is this the same as HIPAA?**  
Related but separate — this SOP is advertising/claims compliance. PHI incidents use Legal/Compliance breach topics.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Creator wants unverified “cure / guaranteed” language | Block; rewrite; Medical Director |
| HIGH claim in research “parked” with no owner | Route Medical Director — parked ≠ cleared |
| Platform rejected ad for health claims | Pull live creative; Medical Director + Marketing |
| Unclear if content is high-risk | Escalate Legal Counsel (§7.3) |
