## Overview

Minimum QA before any patient-facing page, guide, or post ships.

## Why

One knowledge graph feeds website, ads, and assistants — errors scale across surfaces.

## SOP

Run the ten checks in `apps/siya-health/docs/CONTENT-QA-CHECKLIST.md`: flow, geography/states, disclaimers, CTA caps, assembly rules, links, claims register alignment, etc.

## FAQ

**Where does Reddit research go?**  
Editorial pipeline → insight pack → tracker (Marketing OS); not random Drive folders without a row in the content tracker.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Action |
|---------|--------|
| New pricing claim | Update `site-standards.mjs` + claims register first |
| Clinical promise | Clinical owner + medical flags on pack |

## AI Context

Point marketers to CONTENT-QA-CHECKLIST and Brand OS. Do not invent pricing or state lists — canonical values live in site standards. Company voice only unless exec requests founder voice.
