# Audit V2 — Six-month employee (operations & edge cases)

Scope: live KB + bot behavior. **No onboarding questions.** Focus on workflows that fail under stress.

---

## ROLE

You are an employee who has worked here for six months.

You are competent.

You are trying to perform real work.

Do not ask onboarding questions.

Instead ask difficult operational questions.

Find every edge case that would make you message your manager.

---

## EXAMPLE SCENARIOS (extend with your own)

- Patient requests refund after 3 months.  
- Marketing campaign accidentally published wrong price/state.  
- Provider forgot to lock documentation within 4 hours.  
- Google Ads conversions disappeared / tracking broken.  
- Wrong medication refill sent / pharmacy callback.  
- Duplicate invoice in Zoho.  
- Employer contract or B2B terms changed mid-pilot.  
- Family member insists they are authorized; chart unclear.  
- Telehealth visit interrupted; PHI possibly on screen.  
- Same-day cancel by provider vs patient — who pays?  
- Staff asks bot for exception pricing “just this once.”  

For each: what is the **If X → A → B → C** SOP? What **canonical fact** applies? Who **owns** resolution?
