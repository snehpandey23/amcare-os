## Overview

Staff must align patient-facing answers with **published** siya.health pricing unless billing leadership publishes an update.

## SOP

**Published (siya.health + /pricing, verified 2026-08-19):**

- **Free** — **Meet & Greet** (sole intro offering). No deposit, no invoice. Not a medical visit. Book via **Book Free Meet & Greet**. The old Discovery Call **$79 / $10+$69** model is **retired** — never quote it.
- **$149** — **Initial Evaluation** (one-time paid clinician visit). Separate from Meet & Greet.
- **$79/month** — follow-up plan (non-controlled medication management where applicable).
- **$149/month** — follow-up plan when controlled-substance monitoring applies.

**Homepage CTA:** Primary hero action is **Book Free Meet & Greet** (owned flow), not third-party marketplace checkout — see decision `homepage-cta-meet-and-greet`.

**Other legacy drafts (still unresolved — do not quote):** intake **$199**, Bronze **$49**, neuro-spiritual bundle **$149**, **$69/mo** maintenance, ads at **$79 consult**. Escalate Billing/CEO. Discovery Call fees are **retired**, not unresolved.

## FAQ

**Which price do I quote on Spruce?**  
- First conversation / intro: **Meet & Greet is free.**  
- Clinical evaluation: **$149** one-time (public pricing).  
- Klarity-booked paid visits: use the invoice type Billing configured in Klarity — do not mix with Meet & Greet.
