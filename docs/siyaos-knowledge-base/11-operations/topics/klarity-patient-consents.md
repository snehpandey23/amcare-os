## Overview

Patients booking on Klarity acknowledge multiple **platform agreements**. MAs need to know **what exists** and **where to send patients**, not to reinterpret legal text.

This topic is **training / retrieval aid**. For disputes, use Klarity published pages + Privacy Officer / Compliance.

## Why

Incomplete or misunderstood consents delay visits and create PHI / billing risk. MAs should verify forms are **signed in portal**, not paraphrase law.

## SOP — Telehealth consent (staff bullets)

From Klarity Consent to Telehealth Services:

1. Telehealth = remote care via phone/computer/tablet (records, audio/video, device data).  
2. Benefits: access, less travel, less clinic exposure.  
3. Risks: limited exams, may need in-person, tech failures, privacy interception risk.  
4. Klarity **will not record** visits; patient should be in a private space.  
5. Patient may **withdraw** consent anytime.  
6. Visit cost not more than in-person for telehealth itself; extra in-person visit may be separate.  
7. Insured patients own copays/coinsurance/deductibles/non-covered services.  
8. Klarity **does not** submit claims or verify coverage.  
9. Questions: patientsupport@helloklarity.com.

## SOP — Payment agreement (staff bullets)

1. Self-pay: responsible for full cost; superbills for OON reimbursement **not guaranteed**.  
2. Insured: verify benefits; denied/partial → patient owes remainder; prior auth is patient’s duty when required.  
3. Medicare/Medicaid: extra consents/ABN possible; cash-pay restrictions vary — escalate Compliance if unsure.  
4. Providing card/HSA/FSA authorizes Klarity to charge; details may be stored until revoked.  
5. Refunds: align with 24h cancel rule (see billing topic).

## SOP — HIPAA transfer authorization (staff bullets)

1. Patient authorizes providers/insurers to disclose PHI to **Klarity Health, Inc.** and affiliates.  
2. Purposes include claim processing, scheduling, care continuity, limited admin review.  
3. Generally expires **5 years** from signature (or later personal-injury settlement language on form) unless revoked.  
4. Revocation in writing to providers where filed; does not undo prior disclosures.  
5. Questions: compliance@helloklarity.com.  
6. Staff: before discussing PHI with family/third parties, check **authorization on file** (Klarity marketplace privacy guidance).

## FAQ

**Can MA explain every legal clause?**  
No. Point to the portal form + Klarity URL. Escalate Privacy Officer / Compliance for disputes.

**Patient refuses telehealth consent?**  
Cannot proceed with telehealth under Klarity rules; escalate Clinical / Klarity support for alternatives.

**Siya Assist legal advice?**  
Never. Retrieve this brief + escalate.
