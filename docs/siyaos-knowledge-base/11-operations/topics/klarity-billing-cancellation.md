## Overview

Staff-facing summary of **Klarity Health** published billing / cancel / no-show / chargeback rules for patients booked on Klarity. **Do not invent Siya-only exceptions** in chat.

Klarity Billing Policy last revised on Klarity site: **Jul 30, 2025** (verified 2026-08-03).

## Why

Refund and cancel language is a liability magnet. Klarity already publishes rules; MAs need one place to retrieve them and escalate correctly.

## SOP — Self-pay charges

1. **Initial visit:** $10 **non-refundable** deposit; remainder charged **24 hours before** appointment.  
2. **Follow-up / refill:** charged **24 hours prior** to card on file.  
3. **Other subsequent services:** full fee charged **immediately** (per Billing Policy).  
4. **Incomplete intake / admin delay:** provider may charge **additional fees** for extended appointment time.  
5. Self-pay must be completed **before the appointment begins**.

## SOP — Refunds (self-pay)

| Situation | Klarity published outcome |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| Cancel **≥ 24 hours** before | Refund available; **$10 initial deposit stays non-refundable** |
| Late cancel (&lt; 24 hours), including same-day book then cancel | **No refund** (support article also: even if booked same day) |
| No-show | **No refund** / may be charged in full (provider discretion on insured custom amounts) |
| Completed visit | **No refund** |
| Pharmacy / Rx issues, dissatisfaction with med plan | **No refund** (Billing Policy + support) |
| Provider-directed rare cases (care not initiated / clinical mismatch before visit) | Possible refund — **provider / billing decision only** |

**Insured refunds:** Klarity does **not** process insurance refunds; patient works with **provider**. Claim denials → patient still responsible for balance; Klarity may **auto-charge** card on file.

## SOP — How patients cancel / reschedule

1. Klarity Patient Portal → Appointments → select visit → ⋮ menu → **Cancel or reschedule**.  
2. Or email **patientsupport@helloklarity.com**.  
3. Remind: **≥ 24 hours** notice required for refund eligibility (deposit still non-refundable on initials).

## SOP — Chargebacks

1. Unauthorized chargebacks after care / outside refund policy = Terms violation.  
2. May cause suspension, ban, collections.  
3. Tell patients to contact Klarity **before** disputing with the bank: patientsupport@helloklarity.com.  
4. Staff: never coach a chargeback; escalate Billing lead.

## SOP — Late arrival / tech

1. Arrive **5–10 minutes early**.  
2. Late arrival → shortened session; **full charge** may still apply.  
3. Tech failure preventing visit → Klarity **may** allow penalty-free reschedule at discretion — do not promise.

## FAQ

**Can MA approve a refund?**  
No. Billing lead / provider / Klarity support only.

**Is the $10 deposit ever refundable?**  
Klarity publishes it as **non-refundable** for initial visits.

**Klarity vs Siya.health cancel rules?**  
Different channels. Use this topic for Klarity bookings; use Siya finance/clinical policies for direct Siya.health bookings.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Patient demands same-day refund | Empathy + Klarity 24h rule; escalate Billing; no promise |
| Chargeback threat | Direct to Klarity support first; Billing lead |
| “Klarity charged me after insurance denial” | Expected per Payment Agreement; escalate Billing / provider |

## AI Context

Klarity self-pay: $10 non-refundable initial deposit; remainder and many follow-ups charge ~24h prior. Refunds only if cancel ≥24h (deposit kept). No refunds for late cancel, no-show, completed care, Rx issues, or dissatisfaction. Chargebacks discouraged — contact Klarity support. MAs and Assist never authorize refunds. Escalate Billing lead.
