## Guardrails for Assist + MA training

1. Klarity is a **marketplace platform**, not Siya’s clinic entity — clinical care is from **independent providers**.  
2. **Do not promise refunds** in chat; route to billing lead / Klarity patient support per policy.  
3. **No PHI** in Siya Assist.  
4. Siya cash-pay site pricing (`siya.health`) is a **different channel** — do not mix Klarity deposit/refund rules with Siya public pricing KB.  
5. Re-check Klarity pages when policy dates change; update `reviewDate` on topics.
