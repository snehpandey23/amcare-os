## Overview

Handling others visible or audible during a video visit.

## SOP

Require a private setting. If unauthorized viewers may hear/see PHI — pause visit, request private space, report if disclosure may have occurred.

## AI Context

Explain pause-and-reposition workflow. Escalate to Privacy Officer if PHI may have been disclosed.
