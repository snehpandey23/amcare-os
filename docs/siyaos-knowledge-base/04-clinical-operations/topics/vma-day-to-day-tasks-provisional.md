## Overview

**Provisional** checklist of day-to-day **virtual medical assistant** tasks. Pasted from a landing page. **May not be accurate.** Not a signed SOP. Do not cancel visits, clear invoices, or give clinical advice from Ask.

When this list and a **live** topic disagree, use the live topic and ask the **Clinical Program Manager**.

## Why

Staff asked what a VMA does day to day. This file holds the pasted list so Ask can point at it without inventing a handbook. Named helper docs were not supplied.

## SOP

Work the list as written, then confirm anything that conflicts with a live guide. Keep names, chart numbers, and message text out of Ask.

1. Check all appointments on the calendar for payment. Cancel unpaid ones under the stated **24-hour policy**.
2. Message cancelled appointments to clear the invoice and reschedule when that applies.
3. Check all appointments for form completion.
4. Send a form-completion message when that applies.
5. Complete precharting in chronological order for scheduled patients.
6. Check patient chats for the **past 48 hours** and make sure queries are resolved.
7. Check and resolve all faxes.
8. Check appointments for the **past 48 hours**. Confirm notes are locked and status/time are saved.
9. Send forms, create invoices, and book appointments as instructed, in order, within **30 minutes**.
10. Address patient-support messages and resolve them within **24 hours**.
11. Call existing patients for re-engagement when identified and instructed.
12. Create a spreadsheet for the patient database in **TX** and **PA**.
13. Maintain and provide daily, weekly, and monthly reports: appointment number, type, and state; insights; last visit; controlled vs non-controlled.
14. Track feedback and complaints in a separate spreadsheet (reputation).
15. Answer patient calls and inform providers when needed.
16. Maintain a database of standard messages to send to patients.
17. Answer practice calls during working hours. Call patients for follow-up when instructed.
18. Review the task platform every **30 minutes**. Complete pending tasks, then hide finished rows so only pending work stays in view.

**Helper names from the paste — URLs were not provided. Do not invent them:** Messages doc · Appointment links · Spreadsheets.
